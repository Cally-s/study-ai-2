# Assignment AI Student Attempts

> **Step 41 integration:** Student-Contribution Checkpoints record intentional, policy-authorized student decisions and artifacts across the exact nine-stage workflow. They preserve provenance, privacy, accessibility neutrality, Step 37–40 and learning-mode restrictions, final-answer gates, and immutable history without surveillance, authorship scoring, or automatic discipline.

Attempt requirements can target selected permissions and accept only student-authored, confirmed work with recorded provenance. Conditions are evaluated server-side before the assistance request. StudySpark does not fabricate attempts, infer effort or ability, punish incomplete work, or convert an attempt requirement into misconduct evidence.
