# Age-Safeguard Final Answers

Age safeguards compose with Assignment AI Policy, Assistance Ladder, Progressive Hints, Guiding Questions, Similar Examples, and Student-Contribution Checkpoints. Younger defaults require a student attempt and policy check before an answer in permitted ungraded practice; restricted work remains coaching-only. The server, provider gateway, retrieval, and tools enforce the rule. Hidden metadata, tool output, accessibility text, or near-identical examples cannot leak an answer. Accessibility support is never classified as answer generation.
