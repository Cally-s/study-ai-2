# Parent Summary Content Standard

Current situation uses neutral, verified counts or general wording. Priorities reuse the approved Step 9/10 decisions and show no more than three by default. The seven-day section uses exact dates and Minimum actions only, with at most three actions per day; Stretch, private notes, capacity reasons, buffer calculations, and internal dependencies remain hidden.

Deadlines preserve Confirmed, Student Reported, Proposed, Needs Confirmation, Conflicting, and Outdated status. Progress must have a validated source; opening a page, timer expiry, copying a message, or uploading a file is not completion. Support and family-help requests are student selected, practical, bounded, and never promises of adult availability.

Use “The student has identified…” and “Teacher confirmation is needed…” rather than judgements about motivation, effort, character, or comparison with others.
