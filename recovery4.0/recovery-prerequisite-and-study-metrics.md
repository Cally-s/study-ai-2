# Prerequisite and Study Metrics

Identifying or diagnosing a prerequisite is not repair. Count a narrow concept/course/repair cycle only after completed repair reaches task-specific readiness; never claim permanent mastery, and show later Review Needed separately. A completed study block needs an objective, definition of done, valid start, meaningful student action/output, evidence, and minimum-goal-achieved or completed status. Timer start, expiry, page time, media playback, and AI-only output never count.
