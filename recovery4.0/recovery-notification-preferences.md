# Recovery Notification Preferences

Academic reminders default Disabled. Push and email require separate explicit consent. Onboarding offers No Reminders, Confirmed Sessions Only, A Few Academic Reminders, or Choose My Settings without preselection pressure. Students control Disabled/Essential/Low/Standard/Custom frequency, each category, each channel, quiet/supportive/direct/simple tone, language, privacy, lead time, digest, and Disable All. Disabling requires no explanation and carries no penalty.
