# Notification Frequency and Bundling

Low permits at most one interruptive item per local day and three per rolling week. Standard permits two per day, seven per week, and normally 120 minutes between items. Category maximum is one daily; stricter policy wins and frequency never increases automatically. Notification-centre-only items do not consume interruptive caps. Low-value updates are bundled or placed in optional daily/weekly digests; confirmed session times stay clear. No floods after quiet hours or reconnect.
