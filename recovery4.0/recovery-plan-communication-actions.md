# Recovery Plan Communication Actions

Draft, ready for review, reviewed, copied, sent-confirmed, failed and waiting-for-response are distinct states. A “send” action completes only from confirmed delivery evidence; copied/opened/selected states do not imply sending. A draft-only action may complete at its reviewed definition.

Nothing is sent automatically. Failures preserve the draft and other progress. Recipient authorization, exact review and explicit confirmation are production requirements. Message content and status remain private and absent from ordinary analytics.
