# Overwhelmed Privacy

Mode/choice/drafts/support/today/action/pause and distress/safety/resource/trusted-adult data are private. Safety records are logically restricted to student and disclosed authorized safeguarding roles with audit/retention. Public, ordinary teacher/tutor/parent/guardian serializers expose nothing. Ordinary analytics use allowlisted aggregate events only; notifications stay neutral. AI receives minimum context and never medical/diagnostic/family/protected details.
