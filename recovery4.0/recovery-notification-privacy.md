# Notification Privacy

Device tokens, provider IDs, content, delivery/interaction history, and preferences are private. Tokens must be encrypted, scoped, revoked on logout/device removal, and never exposed to clients/logs. Generic lock-screen content and authenticated deep links are mandatory defaults. Parents, teachers, tutors, counsellors, public profiles, and ordinary analytics receive no reminder history/content. AI input is minimized. Engagement never affects grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, fraud, integrity, safety, discipline, recognition, or metrics.
