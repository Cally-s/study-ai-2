# Prerequisite Help Privacy

Statements, task links, candidates, responses, diagnostic evidence, approach history, bridges, readiness and human-help recommendations are owner-private. Public, teacher, tutor, parent and guardian serializers expose nothing by default. Exact consented previews share only necessary approved fields. Data cannot affect grades, placement, tutor/service/Help Credits, safety/fraud/integrity scoring, advertising or ordinary analytics; AI receives minimum authorized context without diagnosis/family data.
