# Scenario-Based Assessments

## Step 58 progression
Scenario evidence may feed a Performance Evidence Portfolio, but authentic pathway evidence remains required. Scenario completion or choices never replace the Understand identification package, Apply prompt/source/correction/disclosure package, or Create design/prototype/testing/reflection package.

## Purpose
Scenario-Based Assessments evaluate whether students can apply AI-literacy concepts to realistic decisions, explain their reasoning, identify uncertainty, and choose responsible next actions.

Vocabulary questions remain useful for low-pressure preparation, terminology review, optional practice, and recognition checks, but recall alone cannot demonstrate applied policy, privacy, verification, ownership, fairness, oversight, system evaluation, or responsible integrity decisions.

## Required reasoning
Substantive tasks show **Explain your reasoning**. A selected answer without reasoning is not normally sufficient evidence of AI-literacy competency. Evidence comes from the submitted decision, explanation, comparison, verification process, design choice, or recommended action—not AI-use frequency or behavioural surveillance.

## Modes and evidence
Modes are Practice, Formative, Diagnostic, Summative with Human Review, Capstone Evidence, Competency Demonstration, Independent Retry, and Teacher-Created Activity. Practice cannot secretly become graded; formal policies and resources appear before an attempt. Evidence describes one response, never permanent ability.

Scenario responses never create misconduct findings, Step 55 cases, alerts, grade penalties, parent notifications, discipline, rankings, or risk scores. Ambiguous formal responses require authorized human review.
# Step 64 offline scenarios

Eligible fictional scenario responses may be drafted offline with the current scenario, rubric, policy, safeguard, accessibility, and language versions. Offline steps do not create assessment completion, grade, evidence, or case. Reconnection validates versions and requires intentional submission and configured human review.
