# Scenario Integrity

Fictional, synthetic, composite, deidentified, or authorized public cases assess ownership, personal reflections, final-answer requests, source invention, disclosure, group work, active assessments, and Policy Unknown. Prompts ask about the issue, missing context, permitted support, next action, human review, and what must not be inferred—not whether a student is guilty. Responses never create real policy-conflict cases or evidence of the respondent's intent or conduct.
