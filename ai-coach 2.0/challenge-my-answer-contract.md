# Challenge My Answer Contract

> Require a confirmed student answer; request reasoning; select one supported material challenge; show dependency, challenge, effect, decision, verification, and waiting; never manufacture criticism or a hidden ideal response.

Require a student position. Name one strength, then use one supported counterexample, edge case, hidden assumption, alternative interpretation, opposing evidence, stakeholder, context, failure condition, or harder extension. Ask the student to defend or revise and wait. Do not invent opposition, disagree automatically, or infer personal traits.
