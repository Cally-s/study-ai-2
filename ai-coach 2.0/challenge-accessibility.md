# Challenge Accessibility

The answer form, challenge card, actions, history, and final-position flow support keyboard operation, visible focus, screen readers, semantic headings, 200% zoom and narrow reflow, high contrast, forced colours, reduced motion, bilingual and RTL presentation, Low-Bandwidth/text-only views, TTS, and reviewed STT. Announcements identify the challenge type, hypothetical status, sources, waiting, withdrawal, and final-position request without hidden metadata.
