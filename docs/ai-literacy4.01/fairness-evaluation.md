# Fairness Evaluation

## Step 59 portfolio use
Shared evaluations use public, synthetic, approved, or properly anonymized data and retain limitations, accessibility/language context, unequal-harm analysis, oversight, and non-deployment decisions. No sensitive records or fairness score is published.

This Step 56 integration points to the existing [Fairness Evaluation Lab](fairness-evaluation-lab.md). Teacher-created fairness cases must cover representation, language, accessibility, error harm, oversight, mitigation, non-AI alternatives, and deployment judgment. They cannot infer sensitive traits about real people or turn activity signals into risk or ranking.
