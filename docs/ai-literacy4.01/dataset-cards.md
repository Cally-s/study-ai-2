# Dataset Cards

Record name/use/source/version, creator/steward/represented subjects, type/schema/count, collection/generation, permission/licence, personal/sensitive/de-identification status, missing groups, quality/bias, retention/access/external processing/deletion/withdrawal, review date, and conditions not to use. Evidence and versions remain linked. A card does not certify anonymity, fairness, accuracy, legality, or universal suitability.
