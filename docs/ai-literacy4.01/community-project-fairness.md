# Fairness

Review exclusion, differential errors and consequences, burden, language and culture, devices, bandwidth, transparent matching, correction, opt-out, and non-AI access using synthetic or consented scenarios. Do not infer sensitive traits or produce one fairness score.

## System-card disclosure

Current exclusions, differential errors and burdens, language/cultural assumptions, and unresolved concerns remain visible without a fairness score.

## Dataset review

Coverage and quality records disclose missing people, languages, accessibility modes, connectivity, places, periods, unequal labels, and historical bias without claiming diversity proves fairness.

## Step 26 canonical lab

Fairness Evaluation Lab extends the existing review and test records with scenario matrices, comparability, denominators, language/stereotype/accessibility/harm findings, mitigation/retest, residual risk, reviewer sufficiency, versioning, and student-controlled deployment decisions—never a fairness score.
