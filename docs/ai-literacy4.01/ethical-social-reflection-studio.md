# Ethical and Social Reflection Studio

> Step 35 ethical challenges identify one concrete affected person, burden, exclusion, accountability, privacy, accessibility, or downstream consequence without inferring moral or political character or deciding the student's position.

## Purpose
Step 28 extends the canonical Step 21 Impact Reflection into a versioned, evidence-linked final review. The student remains the final recommender; AI suggestions are labelled “StudySpark suggested — student review required.”

## Exact twelve questions
1. Who benefits from this tool?
2. Who could be harmed?
3. Who may be excluded?
4. What work does the system automate?
5. What human skills must remain?
6. What happens if the tool is wrong?
7. Who is accountable?
8. Could the tool increase dependence on AI?
9. Could a non-AI solution be more equitable?
10. What environmental or resource costs should be considered?
11. What would responsible deployment require?
12. Would you release this tool publicly? Why or why not?

## Ethical and Social Review Gate
All questions, supporting analyses, final limitations, evidence review, release decision, and student confirmation are required. The gate is checked for completion, export, offline synchronization, release, and deployment recommendations. No-deployment and report-only decisions satisfy academic completion but never authorize functional release.

## No-deployment principle
Students may keep a private prototype, test further, narrow scope, use an authorized pilot review, replace AI, publish only a report, decline release or deployment, pause, or stop. These choices do not lower competency, apply a negative label, or cause failure.

## Known limitations
This static prototype models authoritative server gates, ownership, idempotency, privacy-safe export, and offline revalidation in memory. It does not certify ethics, safety, fairness, accessibility, sustainability, legal compliance, school approval, or production readiness.
