# Claims, Evidence, and Sources

A claim is a statement presented as true, likely, current, causal, comparative, or worthy of acceptance. Evidence is the specific information used to support or challenge it. A source is the document, dataset, record, study, policy, transcript, test, or other origin containing that evidence.

A title, URL, organization name, citation, or page repeating the claim is not evidence by itself. A factual map requires an identified evidence item, source or documented provenance, location where available, and student-reviewed reasoning link.
