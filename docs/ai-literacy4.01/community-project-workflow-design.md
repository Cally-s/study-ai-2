# Workflow Design

Use an accessible ordered flow: input; consent, validation, and privacy; rules or AI; sources; uncertainty; human review; output; refusal, escalation, correction, or feedback; retention or deletion. Include non-AI, accessible, and low-bandwidth paths.

## Step 22 gate

## Architecture Design Studio
