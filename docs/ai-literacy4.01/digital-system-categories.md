# Digital System Categories

- Generative AI creates new content from learned patterns.
- Search engines retrieve and rank indexed sources; AI may be one component.
- Calculators apply defined mathematical operations.
- Databases store and retrieve structured records.
- Rule-based systems follow conditions written by people.
- Recommendation systems rank/suggest using rules, machine learning, or both.
- Machine-learning systems learn patterns for prediction, classification, ranking, or generation.
- Human-created digital content remains human authored unless evidence says otherwise.
- Hybrid systems combine multiple components and outputs.

Product names, appearance, speed, polish, digital format, or “smart” labels do not establish category or authorship.
