# AccessibilityPreferences Privacy

Every preference field is private. Public profiles/APIs/search/social metadata/caches/tutor cards contain none. Home/bridge language is not nationality, ethnicity, immigration or proficiency data. Low-Bandwidth use is not financial data. Beginner describes an explanation, never the student.

Tutor/teacher sharing is separate from this table and allowlists selected functional fields only. Grants require subject, recipient, purpose, scope, expiry and revocation. Reasons, full profiles, diagnoses, accommodations, transcripts and recordings remain excluded.

Ordinary analytics may record only that a preference operation occurred and a count of changed fields; values, full objects, language history, advertising and scoring are excluded. Logs/URLs/notifications exclude payloads. Account export may include owner preferences after authorization. Deletion follows account policy. Device records require user-scoped keys and shared-device cleanup. AI providers receive minimal presentation instructions, never diagnostic context.
