# Recovery Fixed Commitments

Controlled commitments cover school, work, tutoring, sports/activities, family responsibility, medical appointment, transportation, meal, sleep, personal care, teacher meeting, religious/community responsibility, other essential commitment and generic unavailable time. Flexibility is Fixed, Somewhat Flexible, Flexible or Unknown; flexibility never authorizes automatic movement.

Only local date/time, generic label, travel, source/version and functional flags are required. Employer, provider, diagnosis, family names and private reasons are not. Medical displays as “Unavailable — appointment.” Essential attendance, sleep, meals, care, work and transportation remain protected.

Tutoring consumes time and is never added again as independent capacity. Transportation is not study time unless the student explicitly marks a safe activity such as audio review, flashcards or reading; unsafe device use is never suggested. Overlaps are reported for review, not silently resolved.
