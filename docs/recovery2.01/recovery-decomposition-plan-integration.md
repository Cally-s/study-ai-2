# Recovery Decomposition Plan Integration

Step 15 replaces a broad Step 14 block with linked observable actions; it does not create a parallel plan. Initial suitable actions may be recommended for Minimum, then optional Stretch, while later work remains scheduled later or visibly unfitted.

Related setup actions may share a fitting block while retaining completion evidence. Acceptance recalculates estimates and affected plan layers, but the revised plan remains under review. Completed actions and protected buffer are unchanged; all actions are never automatically poured into the week.
