# Recovery Decomposition Academic Integrity

Actions are classified as planning, information retrieval, concept review, similar practice, student-authored drafting/revision, submission preparation/action, restricted or needs review. StudySpark may organize verified requirements and guide student-authored work, but cannot create a complete graded submission or reveal active-test answers.

Submission remains a student action with confirmed receipt evidence; upload is not acceptance. Assignment text is untrusted, inputs are minimized, outputs are validated and hidden prompts/answer keys are excluded.
