# Recovery Due-Item Suggestions

Production sources may include authorized assessments, assignments, projects, plan tasks, calendar events and teacher materials. Default results are limited to three; explicit expansion is capped at five. Test Tomorrow comes first; other modes prefer current course-level context rather than complete backlogs.

Exact ISO dates preserve timezone. Confidence is VERIFIED, STUDENT_ENTERED, INFERRED_FROM_AUTHORIZED_CONTEXT or NOT_CONFIRMED. Late acceptance is explicitly NOT_APPLICABLE, CONFIRMED, NOT_CONFIRMED, DECLINED or UNKNOWN; overdue never implies acceptance.

Help Me Find Out checks one course and at most three authorized sources/items, then permits unknown or clarification. Offline cached items require an exact last-synced label; current facts are not fabricated. The static prototype has no authorized academic-data service, so runtime UI offers manual/unknown paths only.
