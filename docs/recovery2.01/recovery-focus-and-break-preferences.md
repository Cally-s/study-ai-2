# Recovery Focus and Break Preferences

Preferred maximum focus blocks are 10, 20, 45 or 90 minutes, task-dependent, custom or unknown. Concentration preferences cover morning, confirmed school study period, after school, afternoon/evening, variable, custom or unknown. Preferences never override availability, commitments or protected sleep and never represent ability/motivation.

Breaks are 5, 10, 15, custom, task-dependent, unknown or none for very short blocks. Functional movement/food/water/quiet/screen/assistive-technology breaks need no medical explanation. Breaks, setup and transitions stay inside the planning envelope.

Recovery-only supports use an allowlist: shorter blocks, extra breaks/setup/transitions, one task, text/audio/access technology modes, low bandwidth/offline, no camera/microphone, video spacing, variable capacity and quiet setting. Diagnosis fields are rejected and account accessibility defaults are not changed.
