# Recovery Effort and Dependencies

## Estimated time still needed

Step 4 stores remaining-time ranges, not guaranteed total durations. Choices cover 5 minutes through four hours, more than four hours, several sessions, unknown and Help Me Estimate. Sources distinguish student, teacher, platform, AI-assisted, imported and unknown estimates; confidence is high, medium, low or unknown. AI-assisted ranges are provisional. Students may change or keep an estimate unknown.

## Grade weight

Grade weight supports percentage, points, category, unknown and not applicable with nullable value and source. Percentages outside 0–100 are rejected. Weight is private, never fabricated and never becomes the sole priority factor.

## Dependencies

Normalized links preserve prerequisite item, optional dependent item/description, source and confidence. A link means one item may help another; it does not establish final priority, continued acceptance or unlimited effort. Self-links and graph cycles are rejected for review without deleting information.

## Instructions, materials and blockers

Instruction understanding is Yes, Partly, No, Unknown or Not Applicable. Material readiness is Have All, Missing Some, Have None, Unknown or Not Applicable. Missing-material text is private. Controlled blockers cover prerequisite, instructions, materials, feedback, technical/access, teacher decision, human help, unavailable time, Other and Unknown; no diagnosis or private medical/family explanation is collected.
## Capacity use

Remaining-time estimates feed only a transparent known-workload total. Unknown estimates remain unknown. Step 6 compares that total with capacity without making dependencies, weights or duration the final priority decision.

Step 7 totals remaining-time ranges in integer minutes. Prerequisites contribute once to the earliest relevant deadline; unknown dependencies require clarification and cycles fail closed. Grade weight never changes Recovery Load.

Step 11 records only the smallest task-specific prerequisite dependency. It does not add an entire unit; shared active prerequisite bridges are reused across related tasks.
