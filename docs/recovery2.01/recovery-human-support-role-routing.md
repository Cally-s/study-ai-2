# Human Support Role Routing

Teacher: course methods, policy, late work, acceptance, and submitted-work feedback. Verified tutor: live concept observation and guided practice only after subject/grade verification. Counsellor or authorized school support: extended absence, multi-course coordination, infeasible workload, and significant distress. Accessibility professional: formal accommodations and authorized assessment support. Parent, guardian, or trusted adult: student-selected practical or safety support. Authorized supervisor: labs, groups, practical work, performances, equipment, and facilities.

Authority boundaries are explicit: tutors do not grade or approve policy/accommodations; parents do not approve school accommodations; AI grants no extensions; counsellors are not course teachers; teachers and tutors do not replace crisis support; completion is never certified without authorized evidence.
