# Recovery Focus Block Standard

Broad assignment labels must be decomposed before activation. Related observable actions may share a block only when the upper estimate fits and every action retains its definition of done, dependency and completion evidence.

Blocks have one specific purpose: teacher contact, verification, prerequisite repair, setup/work/test/practice, material retrieval, submission check, review/catch-up/replan, tutor/application preparation or another validated action.

Each block retains level, course/task, min/max duration, order, optional window, dependency, material/communication requirements, completion definition and reasons. Related micro-actions may group up to three while retaining individual plan-item history. Unrelated subjects cannot be grouped to hide overload.

Preferred block length is authoritative. Unknown preference uses a conservative 20-minute proposal. Long work requires a validated natural split or decomposition; generic “work on schoolwork” blocks are prohibited. Break/setup remain within availability.
