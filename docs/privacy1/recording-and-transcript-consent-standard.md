# Recording and Transcript Consent Standard

Optional voice, recording, personal transcript, shared transcript, tutor sharing, teacher sharing, and export choices are separate. Optional controls are never preselected. Session audio authorization is limited to one action or one session; account-level blanket recording consent is rejected.

Notices must show content, raw-audio handling, transcript status, purpose, recipients, processors, retention, decline, alternative, withdrawal, deletion, derivatives, downloads, and contact. Recording cannot begin until the current notice and policy authorize it. Withdrawal stops future optional processing but does not claim existing data was deleted. Captions do not imply a saved transcript.
