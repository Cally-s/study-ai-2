# Voice and Transcript Deletion Standard

Temporary speech audio, voice messages, pronunciation audio, session recordings, synthetic summaries, live buffers, personal/shared transcripts, notes, and derivatives are distinct. Deletion must separately track access revocation, primary copy, provider deletion, backup expiry, holds, shared-record review, and derivatives. “Completed” is forbidden until the applicable work completes. External downloads and other participants’ copies cannot be promised removed. The prototype prepares drafts only.
