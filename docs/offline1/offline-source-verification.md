# Offline Source Verification

Offline drafts retain observations, claim analysis, source/citation/quotation/currentness notes, disagreement, questions, online-check plan, and preliminary revision. Cached material is labelled **Cached source copy** with capture date/version/limitations and proves neither live availability, authenticity, currentness, unchanged content, nor exact support. Status stays **Verification pending internet access** after synchronization until authorized online/human checks confirm identity, publisher, availability, dates, current content, quotation/citation, exact support, and disagreement. Changed/disappeared sources preserve work and offer revision or human review. Pending work is ineligible for Applied Independently, Source Verifier, competency, and formal completion.
## Step 70 metric eligibility

`Verification pending internet access` is never completed verification. Offline evidence enters a later computation only after authentication, synchronization, package/activity/policy/safeguard version validation, required source checks, and required human review. Existing published snapshots are not silently rewritten.
