# Audio-Only Lesson Summaries

Audio-only means no video is required, not no text. Summaries are user initiated, controllable, optionally downloadable, size-labelled, pronunciation-aware, and always paired with a transcript. They preserve objective, academic terms, warnings, uncertainty, sources, policy/privacy reminders, and next actions. They never autoplay, expose hidden answers/identity metadata, replace necessary full lessons/rubrics/sources, or store raw student audio.
