# Why AI Can Invent Information

This Understand lesson teaches students to investigate Unsupported AI Output: AI-generated information that lacks adequate evidence, mismatches a source, contains an error, omits context, or overstates certainty. “Hallucination” is a common metaphor; it does not mean AI perceives, intends, believes, or lies like a person. Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect move from TRACE to a corrected answer and private verification summary. The static prototype lacks production search, authentication, persistence, speech, localization, and competency ingestion.

# Prompt-Writing Coach integration

Step 12 teaches that asking for accuracy, sources, or confident language does not verify an answer. Response Review routes claims to source, quotation, calculation, currentness, or code checks.

Step 13 reinforces that a clearer or longer prompt can still produce unsupported claims, invented citations, incorrect calculations, outdated information, or missing context. Revision and verification remain separate.

Step 14 turns this caution into claim-level checks that distinguish absent support, contrary evidence, irrelevant sources, outdated information, conflicting evidence, and non-factual statements.
