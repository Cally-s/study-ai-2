# What AI Is Evidence

Candidates may cover scenario/system/output/AI-involvement classification, responsibility explanation, misconception correction, tool selection, student explanation, fictional/authentic audit, and consequence reflection. They map primarily to AI Foundations and Human Agency; Application Skills is included only for meaningful verification/tool selection.

Students review, edit, accept, decline, or delete candidates. Neither lesson completion nor candidate acceptance automatically awards a competency; the Step 3 engine remains authoritative.
