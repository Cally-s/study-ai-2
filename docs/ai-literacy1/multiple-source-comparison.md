# Multiple-Source Comparison

Important, surprising, broad, contested, current, quantitative, causal, or submission-related claims often warrant more than one independent source. Compare underlying data, population, definition, dates, outcomes, and methods.

Record agreement, partial agreement, disagreement, dependence, or need for more sources. Source count is not a quality score, and incompatible numbers are never averaged automatically.

Step 15 adds semantic dependence grouping and student-controlled different-sources-for-different-parts decisions.
