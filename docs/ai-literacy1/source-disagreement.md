# Source Disagreement

Reliable sources may differ by date, definition, population, location, method, sample, outcome, policy scope, version, interpretation, or evidence quality, or directly contradict each other. Show sources side by side with scope and limitations.

Do not hide or mechanically average disagreement. Use Conflicting Evidence, Partially Supported, Insufficient Information, or human review while uncertainty remains.

Step 15 surfaces disagreement beside methods, definitions, dates, populations, outcomes, limitations, and independence before weight is assigned.
