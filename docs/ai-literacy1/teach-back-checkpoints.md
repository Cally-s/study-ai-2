# Teach-Back Checkpoints

A submitted Teach-Back may be response-level reasoning, application, revision, verification, or decision evidence for a Student-Contribution Checkpoint and Student Agency Record. The checkpoint references the immutable attempt/feedback, criteria version, target, provenance, and evidence status. Submission never auto-completes a checkpoint; required checks remain incomplete with a clear explanation when skipped or partial. Competency changes follow configured multi-evidence rules and human review, not a single response or AI confidence.
