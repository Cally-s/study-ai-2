# No Source Does Not Mean False

No Reliable Source Found means reasonable checks did not locate adequate support among accessible sources. It does not mean false or contradicted. Contradicted requires contrary evidence.

Students record search scope, access limitations, unavailable sources, and remaining uncertainty. A failed search or unlocated AI citation is not automatically proof of fabrication.
