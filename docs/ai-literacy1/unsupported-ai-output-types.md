# Unsupported AI Output Types

The lesson covers plausible errors, invented or unverified facts, invented/unverifiable references, real but irrelevant or misrepresented references, misattributed/not-found quotations, calculation and unit errors, outdated information, missing context/limitations, unsupported statistics, causation/generalization overclaims, and overconfident language. An answer may mix supported and unsupported claims.
