# Source Relevance

Review whether a source addresses the same topic, population, outcome, date range, location, policy scope, and exact wording. Statuses include directly/partly relevant, background/related only, wrong population/date/geography/policy/outcome, irrelevant, and insufficient information.

Existence, authority, title, link, or search rank alone does not establish support. A relevant source may support only part of a claim.

Step 15 compares relevance side by side with currentness, evidence, scope, and weight for the selected claim.
