# What AI Is Classification Activity

Required synthetic scenarios cover calculator, search, generative chatbot, streaming recommendation, teacher feedback, and rule-based account locking; database and hybrid educational-tool challenges are included.

Students select primary/secondary concepts, AI involvement, information, output process, responsibility, and an optional explanation. Ambiguous cases accept supported alternatives and “Not enough information” with missing-information reasoning. Feedback is Supported, Partly Supported, Needs Another Look, Responsible Uncertainty, or Hybrid Recognized. Initial and revised answers remain separate; evidence remains a candidate.
