# Source Evidence and Limitations

Evidence records type, summary, location, method, sample/coverage, date/geography, measured outcome, and directness. Notes use precise locations and short excerpts rather than unnecessary copyrighted text.

Limitations include age, sample/scope/duration, method/data/access, causation, independence, interest/funding/expertise/publisher/peer-review uncertainty, differing definitions/outcomes/policies, translation, correction/retraction/supersession, and selective reporting. Use “No Material Limitation Identified Yet,” not “No limitations.” A limitation changes interpretation; it does not automatically make a source useless.

## Step 16 — Claim–Evidence Map

Step 16 distinguishes the source from the specific evidence and records limitations separately for source, evidence, reasoning link, and claim wording.
