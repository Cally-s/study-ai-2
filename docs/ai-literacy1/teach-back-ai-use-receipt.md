# Teach-Back and AI Use Receipt

Creating the prompt/feedback is AI contribution; an intentionally submitted Teach-Back artifact is student contribution with validated provenance. Step 50 receives a privacy-safe summary event linked to the check, attempt, and response-level evidence. The private receipt may reference the full private artifact under retention controls; a shareable receipt excludes the full response by default and offers summary selection/redaction. Suggested disclosure accurately distinguishes the AI prompt/feedback from the student response and never claims proof of mastery or authorship.

The attempt permits accessibility and approved resources but no substantive AI hints. Support requests and task reports preserve work without penalty; only a valid submitted attempt may produce descriptive transfer evidence. Expected answers and evaluation keys remain server-side.
