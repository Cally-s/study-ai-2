# Meaningful Badges

> Step 65 canonical mapping: recognition consumes verified competency evidence by exact definition and artifact version. It does not write competency status directly, use a hidden/ranking score, or award recognition from source-verification-pending evidence.

Meaningful Badges recognize demonstrated AI-literacy competencies supported by explicit evidence. They must not reward AI consumption, prompt volume, activity frequency, speed, popularity, or platform engagement.

The exact core badges are AI Foundations, Privacy Protector, Source Verifier, Prompt Designer, Responsible AI User, Bias Investigator, Human-Centred Designer, AI Tool Creator, Community AI Builder, and Ethics-by-Design. Every badge requires criterion-level authentic evidence, student reasoning/decision-making, provenance/version, limitations, and authorized human review. Awards are private by default; consumption badges such as “100 Prompts Sent,” “Daily AI User,” and “Most AI Answers” are prohibited.
# Step 64 recognition boundary

Badge applications and evidence explanations may be drafted offline, but awards, candidate eligibility, review submission, public verification, and competency recognition require authenticated server validation and intentional action. Pending source verification cannot support Source Verifier or Applied Independently. Offline activity/usage/package counts never affect badges.
