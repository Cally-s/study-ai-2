# Meaningful Badge Display

Awards are private by default. Students may hide them or choose display in their Competency Portfolio; public verification uses a Step 59 privacy-reviewed immutable snapshot and exposes only issuer, scope, criteria/version/date, summary, and limitations—not the full package. The required notice disclaims permanent mastery, independent authorship, and a complete record. Display/publication never affects grades or recognition and produces no rank, counts, likes, views, or leaderboards.
# Step 62 badge audiences

Badges remain private by default. Younger profiles may use student, teacher, course, or school-authenticated display; public verification is disabled by default and requires separate eligibility, approval, and student confirmation when permitted. Full evidence remains private. Profile changes never revoke an earned badge, and no age-based, usage, streak, ranking, or negative badge is created.
