Badge discovery, criteria, applications, evidence mapping, package preview, review, feedback, award, display, verification, correction, reconsideration, and export support keyboard/screen readers, visible text labels and focus, no colour-only meaning, TTS without autoplay, reviewed STT, 200%/320px, high contrast/forced colours, Reduced Motion, bilingual/meaning-based evaluation, RTL, mobile, Low-Bandwidth, text-only/print, and offline private drafts. # Step 63 badge equivalence

Badge applications, criterion maps, human review, displays, and verification support the shared accessibility/language profile and equivalent evidence formats. Canonical criterion terms remain visible in approved translations; private settings and diagnoses remain absent from badge records and public pages.
