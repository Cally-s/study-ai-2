# Digital Skills Privacy Standard

Step 22 defines a synthetic “Practice Privacy Controls” scenario contract. It may preview choices and simulated requests only; it must not alter production consent, grants, exports, deletion, or another user’s data. A production sandbox remains unavailable.

Guide search/use and practice attempts remain private and are excluded from profiles, grades, matching, ratings, safety/fraud/integrity scoring, service hours, Help Credits, standing and advertising. No digital-ability score, ranking, label or inference exists. Shared-device production storage is unavailable; practice memory expires/removes without recovery.
