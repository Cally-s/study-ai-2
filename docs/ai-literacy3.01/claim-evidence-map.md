# Claim–Evidence Map

## Purpose

Connect each factual claim to specific evidence, its existing Step 15 source, a student-reviewed support reason, and limitations. The semantic sequence is Claim → Evidence → Source → Reason the evidence supports the claim → Limitation or uncertainty. Structured cards replace any need to drag graph nodes.

## Integration

The map references Step 14 claim and answer versions and Step 15 source and comparison records. Map decisions can feed a reviewed new Step 14 decision version but never silently overwrite it. Original responses and every revision remain preserved.

## Known limitations

The static prototype uses private in-memory state. Production persistence, server authorization, live currentness checks, durable offline sync, and real speech/translation services remain required.

## Step 17 — AI Citation and Disclosure

Step 17 source links may reference saved Step 16 maps only when specific evidence and a support reason exist.

## Step 18 — AI Brainstorming

Step 18 assumptions and proposed factual premises may be added to Step 16; an idea seed itself is not evidence.

## Step 19 — AI Feedback

Step 19 missing-reasoning and unsupported-claim issues may open Step 16; issue guidance never supplies the missing reasoning paragraph.

## Step 20 — Revision With Judgment

Step 20 uses Step 16 for added/removed claim/evidence/limitation review while preserving exact revision provenance.

## Community projects

Claim–evidence links may support need statements, problem definitions, model-card claims, accuracy findings, and impact conclusions.
