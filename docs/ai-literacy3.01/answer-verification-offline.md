# Answer Verification Offline

Cached synthetic cases/sources, claim review, evidence planning, source notes, draft statuses, explanations, and revisions may work offline. External search, current-source verification, live calculation/code tools, sharing, and current policy confirmation do not.

Reconnection validates answer/source/policy/workflow versions and currentness, reruns privacy review, preserves history, prevents duplicate records, isolates accounts, and requires review before external work.
