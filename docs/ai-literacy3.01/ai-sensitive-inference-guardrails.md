# Sensitive-Inference Guardrails

Never infer a real person’s protected or sensitive traits from a name, image, video, voice, accent, clothing, address/postal code, school, neighbourhood, writing, device, browsing, disability aid, translation, or accessibility setting. Do not upload or rank real résumés, photographs, voices, candidates, or neighbourhoods. Use explicitly fictional profiles, self-described information, or privacy-safe aggregates.
