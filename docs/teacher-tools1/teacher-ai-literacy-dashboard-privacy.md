# Dashboard Privacy

Role, tenant, course, assignment, section, and student authorization apply to every record. Private conversations, private receipts, unsubmitted reflections, safety content, accessibility diagnoses, and other-course work are excluded. Cohort aggregates require at least five students and five records by default, with stricter institutional rules prevailing. Students receive transparency about which submitted evidence may be teacher visible.

## Step 55 — Teacher Review Instead of Automatic Discipline

Trusted structured policy mismatches extend Step 40 with an educational reminder, server-permitted alternatives, optional separately stored student context, explicit published-rule escalation, minimal review packages, authorized human outcomes, and neutral versioned resolutions. Simple reminders and changed requests do not escalate by default. StudySpark never infers intent or misconduct, sends full conversations/private artifacts, uses behavioral signals or frequency scores, applies consequences, changes grades, restricts accounts, notifies parents, or creates discipline. Accessibility and safety remain separate.
# Step 62 visibility

Teachers receive only activity-relevant safeguard summaries such as final-answer, sharing, and oversight rules. They do not see exact age, birthdate, guardian details, consent evidence, private conversations/drafts/receipts/reflections/safety content, correction text, or age/maturity/risk scores. Unknown/conflict states request authorized support, not discipline.
