# Teacher Lesson Tools

## Step 60 badge templates
Curriculum leads may configure evidence-based versioned badge templates with human approval. Consumption metrics, scarcity, competition, negative labels, hidden criteria, and unauthorized certification claims are rejected.

## Step 59 portfolio suggestions
Teachers may suggest eligible published evidence or templates but cannot add private items, choose a public identity, broaden sharing, or publish student work. Private course submission remains distinct from public publication.

## Step 58 builder integration
Teachers may author Understand, Apply, or Create tasks and rubrics as private drafts, preview requirements, validate privacy/policy/source/accessibility/prototype safety, then publish and assign intentionally. Authoring never grades or recognizes students.

## Purpose
Teacher Lesson Tools is the Step 56 curriculum-design workspace. It reuses the platform's existing competencies, lessons, scenarios, sources, assignment policies, dashboard evidence, human-review records, System Cards, and Capstone reflections.

## Actions
- Assign Specific Competency Lessons
- Add School-Specific Scenarios
- Create Integrity Case Studies
- Upload an AI Policy
- Correct Generated Explanations
- Add Approved Sources
- Create Prompt-Comparison Activities
- Create Source-Verification Challenges
- Build a Fairness Case Study
- Review Student System Cards
- Assess Capstone Reflections

## Required notice
> Teacher Lesson Tools support curriculum design and human review. AI-generated drafts must be checked before publication, assignment, source approval, policy use, or assessment.

Draft, validation, publication, assignment, source approval, and assessment are separate intentional actions. The workspace never automatically publishes, assigns, approves a source, binds policy, grades, grants competency, or decides a System Card or Capstone outcome.

## Student provenance
> Some StudySpark lessons and activities may be created or adapted by your teacher. Your teacher remains responsible for reviewing the assigned content.

## Step 57 assessment authoring
Published Step 56 scenarios may be selected for Scenario-Based Assessments. Authoring drafts remain private and separate from formal assignment; AI drafts require teacher review, real incidents require deidentification, and answer keys remain server-protected.
# Step 64 package authoring

Teachers can select exact versions, eligibility, languages, accessibility assets, compressed media, worksheets, cached-source permissions, policy/safeguard snapshots, size/expiry, and synchronization requirements. Server validation blocks missing text/alternatives, hidden answers/rubrics, unlicensed/private data, source-completion claims, unsigned content, or weakened policy before immutable publication.
