# Performance Recognition

## Step 60 badge evidence
Authorized performance recognition and its criterion evidence may support badge applications, but never award automatically. Every badge independently reviews its own definition/criterion versions and limitations; one badge never cascades into another.

## Step 59 display
Students may display authorized recognition records in selected snapshots. Curation, omission, sharing, views, downloads, and feedback never award, remove, rank, or modify recognition, and the public notice disclaims permanent mastery.

Statuses include evidence in progress/submitted, insufficient/partial evidence, Understand/Apply/Create demonstrated, Applied independently, Demonstrated with revision, and Human review required. Recognition links an authorized human decision to criterion evidence, competencies, portfolio version, and limitations. Applied Independently additionally requires valid Step 52 evidence. It never depends on counts, volume, one opaque score, or vocabulary alone and creates no rank, authorship/honesty proof, or permanent mastery.
