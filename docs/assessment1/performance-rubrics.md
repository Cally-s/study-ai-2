# Performance Assessment Rubrics

Rubrics use relevant criterion dimensions and the statuses Demonstrated, Applied independently, Partly demonstrated, Clarification needed, Revision required, Not enough evidence yet, Human review required, Not applicable, and Task Invalid. Understand covers five identification areas and explanation/action. Apply covers prompt, privacy/ownership, claims, sources, correction, recheck, disclosure, and decisions. Create covers problem, appropriateness, alternatives, workflow/data, prototype, testing, harm, oversight, feedback, iteration, limitations, deployment, social consequences, disclosure, and group contribution. No combined hidden score is required; humans confirm formal outcomes.
