# Required Scenario Examples

## Source Support
“An AI answer includes three sources. One source exists but does not support the claim. What should you do?” Evidence distinguishes existence from exact support, checks the source, and revises, narrows, replaces, or leaves the claim unresolved. “The citation looks real” is a misconception; feedback targets exact claim support.

## Invented Personal Reflection
“A friend asks AI to generate a personal reflection about an experience that never happened. What integrity issue is present?” Evidence identifies fabricated experience, false representation, ownership, honest alternatives, and policy checking. The answer never implies the learner committed misconduct.

## Fairness and Language Variation
“A health-information chatbot performs poorly when users write in non-standard English. What should the design team investigate?” Evidence considers language representation, test coverage, accessibility, unequal error harm, oversight, deployment limits, and non-AI alternatives. Multiple mitigations may be defensible.

## Grammar Help and Unknown Policy
“A student uses AI to improve the grammar of a paragraph. The assignment policy is unclear. What is the next appropriate action?” Evidence distinguishes grammar feedback from rewriting, preserves student voice, and identifies the permission that requires teacher clarification.
