# Scenario Teacher Review

AI or deterministic evaluation may draft formative, criterion-linked evidence and feedback. Authorized reviewers see submitted responses only, never private drafts, and cannot edit originals. Review records preserve automated evidence, reviewer identity, criteria decisions, clarification, revision request, feedback, limitations, version, and timestamps. Ambiguous open responses, summative work, Capstone evidence, formal grades, and configured high-stakes competency decisions require human review; grading remains a separate authorized workflow.
