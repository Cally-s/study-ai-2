# Scenario Competency Recognition

## Step 60 integration
Reviewed scenario reasoning may support one or more badge criteria when exact evidence versions and independent satisfaction are documented. Scenario completion or answer selection alone is ineligible.

## Step 59 display
Selected scenario evidence may appear only when release policy permits; otherwise a student-created summary can describe learning without revealing the protected scenario. Display does not alter evidence or recognition.

## Step 58 progression
Performance recognition combines explicit criterion evidence across a submitted portfolio and authorized review. Scenario evidence is one possible artifact, not automatic recognition or permanent mastery.

Understand identifies and explains a principle. Apply uses it in a realistic decision and recommends action. Create designs or evaluates a workflow, mitigation, fairness test, data plan, or system response. Vocabulary recall and choice without reasoning cannot demonstrate Apply or Create. Applied Independently requires a valid Step 52 condition. Evidence is versioned and linked intentionally after configured review; opening or completing scenarios never awards competency, creates rank, or claims permanent mastery.
