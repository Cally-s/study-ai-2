# Scenario Source Verification

Source scenarios reuse Step 43 trusted records or sources visibly labelled **Fictional practice source** and **Do not use in a bibliography.** Existence, identity, quotation presence, citation match, currentness, primary/secondary status, and exact claim support remain separate conclusions. Approval does not prove claim support; search snippets are not sources; invented citations are blocked; and no reliable source found leaves a claim unresolved.
