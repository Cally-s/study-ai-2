# Performance Assessments

## Step 60 integration
Submitted Understand, Apply, and Create artifacts are authentic badge evidence when mapped to explicit criteria. Completion, artifact quantity, prototype polish, source count, or one global score is never enough.

## Purpose
Performance Assessments ask students to demonstrate AI-literacy competencies through authentic decisions, evidence, revisions, designs, prototypes, testing, and reflection—not through vocabulary recall or activity completion alone.

The three pathways are **Understand Assessment**, **Apply Assessment**, and **Create Assessment**. Vocabulary may prepare learners, but Apply and Create require authentic evidence. Assessment examines both product and process: verification, revision, feedback response, limitations, contribution, and disclosure.

AI may draft criterion evaluations and feedback, but authorized humans decide formal recognition and grades. Results remain criterion-level and submission-specific; no opaque global score, rank, authorship claim, misconduct inference, or permanent mastery is created.
# Step 64 offline preparation

Packages may support problem framing, workflow, source plans, safe data, paper/mock prototype descriptions, reflection, and accessible evidence drafting. They exclude protected evaluation logic/answers and cannot grade, publish, deploy, or recognize competency offline. Authenticated sync validates rubric/activity/policy/safeguard versions before intentional submission.
