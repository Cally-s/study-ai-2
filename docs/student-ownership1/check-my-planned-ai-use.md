# Check My Planned AI Use

> **Step 50 AI Use Receipt contract:** Meaningful validated events—not transcripts, clicks, timing, accessibility, safety, or model recollection—compose an immutable private receipt. A separate physically redacted shareable version preserves the exact eleven labels, policy/map snapshots, AI-versus-student provenance, preview and intentional recipient confirmation, immutable share history, and no automatic sharing, scoring, surveillance, or discipline.


The student selects the task, requested use/variant, purpose, and intended contribution. The server resolves policy and presents guidance, actual status, conditions, disclosure, tools, final-answer rule, and safer alternatives. The student may choose permitted support, add an attempt, review policy/disclosure, ask a teacher, pause, or decline AI. Clarification is an editable unsent draft. Planned use remains private and distinct from actual assistance; it creates no receipt, alert, flag, or misconduct evidence.
