# AI Use Receipt Contributions

> **Step 51 Teach-Back contract:** Teach It Back is one policy-controlled Step 47 final thinking activity with five varied types, explicit server-held criteria, immutable student-provenance attempts, response-level evidence and concise formative feedback. It preserves answer withholding, essential terms, multilingual/access formats, retry/human support, privacy, and no automatic grade, mastery/ability label, copying accusation, misconduct inference, scoring, surveillance, sharing, checkpoint completion, or full-response receipt disclosure.


AI contribution describes validated actions such as explanation, hinting, feedback, source work, debugging, image/model-answer generation, verification, or disclosure; it distinguishes retrieval from verification, feedback from rewriting, and debugging from complete generation. Student contribution uses validated provenance for attempts, ideas, drafts, code, sources, tests, revisions, decisions, accepted/rejected suggestions, and disclosure review. AI text is never student-authored. Keystrokes, speed, clicks, detectors, and surveillance are excluded; no authorship or contribution score is produced.
