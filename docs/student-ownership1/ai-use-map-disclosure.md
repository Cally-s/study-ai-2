# AI Use Map Disclosure

> **Step 50 AI Use Receipt contract:** Meaningful validated events—not transcripts, clicks, timing, accessibility, safety, or model recollection—compose an immutable private receipt. A separate physically redacted shareable version preserves the exact eleven labels, policy/map snapshots, AI-versus-student provenance, preview and intentional recipient confirmation, immutable share history, and no automatic sharing, scoring, surveillance, or discipline.


Conditions can require disclosure, an AI Use Receipt, student contribution, source verification, or review. A receipt is created only after meaningful assistance, never from opening the map or planning. Suggested disclosure distinguishes AI and student contributions, excludes accessibility presentation use and safety content, remains private by default, and is reviewed/edited by the student before intentional sharing. No teacher, parent, tutor, or administrator is notified automatically.
