# AI Use Receipt Corrections

Trusted assignment, event, source, policy, mode, and timestamps are system facts; purpose, privacy-safe summaries, disclosure narrative, inclusion, and redaction are student-controlled layers. “Report an Inaccuracy” records the field, reason, and proposed correction. Trusted event validation determines whether a new receipt version is created. The original, underlying private record, correction request, and prior shared snapshots remain unchanged and historically available under retention policy.
