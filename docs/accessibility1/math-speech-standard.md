# Math Speech Standard

Use semantic math before raw visual text. Common English rules expand squared, cubed, powers, roots, plus, minus, equals, multiplication, and division. Required example: `x² + 4x + 4` becomes “x squared plus four x plus four.”

Unsupported or ambiguous notation must not be guessed. Keep the visible equation and provide an explicit mathematical-expression fallback. Non-English math speech remains partial pending locale-specific rules and expert review.
