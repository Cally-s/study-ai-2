# Translation Authoring Standard

Step 24 requires human language, RTL and academic-equivalence review; automated preservation checks do not establish translation quality.

Translations are independent drafts generated from the original version. English academic terms, formulas, code, course codes, numbers, dates, units, conditions, warnings, citations and assessment restrictions remain exact where required. Language metadata and RTL review are mandatory. Active/restricted assessments and hidden answer material cannot be exported. Human language review precedes publication.
