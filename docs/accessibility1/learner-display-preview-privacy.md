# Learner Display Preview Privacy

Allowed fields are text size, contrast, reduced motion, captions preference, text-only participation, explanation languages, explanation level, and Low-Bandwidth Mode. Diagnosis, medical information, learning-difficulty information, accommodation records/documents, full profiles, transcripts, recordings and reasons are excluded. Production requires teacher/tutor authorization, active sharing grant, purpose, expiry, revocation on every read, and sensitive-access history. Preview is read-only and does not modify or impersonate the learner.
