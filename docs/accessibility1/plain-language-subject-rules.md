# Plain-Language Subject Rules

- Mathematics: preserve visible expressions, symbols, conditions, proof/reasoning steps and units; never reveal an active answer.
- Science: preserve relationships, uncertainty, quantities, units, named processes and safety warnings.
- Coding: preserve code blocks, identifiers, syntax, order, preconditions and error conditions.
- Language arts: preserve quotations, author/title, evidence, interpretive qualification and citation.
- History and social sciences: preserve dates, names, chronology, cause/effect, perspective and uncertainty.
- Business: preserve amounts, percentages, units, assumptions and conditions.
- Language learning: identify formal academic terms and do not present translation as simplification.
- High-stakes content: keep authoritative text; only an authorized human-reviewed alternate may act as official wording.
