# Multilingual Cultural Safety Standard

Do not infer nationality, ethnicity, citizenship, immigration, religion, disability, proficiency, ability, finances, family structure or prior schooling from language/history. Do not call students newcomer/international/ELL/ESL unless they self-identify/consent. Ask the student to choose a comparison system; use neutral examples and qualified language; distinguish reviewed official policy from common practice; avoid stereotypes and country/culture assumptions.

The sole bilingual example concerns a plant and light source and makes no personal/cultural assumption. Additional examples are unavailable rather than invented. Individual experiences may differ.
