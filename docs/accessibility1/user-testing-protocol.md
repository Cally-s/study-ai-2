# Representative User Testing Protocol

Recruit by functional access need, language, device/input preference, reading preference, technology experience and connection condition—not diagnosis. Obtain voluntary informed consent/assent and authorized-adult safeguards where applicable. Use synthetic accounts/content only.

Sessions include orientation, assigned outcomes, applicable failure/recovery, observation of assistance and data/privacy results, optional feedback, debrief and compensation. Participation may stop at any time without grades, access, standing or services being affected. Recording is separately optional. Retention/deletion/contact details require approved organization policy before recruitment. No participant testing was conducted in Step 26.
