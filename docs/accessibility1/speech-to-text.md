# Speech-to-Text

STT requires intentional microphone activation and visible/announced status. Interim text is labelled and not assessed. A final editable transcript is reviewed and corrected, then Step 61 Privacy Check runs where relevant before intentional confirmation and submission. Raw audio is not retained by default. Voice is not used to infer age, disability, identity, emotion, honesty, accent quality, or fluency. Equivalent assessment responses remain eligible.
