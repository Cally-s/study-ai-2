# Live Captions

Live and prerecorded speech provides synchronized, keyboard-accessible captions with language, speaker labels, punctuation, academic terms, meaningful sounds, and low-confidence markers. Translated live captions display a machine-translation notice until reviewed. Captions do not obscure controls or rely on colour. Errors are correctable before assessment and never automatically become student errors. Text-only activities explicitly use Not Applicable; speech media also requires a final transcript.
