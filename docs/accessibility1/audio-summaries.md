# Audio Summaries

An Audio Summary may contain objective, concept, required terms, one example, limitation, source/policy reminder, and next action. It is user initiated, controllable, pronunciation-aware, validated, and always has a transcript. It never reveals protected answers, adds unsupported claims, omits material conditions, replaces necessary full policy/rubric/source review, or retains raw student audio. The full lesson remains available.
# Step 64 downloads

Permitted audio summaries may be downloaded with displayed size, language, term/pronunciation versions, transcript, source/policy preservation, expiry, and package integrity. Audio never autoplays or becomes the only required form, and downloaded files contain no student identity metadata.
