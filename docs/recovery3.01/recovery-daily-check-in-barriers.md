# Daily Check-In Barriers

The nine choices are Task Took Longer, Did Not Understand, Ran Out of Time, Materials Unavailable, Lower Energy Than Expected, Another Responsibility, Forgot, Something Else, and Prefer Not to Explain.

No explanation or diagnosis is required. Time overrun can update only the remaining estimate; misunderstanding can route to instruction/prerequisite help; time/energy/responsibility revalidates capacity; missing materials keeps dependent work blocked; Forgot may offer an optional reminder but never enables one automatically. Custom text is short, private, sanitized, and safety screened.
