# Recovery Action Quality Standard

Ten-minute selection requires `VALID` quality plus a visible stop point and confirmed capacity. A short duration alone never makes a vague or blocked action eligible.

The deterministic gate rejects vague openings such as “work on,” “continue,” “study,” “finish” and “make progress.” An action needs a controlled verb, specific object, observable completion, valid duration, explicit dependencies, a verified requirement link, permitted academic-integrity type and no duplicate.

Statuses are `VALID`, `TOO_VAGUE`, `TOO_LARGE`, `TOO_SMALL_AND_FRAGMENTED`, `MISSING_COMPLETION_CONDITION`, `MISSING_REQUIREMENT_SOURCE`, `BLOCKED`, `DUPLICATE`, `ACADEMIC_INTEGRITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and `OUTDATED`. Only `VALID` actions may enter an active plan after review. Unknown requirements remain unknown; templates cannot establish actual assignment requirements.
