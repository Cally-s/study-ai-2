# Blocked Accessibility Support

Use functional needs without diagnosis: accessible format, TTS/STT, captions, readable display, keyboard, or existing approved time/break/AT/reduced-distraction support. Immediate preferences and available accessible alternatives may apply now.

Official assessment time/location/method/requirements or unapproved technology require authorized review. StudySpark explains the process, prepares a reviewed message, or routes authorized staff; it never approves/invents accommodations, diagnoses disability, or attaches medical documents automatically.
