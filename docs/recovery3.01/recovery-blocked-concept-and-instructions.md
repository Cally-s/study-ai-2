# Blocked Concepts and Instructions

Current-concept problems may use one explanation/worked example/clarification. Earlier-skill evidence routes to bounded Step 20 prerequisite repair. After two or three distinct unsuccessful approaches, route to teacher/verified tutor. Never solve graded/active-assessment content or declare mastery.

Instruction blockers review authoritative text, retain source/version/uncertainty, provide a plain-language summary, highlight one unclear section, compare a confirmed rubric/example, then prepare a Step 22 draft if ambiguity remains. Missing requirements, methods, rubric rules, and dates are never invented.
