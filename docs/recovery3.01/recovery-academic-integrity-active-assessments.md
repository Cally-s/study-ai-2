# Active-Assessment Protection

Context and assessment-session status are reviewed before substantial assistance. Active quiz/test/exam, timed take-home, oral, practical and supervised assessment rules override general homework labels. Open book does not imply AI allowed. Answer-bearing hints, choice elimination, active-answer review, full code/proof, tutor live answers and exact solutions are unavailable. Permitted process-neutral technical/accessibility support remains. Uncertainty redirects to teacher clarification; released/returned work may be reviewed under current policy afterward.
