# Missed Month Privacy

Gap dates/reason, partial attendance, course status, barriers, work/replacement/support decisions, accommodation references, messages, meetings, capacity, plans and history are owner-private. Public, teacher, tutor, parent and guardian serializers expose nothing by default. Sharing is recipient/field-specific, editable and explicitly confirmed. AI receives only authorized minimum functional/context data. Raw data cannot enter grades, discipline, tutor/service/Help Credits, safety/fraud/integrity scoring, advertising, notifications or ordinary analytics.
