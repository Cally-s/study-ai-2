# Blocked Waiting and Review

Waiting types identify teacher/tutor/group/technical/accessibility/school/guardian/material/website/acceptance/other. Drafted, Ready, Sent, Waiting, Received, Review Required, Resolved, Follow-up Recommended/Declined, No Response, Outdated, and Cancelled remain distinct.

Every unresolved blocker has an exact review point when known or explicit waiting/unscheduled/support state. At review, recheck requirement/deadline/other resolution; keep academic work off READY_NOW; show another action/Finish. No repeated reminders/follow-ups, blame, or unchanged daily Minimum blocker. Resolution evidence is required before reactivation review.
