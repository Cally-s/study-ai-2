# Blocked Human Support

Available support may include teacher, verified tutor, school counsellor/support, trusted adult, parent/guardian, technical/accessibility staff, or group member. The student chooses; requests/messages/summaries require review and explicit action. No booking, notification, accommodation request, or parent summary is automatic.

Emotional/personal support requires no disclosure. Immediate-safety language pauses academics and routes to Step 21; the blocker workflow is not the only response, and safety content never enters ordinary academic/shared records.
