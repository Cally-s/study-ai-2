# Recovery Capacity Intake

Step 6 asks for real usable time, never idealized free time. Full Recovery covers exactly seven local calendar dates starting with today; Quick Rescue covers today and the relevant next-day portion without forcing a weekly form. Exact dates and a validated timezone are stored.

Students may enter after-commitment daily totals, typed time windows, commitment-assisted estimates, authorized-calendar suggestions, conservative estimates or Unknown. Quick choices include 10, 20, 45 and 90 minutes, No Available Time and I Am Not Sure. No-time is confirmed zero; unsure remains neither zero nor schedulable. Direct totals are not reduced again by commitments.

Commitments, focus block, concentration, breaks, setup, variability, buffer and allowlisted functional supports are editable. The review separates stated availability, planning envelope, explicit breaks/setup, focused-task estimate and protected buffer. Completion creates no final schedule, calendar edit or message. Local user-scoped drafts support pause/resume; production persistence, trusted clock/calendar and manual accessibility evidence remain unavailable.
