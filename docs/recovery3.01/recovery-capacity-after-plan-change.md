# Capacity After a Plan Change

Remaining schedulable time equals confirmed focused capacity minus already approved remaining Minimum, required breaks, and setup/transition not already included. Completed/removed/replaced work and previously counted breaks/setup are not subtracted again.

Sleep, fixed commitments, breaks, and protected buffer are hard constraints. Buffer Would Be Required means Does Not Fit. No-capacity days receive no new work; uncertain days remain provisional. Increased capacity is not filled automatically, and Stretch appears only after Minimum stability and student selection.
