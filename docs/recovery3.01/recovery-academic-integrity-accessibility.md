# Academic Integrity Accessibility

Context, policy, contribution, boundary, citation and review flows use keyboard/screen-reader structure, plain language, Language Bridge, TTS and reviewed STT. English integrity terminology and status/negation/authority meaning remain visible and equivalent. Typed text, STT transcript, choices, ordered steps, highlighted evidence, formulas and uploads are equal contribution methods. Translation, grammar support, accommodations, screen readers, TTS/STT and bilingual use are not misconduct signals. No disability inference or diagnosis is collected.
