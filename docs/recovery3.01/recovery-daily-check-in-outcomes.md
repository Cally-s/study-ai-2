# Daily Check-In Outcomes

- Completed preserves validated completion and offers Finish for Today or tomorrow preparation without revealing Stretch.
- Partly Completed explicitly counts as progress, preserves completed subactions, and works only with the remaining portion.
- Not Started uses neutral language and offers a small setup, material retrieval, help, or Finish for Today.
- Blocked means information/support is needed, never failure; routing may use materials, clarification, prerequisite help, teacher decision, verified tutor, another ready task, or Finish.
- Waiting for Someone records teacher/tutor/school/guardian/group/material-access dependencies without calling it inaction or sending follow-ups.

There is no Failed, Noncompliant, Insufficient Effort, Unproductive, Failed Day, Missed Day, or Unproductive Day state. Optional success states describe supported progress and are never scores.
