# Recovery Account Governance

The supported modes are Personal, School-Managed, Organization-Managed, Supervised Minor, Guest or Ephemeral, and Governance Unknown. Unknown fails cautiously. Personal accounts preserve optional student choices; managed accounts display the responsible organization and precise required purposes; supervised accounts separate feature authorization from content access; guest sessions explain retention, lack of sync, and export-before-exit.

The effective policy notice includes version, required storage and retention, institutional roles, purposes, additional authorization, ordinary notice, correction/deletion rights, and contact. The interface never promises “Only you can ever see this” when a validated policy authorizes narrower institutional access.
