# Daily Check-In Replanning

Validated completion and partial products remain preserved. Original estimates remain in history; only remaining estimates may change. Minimum and Stretch remain separate, and Stretch reduces before Minimum when capacity falls. Sleep and protected buffer are never consumed.

Incomplete work is not rolled to tomorrow automatically. A move requires an exact target date, current capacity validation, displaced-work and urgency visibility, options to move/split/leave unscheduled/cancel, and explicit confirmation. Preview never activates a plan. Urgent work that cannot fit remains visible for teacher/support decisions.
