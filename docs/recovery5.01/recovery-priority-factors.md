# Recovery Priority Factors

The six allowlisted factors are urgency 0–5, academic impact 0–5, prerequisite importance 0–4, recoverability 0–3, student importance 0–3, and immediate usefulness 0–2. Every value retains source and confirmation status. Unknown, conflicting and stale inputs remain explicit and never become zero.

Deadlines alone do not establish acceptance. Grade weight alone does not establish importance. Student importance is voluntary and changeable. Diagnosis, disability, language, nationality, race, religion, gender, income, immigration status, voice, typing speed, response latency, notification behavior, missed days, recalculations, parent involvement, motivation, effort, responsibility, honesty, resilience, potential and predicted failure are prohibited factors.
