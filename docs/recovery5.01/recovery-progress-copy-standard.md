# Recovery Progress Copy Standard

Use one or two sentences naming the specific verified action and why it matters. Partial progress remains partial. Suggested structure: “You [specific action]. That [specific recovery value].”

Never use peer/sibling comparison, rank, percentile, streak/loss language, judgment about effort/motivation/character, unsupported completion/mastery/grade claims, sensitive disclosures, safety gamification, excessive hype, forced emoji, or repeated generic praise.

Examples: “You clarified the deadline. That makes the next decision easier.” “You completed one small, observable action. The remaining work is more manageable.” “You asked for help before the deadline. That is an important recovery skill.”
