# Seven-Day Plan Privacy

Dates, daily/block order, levels, capacity, breaks, buffer/reserve, no-work days, teacher/repair actions, progress, changes, notes and history are private. Public, teacher, tutor, parent and guardian serializers expose nothing automatically.

Later sharing/calendar export requires exact recipient/calendar/section preview, editing and explicit confirmation. Analytics contain allow-listed event/time only—not titles, dates tied to identity, order, durations, capacity, buffer, notes, reasons or Stretch noncompletion.

Plan data cannot affect grades, discipline, tutoring, service hours, Help Credits, safety, fraud, integrity, ads, badges or streaks. AI may word validated placement reasons but cannot assign days or change facts/capacity.
