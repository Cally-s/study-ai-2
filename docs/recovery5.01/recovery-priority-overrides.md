# Recovery Priority Overrides

The student may change a recommended priority. StudySpark should show the likely effect before applying the change.

Students may request another category because of personal importance, new information, a personal commitment, teacher direction, dependency context, wellbeing, an accessibility need, or another private reason. Reasons are stored privately and excluded from ordinary analytics and role serializers.

Overrides cannot hide the system recommendation. A provisional item under 90 minutes may enter Do Now only as provisional. Substantial or unknown-duration provisional work is denied by the safety rule until critical facts are verified. Overrides cannot send a message, release or delete work, use protected time, change official status, or create a final schedule.

Move up and Move down controls provide non-drag reordering. Optimistic version checks protect importance changes, and every mutation uses an idempotency key. Source or policy changes require recalculation; restoring the system recommendation is represented by recalculating current rules.

Step 10 requires a server-derived impact preview and explicit confirmation before calling this override path. It preserves system-versus-student comparison, private reason codes, capacity warnings, undo/restore and history.
