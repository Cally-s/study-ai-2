# Recovery Release Gates

Each stage/scope requires Product, Data, Privacy, Security, Accessibility, Academic Integrity, Safety, Quality and Operations gates. Statuses are Not Reviewed, In Progress, Passed, Passed with Limitations, Blocked, Invalidated or Outdated.

Passed states require evidence references, reviewer identity/time and policy version. Any missing or nonpassing gate blocks release; Critical issues cannot be waived through feature flags. Automated evidence and manual evidence remain distinct. Current manual accessibility, production security/data, migration, build and operations gates are blocked or not reviewed.
