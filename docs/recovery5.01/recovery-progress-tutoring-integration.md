# Recovery Progress Tutoring Integration

A tutor recommendation, request, matching result, booking, and link are not attendance. Recognition requires a verified session attendance record and may accurately mention the reviewed session outcome. It does not expose tutor notes or learner diagnostics.

Booking creates no attendance, service hours, or Help Credits. Attendance still does not approve official service hours; those require the existing verifier process. Help Credits remain a separate ledger and are never awarded for recovery recognition.
