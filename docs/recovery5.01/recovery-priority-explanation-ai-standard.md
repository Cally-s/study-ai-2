# Recovery Priority Explanation AI Standard

AI wording is optional and unavailable in this prototype. Deterministic structured claims remain the source of truth. A future provider may receive only validated category, rank, reason codes, minimal facts, verification labels, capacity summary, selected depth/language and a sanitized private reason only when required.

Structured output must preserve category, rank, dates, durations, percentages, uncertainty, sources, capacity and release safeguards. It may improve readability or produce reviewed plain-language/bilingual variants; it may not decide facts, categories, rank, releases or messages.

Every generated claim must validate against source claims. Invalid output produces Generation Error and the deterministic fallback remains visible. No private chain-of-thought, prompts, unstructured deliberation, unrelated schedules, diagnoses, family/employer details, safety/fraud data, hidden answers or credentials are sent or shown.
