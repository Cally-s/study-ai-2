# Recovery Prerequisite Privacy

Readiness answers, diagnostic responses, evidence, bridge history, disagreements, and student notes are owner-private by default. Public, parent/guardian, teacher, and tutor serializers expose nothing automatically. Analytics use allow-listed event names without concepts, answers, task titles, notes, diagnoses, or identifiers.

Teacher questions and tutor help are unsent drafts. Sharing requires student approval and authorized role checks. Full diagnostics and private notes are excluded. The prototype uses memory storage and does not claim production encryption, retention, deletion, or cross-device sync.
