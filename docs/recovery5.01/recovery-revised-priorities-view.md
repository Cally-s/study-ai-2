# Revised Priorities View

Default results show at most three source-labelled, actionable, duration-ranged priorities; one next smallest action; brief What Changed; teacher contact when needed; Finish for Today; and Save/Pause. Overwhelmed Mode shows one priority and hides capacity detail behind a control.

Deferred-work disclosure is collapsed. The full backlog/calendar, Stretch, scores, productivity metrics, every estimate/change/dependency, and private reasons remain hidden. Student review and approval are required.
