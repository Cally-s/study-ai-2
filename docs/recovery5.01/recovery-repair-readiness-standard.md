# Recovery Repair Readiness Standard

Ready to Continue requires successful guided work plus a correct independent or valid self-corrected check with no remaining instruction/material barrier. Ready with Support distinguishes light hints or minor correction. One More Example is appropriate for a consistent bounded mistake. Tutor or teacher help is recommended for unclear, interacting, conflicting or method-specific evidence. Skipped, technically interrupted or conflicting evidence is inconclusive.

Student preference can make the outcome more supportive. A student asking for help is never forced back to the task. A student who feels ready may try provisionally with the reminder card while the system recommendation remains preserved.

No outcome is a grade, diagnosis, deficit score or permanent mastery claim. Use “ready for the current task,” not “mastered.”
