# Recovery Priority Hard Gates

Completed, accepted and authoritatively not-required work is excluded before scoring. Active-assessment restrictions, blockers, waiting, unknown late-work acceptance, unknown requirements, teacher decisions, missing material, unclear instructions and group dependencies route to Ask First. A release candidate remains Consider Releasing until authoritative evidence and student review; AI never marks No Longer Required.

A high-priority blocked task belongs in Ask First. The short action that resolves the blocker may become Do Now. Substantial work with unknown acceptance is never scheduled. Gate reasons, missing source and unlocking action remain visible, and gate resolution triggers recalculation rather than silently restoring an old result.
