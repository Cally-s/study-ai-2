# Requirement Reconfirmation

Current Required, Late Required, Accepted, and Conditionally Accepted may continue within their confirmed conditions. Optional moves to Stretch/unscheduled. Replaced uses only a confirmed replacement. No Longer Required and Not Accepted leave the active private plan while history remains. Unknown, Conflicting, Stale, and Needs Teacher Confirmation move to Ask First/source review; substantial work is not scheduled.

Teacher, official instructions/policy, authorized LMS, or authorized school staff may confirm requirement/acceptance. Students confirm their progress/materials/time/preferences. Tutors and AI cannot confirm official requirement, acceptance, replacement, extension, or removal.
