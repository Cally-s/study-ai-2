# Recovery Priority Explanation Privacy

Explanations, claims, factors, sources, capacity summaries, importance, disagreements, private reasons, override history, feedback and release-review explanations are owner-only recovery data. Public, teacher, tutor, parent and guardian serializers return empty views; notification previews and ordinary analytics receive none of this content.

Private reasons and feedback are sanitized, never used for diagnosis, discipline, motivation/compliance scoring, grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, safety, fraud, integrity or advertising, and are excluded from public logs. Later sharing requires an exact editable preview, named recipient and explicit confirmation.

The deterministic engine needs no AI provider. Offline cached explanations refresh no school facts and do not apply official changes. Production needs authenticated authorization, organization isolation, encrypted persistence, retention/export/deletion, shared-device cleanup, durable idempotency/concurrency and privacy-reviewed provider controls.
