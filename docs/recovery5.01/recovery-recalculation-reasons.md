# Recalculation Reasons

Supported reasons reuse Daily Check-In: time overrun, understanding problem, time shortage, missing materials, lower energy, another responsibility, forgot, waiting, something else, and Prefer Not to Explain; technical interruption may be reviewed separately.

Reasons select adaptations, not blame. Lower energy requires no cause/diagnosis. Responsibilities require no private details. Forgot creates no motivation inference or automatic reminder. Custom text is optional, short, private, sanitized, and safety screened.
