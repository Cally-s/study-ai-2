# Recovery Priority Uncertainty

Confirmed, estimated, assumed, unknown, conflicting, stale and not-applicable data remain distinct. Unknown values produce a range or a hard gate; they are not zero. A recommendation is labelled provisional whenever material uncertainty could change its category or order.

The explanation states what is unknown, what safe action can confirm it, which authority is required and what could change. Offline cached decisions retain their source watermark and cannot claim refreshed facts or current recalculation.
