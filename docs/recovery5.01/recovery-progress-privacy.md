# Recovery Progress Privacy

History is private to the student. Parent, teacher, tutor, counsellor, verifier, public-profile, class-feed, school-feed, notification-preview, ordinary analytics, and public-log serializers receive no recognition content. Separate student approval of a factual progress item does not expose the private recognition message or history.

Raw diagnostics, emotional/safety details, disability/medical data, teacher content, tutor notes, grades, and contacts are excluded. Recognition does not affect grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, fraud, integrity, discipline, safety, or advertising. Production requires retention, export, deletion, shared-device cleanup, encryption, and AI-provider minimization.
