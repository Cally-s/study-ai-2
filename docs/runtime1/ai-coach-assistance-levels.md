# AI Coach Assistance Levels

> Step 67 integration: the capability intersection begins from the immutable Core Decision Result. Every denied capability stays absent from provider/tool contracts; deny overrides allow, active-test and AI-not-allowed rules outrank guided defaults, and accessibility remains outside the academic ladder.

The academic ladder runs from no academic assistance through clarification, concept explanation, guiding questions, hints, similar examples, attempt checking, revision feedback, verification/reflection, practice model answers after an attempt, and explicitly permitted final answers. Accessibility functions and protected human workflows sit outside the academic ladder.

The server intersects platform, safeguard, organization, policy, context, active-assessment, selected-mode, attempt, and tool/source capability sets. It chooses the highest level whose required capabilities are all present; it does not take a numeric minimum. The mode may reduce but never increase permission. Restricted final answers are prohibited during generation—they are not secretly generated and hidden later.
