# AI Coach Confidence and Assumptions

> Step 67 integration: Low and Insufficient Evidence must include both a support-based reason and concrete next step. High-impact uncertainty may require human support. No percentage, student judgment, or claim that “low means false” is permitted.

Confidence values are High, Medium, Low, Insufficient Evidence, or Not Applicable. They describe the strength of available support—not personality, probability of correctness, or student ability. No percentages are used. High requires direct coherent support; unresolved sourced claims cannot receive High.

Material assumptions are concise, visible, and student-correctable. Protected traits and judgments about honesty or motivation are prohibited. “This Assumption Is Wrong,” “Add Missing Context,” and “Recalculate or Regenerate” begin a new run and preserve the earlier response.
