# AI Coach Source Flow

> Step 67 integration: externally verifiable claims require retrieval or an unverified label. Citation capability is removed until metadata, identity, and existence are confirmed; the no-source rule instead requires an explicit reasoning or verification method.

Step 9 explicitly resolves sources as required, applicable, not needed, supplied-content only, cached/pending, unavailable, or human-review required. Search queries are separate, privacy checked, and stripped of unnecessary student identifiers and assignment detail.

Retrieval confirms existence, identity, author/organization, publisher, date, currentness, relevant content, exact claim support, quotation and citation metadata, disagreement, and limitations. Search snippets and plausible citations are rejected. Source presence alone does not prove support. Unsupported candidate claims are removed or visibly qualified. Cached offline sources remain `Verification pending internet access` and cannot become formal evidence.
