# AI Literacy Diagnostic

The optional AI Literacy Learning Check is a private, low-pressure 5–10 minute activity with approximately eight questions. It recommends useful practice; it does not rank, diagnose, certify, grade, or permanently classify a student.

It samples Privacy Protection, Unsupported Claim Recognition, Prompt Purpose and Agency, Verification Judgement, Assignment-Assistance Boundaries, Bias and Training Data, Source Selection, and AI-Use Disclosure. Students can skip the check, pause, retake, revise, revisit, select “I Am Not Sure Yet,” use hints, and choose any pathway—including Create. The in-memory prototype has no durable database or production server authorization.

Diagnostic observations may become private competency evidence candidates, but are capped at Practising and never prove all competencies.

Diagnostic suggestions may link to a structured lesson; they never skip lesson phases or pre-fill student reasoning.

AI Foundations suggestions may link to What AI Is and Is Not without assigning a level or pathway.
# Step 6 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend the Human Agency lesson but does not inspect private lesson responses, plans, reflections, or disclosures and does not infer misconduct or authorship.
# Step 7 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend unsupported-output practice but cannot inspect private searches, corrected answers, or investigation records, and cannot produce a truth or research-ability score.
# Step 8 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend bias-audit practice but never infers identity, prejudice, fairness awareness, or sensitive traits and cannot inspect private audits or redesigns.
# Step 9 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend privacy practice but cannot inspect raw prompts/findings or assign privacy/safety/carelessness labels.
# Step 10 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend ownership practice but cannot classify real work, infer misconduct/authorship, or inspect private plans/disclosures.
# Step 11 boundary

The diagnostic may recommend policy-awareness practice but cannot infer compliance/misconduct or inspect private sources/messages.
