# Diagnostic Feedback

The initial response is preserved separately from an optional revised response. Hints clarify without immediately revealing the answer and carry no penalty. Feedback explains the applicable principle, missing evidence, a related idea, and a next learning action in neutral language.

Responses may be kept, revised, skipped, or marked unsure. Revision is learning, not cheating. There is no pass/fail state, red failure treatment, negative sound, timer, or shame wording.
