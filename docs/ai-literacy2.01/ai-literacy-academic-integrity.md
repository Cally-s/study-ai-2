# AI Literacy and Academic Integrity

> AI may organize Step 28 questions but cannot decide the ethical conclusion. Suggestions require student review, evidence links, uncertainty, and an AI Use Receipt.

StudySpark can explain, guide, review, and help a student practise. It will not complete graded work or represent AI-generated work as the student’s own.

The self-check is neutral: it does not assign an integrity score, claim detector proof, or automatically report a learner. Active-assessment assistance is restricted. Unknown policy means ask the teacher. Disclosure is offered when required, while authorship remains with the learner.

Diagnostic selections are formative observations only. They never trigger an accusation, detector validation, teacher report, discipline, or misconduct prediction.

Answer-replacing, active-assessment-restricted, or invalid evidence cannot support Demonstrated or Applied Independently and triggers coaching, not accusation.

Each lesson declares an Integrity mode; real assignment contexts load applicable policy, and fictional practice remains available.

What AI Is and Is Not uses GENERAL_LEARNING and never infers authorship or misconduct from digital content or writing style.
# Unsupported AI Output lesson

Step 7 is preventive education, not misconduct detection. It never completes graded research, investigates active-test answers, treats a detector or missing citation as proof, reports a student automatically, or invents that a source was checked. Formal decisions require human review.
# Step 8 boundary

The bias lesson does not rank real students/candidates, complete graded work, infer identity or ability, or make legal/fairness findings. High-impact conclusions require appropriate human review.
# Prompt privacy boundary

Privacy findings never create misconduct cases or automatic teacher/parent/tutor/admin reports. Separate safeguarding rules require authorized human review.
# Ownership coaching boundary

StudySpark may coach process evaluation but never makes final cheating, plagiarism, authorship, originality, or disciplinary decisions; those require appropriate human review.
# Policy awareness boundary

Policy Unknown restricts submission generation but never proves violation. StudySpark cannot decide misconduct, discipline, eligibility, examination, scholarship, competition, or publication outcomes.
# Step 12

Prompt wording cannot override assignment policy, active-assessment restrictions, privacy, safety, or student-attempt rules. The Coach rejects fabricated citations, hidden-reasoning requests, system-prompt extraction, and “make it undetectable.”

Prompt iteration cannot disguise or authorize prohibited help. Students must change the type of support, preserve their attempt, or stop—not merely reword a restricted request.

Answer verification supports research and learning but cannot complete restricted work, answer active assessments, change ownership/permission, or automate formal misconduct decisions/reports.

Source comparison supports evidence decisions but does not complete research or submissions, create ownership/permission, or certify truth.

## Step 16 — Claim–Evidence Map

Claim mapping may organize and revise research reasoning but cannot invent sources/evidence, generate a final restricted argument, hide unsupported claims, or submit work.

## Step 17 — AI Citation and Disclosure

Step 17 requires accurate contribution disclosure but does not determine misconduct, prove integrity, authorize restricted use, or submit work automatically.

## Step 18 — AI Brainstorming

Step 18 cannot generate final theses, outlines, products, reflections, assessed code, or active-assessment answers and cannot move ideas into submissions automatically.

## Step 19 — AI Feedback

Step 19 cannot rewrite/apply student work, invent evidence/rubrics, produce full counterarguments/reflections, or provide answer-bearing feedback on restricted assessments.

## Step 20 — Revision With Judgment

Step 20 cannot auto-apply/submit feedback, fabricate explanations/verification/authorship, validate AI claims automatically, or reward acceptance/rejection.

## Community projects

Preserve student authorship, sources, versions, AI contributions, and the AI Use Receipt. Never fabricate research, interviews, testing, consent, or deployment.
