# Competency Privacy

The map, evidence, summaries, artifacts, reviews, history, and next actions are private by default and excluded from ordinary logs/analytics/model training. Public, teacher, tutor, parent, and administrator views exclude individual evidence unless a future governed share is explicitly authorized.

Eligible evidence can be deleted; affected status is recalculated and history retained. Production encryption, retention, access-history, backup expiry, and durable deletion need server infrastructure.
