# Competency Accessibility

The map supports keyboard navigation, semantic tables/cards, screen-reader text statuses, visible focus, skip links, 320-pixel reflow, high contrast, forced colours, Reduced Motion, RTL, text-only, and low bandwidth. Text-to-Speech and bilingual controls inherit platform conventions.

Accessibility, translation, and approved accommodations never reduce independence. Manual AT, zoom, speech, and translation validation remain not run.
