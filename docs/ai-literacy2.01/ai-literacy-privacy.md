# AI Literacy Privacy

> **Step 42 integration:** Substantive educational help is presented through the validated Standard AI Response Card in the exact eight-section order. Trusted mode, policy, ladder, checkpoint, source, confidence-basis, verification, next-step, and AI-contribution data remain authoritative; the card creates no permission, exposes no hidden reasoning, uses no numerical confidence, and cannot leak a prohibited answer.

> **Step 41 integration:** Student-Contribution Checkpoints record intentional, policy-authorized student decisions and artifacts across the exact nine-stage workflow. They preserve provenance, privacy, accessibility neutrality, Step 37–40 and learning-mode restrictions, final-answer gates, and immutable history without surveillance, authorship scoring, or automatic discipline.

> Intervention metadata excludes request/draft text, safety content, accessibility use, disability data, automatic reports/alerts, and ability, motivation, honesty, dependence, cheating-risk, grade, or potential profiles.

> Ladder level, escalation, hint, attempt, revision, verification, timing, accessibility use, and model-answer requests remain private and cannot create ability, dependence, honesty, disability, cheating-risk, grade, or potential profiles.

> Policy logs and teacher review expose only authorized selected artifacts; full conversations, accessibility usage, safety content, hidden reasoning, and profiling signals remain excluded.

> Context selections, policy declarations, limited tasks, conflicts, and corrections remain private and cannot create honesty, misconduct, motivation, intelligence, disability, politics, grade, or potential profiles.

> Adaptive quiz evidence is session-scoped and private; response time, pauses, notes, hints, language, device, and accessibility support cannot become permanent profiles, grades, ranks, motivation, honesty, misconduct, or potential signals.

> Challenge content and version history use private references and cannot produce ability, dependence, honesty, political, religious, moral, diagnosis, disability, misconduct, grade, or potential profiles.

> Reasoning submissions/reviews/revisions use private message/version references and never create permanent error, uncertainty, provenance, or misconception profiles.

> Assignment text, skill profiles, generated practice and student transfer remain private and stored by reference/minimum metadata where possible.

> Student thinking summaries store explicit response elements only and never hidden reasoning, unnecessary personal content, or permanent misconceptions.

> Progressive state uses a problem-context hash and minimum disclosure elements; action events exclude private problem text and permanent learner traits.

> Contract enforcement stores no hidden answer repository; cumulative disclosure uses minimum structured concepts/operations and a privacy-safe context hash.

> Learning-mode choices are private interaction preferences and cannot be used to infer ability, motivation, disability, mental health, honesty, or future performance.

> Step 28 reflection text is private student work by default; public-safe summaries exclude private answers, stakeholder identities, and political or religious belief data.

Progress, project scopes, and reflections are private to the learner by default. Teacher preview excludes prompts, conversations, private reflections, and integrity scores. Tutor guidance excludes full history and reflections. Public views are empty.

Reflections are excluded from ordinary analytics, ordinary logs, and model training and can be deleted. Analytics accepts only allowlisted event names without raw text. This prototype uses memory only; truthful database retention, backup deletion, and production authorization require server infrastructure.

Diagnostic answers, explanations, hints, evidence, recommendations, selections, timings, accessibility/language choices, and history are likewise private and deletable by default.

Competency statuses, evidence, artifacts, reviews, and history are private; deletion recalculates only affected competencies.

Lesson responses, revisions, plans, reflections, speech transcripts, sessions, and candidates inherit this private default.

System classifications and fictional tool audits are also private and are never used for authorship detection.
