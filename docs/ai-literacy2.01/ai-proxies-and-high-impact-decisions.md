# Proxies and High-Impact Decisions

Postal code, school prestige, employment gaps, names, accents, device type, and other fields may act as proxies. A proxy is not automatically inappropriate: investigate task relevance and unequal effects. High-impact decisions need relevant structured criteria, data minimization, accommodations, trained human review, audits, correction and appeal. Never rank real people in this lesson or let AI make the final hiring decision.
