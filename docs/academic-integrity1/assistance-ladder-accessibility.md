# Assistance Ladder Accessibility

All levels and restriction notices support keyboard, screen readers, visible focus, TTS, reviewed STT, 200%/320px reflow, high/forced contrast, reduced motion, bilingual/RTL, approved accommodation translation, mobile, Low-Bandwidth, and offline drafts. Accessibility functions cannot be disabled and never influence level selection, escalation, evaluation, model-answer eligibility, logging, or disclosure by default.
