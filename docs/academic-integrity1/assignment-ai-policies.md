# Step 61 privacy preflight

Assignment AI permission never bypasses privacy preflight. Destination and assignment policy are resolved separately: an AI-permitted assignment can still require identifier removal or a protected human channel. Privacy findings never create an Academic Integrity case, teacher alert, honesty inference, or misconduct evidence.

# Step 63 policy equivalence

Assignment policies use approved, versioned translations and adaptations that preserve permission, prohibition, consent, final-answer, human-review, and disclosure meanings.
