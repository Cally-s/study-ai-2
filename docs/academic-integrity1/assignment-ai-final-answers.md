# Assignment AI Final Answers

> Model answers remain outside the eight-level ladder and require verified practice, attempt, explanation request, compatible mode, every policy gate, and intentional release; never available always wins.

Final-answer generation is independently explicit and defaults restricted. Release may be never available, after configured practice plus intentional student action, or require teacher review. “Never available” overrides assignment-level release paths. Model answers are labelled and cannot reproduce restricted active assessments. Hidden generation and ordinary-log storage are prohibited.
