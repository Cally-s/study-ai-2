# Policy Precedence

> Intervention category/reason and buttons derive from the winning verified policy and cannot select a weaker declaration or inferred permission.

> Ladder availability uses the already-resolved most specific current policy and mandatory restrictions; no level or escalation action can select a weaker source.

> A current assignment policy overrides broader defaults where authorized, while platform safety and mandatory school/course restrictions remain non-overridable; inheritance never turns missing values into permission.

Platform safety and applicable authorized requirements lead. Exact verified assessment/competition, assignment, project, course, teacher, school/admin, student document, student report, then unknown default follow. A current specific verified policy overrides a broader or self-reported conflict. Conflicts remain visible, preserve drafts, and create no accusation.
