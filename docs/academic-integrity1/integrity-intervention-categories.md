# Integrity Intervention Categories

Operational routing categories cover final submissions; active assessments and competitions; complete homework, essays, proofs/math, code, labs, translations, and reflections; fabricated sources/citations; concealment/authorship/falsification; policy/ladder/final-answer/review bypass; missing attempt/draft/source/disclosure/log/review; policy uncertainty/conflict; group permission; private information; selected mode; and other restricted assistance. Categories are not misconduct, severity, risk, honesty, or trait labels.
