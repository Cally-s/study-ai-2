# Stakeholder Feedback

Keep real authorized, consent-review-required, synthetic practice, automated, AI, mentor, student, unknown and withdrawn provenance distinct. Record notice/consent/privacy/withdrawal, reviewed version/task, summary, quotation permission, evidence/conflicts and student interpretation. Disposition every material item: accept, partly accept, combine, clarify, more evidence, defer, reject, out of scope, blocked, conflict, or withdrawn. Feedback never changes or trains a system automatically.
