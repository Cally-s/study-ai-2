# Human Agency: AI-Use Disclosure

An honest disclosure names the assistance accurately, the work that remained student-created, and verification where required. Example: “I used StudySpark to brainstorm three possible topics. I selected the topic, researched the sources, designed the experiment, analyzed the results, and wrote the report.” “I used AI” may be too vague. Disclosure describes use; it does not create permission or make answer replacement acceptable. Fictional practice avoids forced confession.
