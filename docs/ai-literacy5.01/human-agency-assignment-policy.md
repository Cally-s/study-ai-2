# Human Agency: Assignment Policy

Policy states are Allowed, Allowed with Student Contribution, Allowed with Disclosure, Limited Scope, Teacher Approval Required, Prohibited for This Task, Active Assessment Restricted, Unknown, Conflicting, and Outdated. A valid assignment-specific rule can be stricter than a general platform rule. Unknown, conflicting, or outdated policy requires teacher clarification; the lesson never invents permission. Active assessments block answer-bearing support while preserving authorized accessibility support.
