# Independent Retry

## Step 60 badge evidence
When a badge requires Applied Independently, it links a valid fresh Step 52 attempt and support profile. Accessibility and approved resources remain available and never reduce independence.

## Step 58 recognition use
Applied Independently requires a valid fresh Step 52 task without substantive AI hints. Accessibility and approved resources remain available; the retry evidence is linked explicitly rather than inferred from usage.

## Purpose
After substantial guided support, offer a meaningfully new, equivalent task to check transfer without substantive hints. Use the exact heading **Independent Retry** and message: “You completed the guided example. Now try an independent example without hints:” Accessibility and policy-approved resources remain available. This is attempt-level evidence, never a conversation-count metric, score, rank, or permanent label.

## Step 54 — Teacher AI Literacy Dashboard

Authorized teachers receive a strict teacher-ai-literacy-dashboard/1.0 projection of intentionally submitted, server-validated learning evidence. Nine canonical sections preserve evidence source/version, guided or independent conditions, review status, privacy scope, and limitations. Private conversations, receipts, drafts, reflections, safety content, and accessibility diagnoses are excluded. Cohort patterns use small-group suppression; student order is alphabetical. Instructional needs come from explicit recurring criteria gaps—not AI-use frequency, rankings, detectors, hidden risk scores, or missing evidence.

## Step 55 — Teacher Review Instead of Automatic Discipline

Trusted structured policy mismatches extend Step 40 with an educational reminder, server-permitted alternatives, optional separately stored student context, explicit published-rule escalation, minimal review packages, authorized human outcomes, and neutral versioned resolutions. Simple reminders and changed requests do not escalate by default. StudySpark never infers intent or misconduct, sends full conversations/private artifacts, uses behavioral signals or frequency scores, applies consequences, changes grades, restricts accounts, notifies parents, or creates discipline. Accessibility and safety remain separate.

## Teacher-authored activities
Published Step 56 lessons preserve retry, feedback, and equivalent non-AI routes. Retry behavior and elapsed time remain non-evaluative.

## Step 57 integration
An Independent Retry uses a meaningfully different scenario for the same skill with no substantive AI hints. Accessibility and approved resources remain available. Guided work cannot be labelled independent, and evidence links only to the fresh attempt.
# Step 63 accessibility equivalence

Independent Retry preserves the private accessibility/language profile and accepts equivalent response formats. TTS, captions, transcripts, keyboard alternatives, bilingual explanations, STT after review, and reading support do not invalidate independence. Transcription errors are correctable before evaluation.
