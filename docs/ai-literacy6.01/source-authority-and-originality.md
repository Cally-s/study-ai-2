# Source Authority and Originality

Authority is claim dependent. Original/primary, authoritative-for-claim, reliable secondary, contextual, unclear, and not-authoritative states remain separate from originality. Original, independent secondary, derived, copied/republished, aggregator, AI derivative, and unknown originality are recorded independently.

Primary does not mean conclusive; secondary synthesis may better answer broad questions. Multiple pages repeating one study, dataset, or press release are dependent evidence, not independent confirmation.

Step 15 displays these properties beside claim-specific evidence and limitations before any student weight decision.
