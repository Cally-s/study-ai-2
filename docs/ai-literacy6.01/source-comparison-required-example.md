# Required Source Comparison Example

| Source | Supports the claim? | Strength | Concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Government data table | Yes | Primary statistical source | Data are two years old |
| University research paper | Partly | Methods are explained | Small sample |
| Personal blog | Makes the same claim | Easy to understand | No evidence cited |

Question: “Which source should carry the most weight, and why?” The data table may lead for its measured statistic, the paper may interpret with sample limits, and the blog may provide context but not statistical evidence. Current data may still be needed.
