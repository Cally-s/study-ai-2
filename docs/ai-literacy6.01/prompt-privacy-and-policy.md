# Prompt Privacy and Policy

Step 9 privacy preflight applies before preview testing or sending. Secrets and unnecessary identifiers must be removed, and the reviewed redacted prompt replaces the original at the provider boundary.

Step 11 resolves the requested help mode as AI Allowed, Allowed with Disclosure, Allowed for Limited Tasks, Not Allowed, or Policy Unknown. Unknown policy permits general learning and clarification, never submission-ready generation. Active assessments block answer-bearing prompts. Final preview shows both states.
