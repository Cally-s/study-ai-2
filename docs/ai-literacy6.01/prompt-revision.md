# Prompt Revision

A prompt problem may be an unclear goal, missing context, wrong help mode, absent constraint, or unhelpful format. A response problem may be an unsupported fact, invented citation, calculation error, policy violation, or ignored instruction. A task problem may mean AI is inappropriate or human judgement is required.

Students should not endlessly rewrite a prompt to compensate for missing evidence or an unsuitable task. Every revision creates a new version; reviewed or sent versions remain immutable.

Step 13 requires an observed problem, proposed change, expected effect, unchanged elements, and evaluation method before the next version. Comparisons record observed effects and tradeoffs without causal certainty.
