# Prompt Iteration Evidence

Meaningful diagnosis, hypothesis, revision, comparison, variability recognition, verification, explanation, and stop/human-support decisions may become private candidates for Application Skills, Human Accountability, Human Agency, Safe and Responsible Use, and introductory Iteration and Feedback Loops.

Opening history, regeneration without review, version/retry count, prompt length, audio, or time create no evidence. Students review, accept, decline, keep private, or delete eligible candidates; Step 3 remains authoritative.
