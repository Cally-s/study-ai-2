# Prompt Iteration Privacy

Prompt/response versions, reviews, hypotheses, comparisons, verification notes, summaries, reflections, and evidence are private by default. Raw content is excluded from ordinary analytics, logs, URLs, profiles, and model training by default.

Step 9 applies to every new version and human-support draft. Eligible records support deletion; production must implement retention and audited access. Teachers, tutors, guardians, administrators, and other students receive no automatic access.
