# Prompt With Purpose

## Purpose and learning goal

This Apply-level lesson teaches students to build, test, evaluate, and revise a learning prompt while preserving privacy, policy, verification, ownership, and the opportunity to attempt the work. Application Skills is primary; Human Accountability, Human Agency, and Safe and Responsible Use are secondary.

## Nine core elements and phases

The nine core elements are Goal, Context, Current Understanding, Constraints, Requested Help, Desired Format, Source Requirements, Verification Requirements, and Privacy Check. Current Attempt, Confusion or Gap, Assignment Policy, Student Attempt Rule, Academic Terms, and Success Criteria are conditional. Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect remain in that order.

## Known limitations

The current implementation is an in-memory deterministic prototype. It does not provide production persistence, model delivery, authoritative policy/source integrations, or competency decisions.

Step 13 extends this foundation with revision hypotheses, controlled comparisons, model-variability instruction, verification, and stop decisions while keeping Step 12 available independently.

Step 14 can open from a resulting AI answer. Prompt clarity and source requests remain separate from claim-level evidence and verification.

## Step 18 — AI Brainstorming

Brainstorming Mode reuses purpose/boundary controls for idea type, count, constraints, prohibited outputs, privacy, and policy.

## Step 19 — AI Feedback

Feedback requests reuse purpose controls for focus, delivery mode, issue limit, no-rewrite behavior, rubric/source access, language, and privacy.
