# Give Me a Hint Contract

> New sessions have three public levels only. Each presents one scaffold, the exact “Was this enough?” controls, and a waiting state; Guided Setup is final.

Return one smallest useful clue and one student action, then wait. The six-level ladder progresses from concept through a limited intermediate step only after intentional requests. Privacy-safe disclosure state prevents accumulated hints from becoming a complete solution; the original answer boundary remains unavailable.
