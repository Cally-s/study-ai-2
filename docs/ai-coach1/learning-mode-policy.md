# Learning Mode Policy

> Step 38 replaces coarse assignment AI permission with sixteen four-state entries, conservative inheritance, immutable publication, student transparency, and server validation.

> Step 37 asks only missing context/policy facts, reuses the most specific current authorized record, labels self-reports accurately, resolves verified conflicts, and fails closed for unresolved task-specific work.

> Quiz policy controls counts, types, sources, notes, feedback, hints, reattempts, and summary while blocking AI-prohibited assessments and preserving historical policy/configuration versions.

> Challenge mode is blocked when AI is prohibited, respects source and answer-boundary restrictions, retains historical policy versions, and never treats challenge-only support as an assessment-policy bypass.

> Reasoning feedback remains unavailable when AI is prohibited or active-assessment rules block it; feedback-only policies never enable completion.

> Similar examples are blocked for prohibited AI use and active assessments; teacher rules cannot reconstruct questions or force original completion.

> Guiding Questions cannot enable AI during prohibited work or active assessments, and historical turns preserve their authoritative policy version.

> Policies may lower maximum hints to 0–3 but cannot create Hint 4, skip a level, or force answer disclosure.

> Runtime resolution applies the strictest policy, assessment, session, and mode restriction before tools or model requests are selected.

The server resolves the most specific active organization, course, assignment, assessment, or teacher-required policy. A stricter policy overrides preference; a student may choose a stricter compatible mode. Required modes visibly lock the selector, restricted modes remain visible with reasons, and AI-prohibited assessments show “AI Coach unavailable for this assessment.” Unknown policy permits general concept learning where appropriate, warns before assignment-specific help, and offers teacher clarification. Mode choice is not proof of cheating or ability.
