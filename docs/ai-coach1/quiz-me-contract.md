# Quiz Me Contract

> Require explicit setup and Start Quiz, show one question at a time, keep keys server-only until feedback is allowed, request reasoning where meaningful, and adapt gradually from current-session evidence without labels or timing penalties.

Present one question, announce the waiting state, and reveal feedback only after a response, “I Am Not Sure,” skip plus feedback request, or explicit answer request when policy permits. Students may hint, skip, stop, or review mistakes. Active assessments, protected banks, rankings, grades, labels, and monitoring remain prohibited.
