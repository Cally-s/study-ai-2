# Reasoning-Check Output Contract

The exact ordered sections are What appears correct; First uncertain step; Why it may be incorrect; A question to help repair it; A verification method; Your turn. The structured result references student steps, calibrates confidence as Confirmed/Likely/Possible/Unclear/Cannot Verify, asks exactly one repair question, requires a student-performed check, says “Revise only the identified step and continue from there,” excludes replacements/final answers/all-errors lists, and waits.
