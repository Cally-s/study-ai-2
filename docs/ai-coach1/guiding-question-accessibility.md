# Guiding-Question Accessibility

The text status announces: “I’ll ask one question at a time and use your answer to decide what to ask next.” Waiting, corrections, questions, hint/prerequisite states, and final-explanation requests are readable in order. Controls support keyboard, screen readers, 200% zoom/mobile reflow, high contrast, reduced motion, bilingual semantic equivalence, RTL, Low-Bandwidth/text-only, TTS, and student-reviewed Speech-to-Text.
