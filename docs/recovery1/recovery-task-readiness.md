# Recovery Task Readiness

Test topics reuse readiness categories for recall, quick review, worked example, prerequisite repair, guided practice, mistake correction, missing material and unconfirmed scope.

Step 11 asks five task-specific questions about instructions, the required concept, the first step, a relevant example, and required notes or materials. Responses are Yes, Partly, No, I am not sure, Skip, or Prefer not to answer. Answers are private, independently saved, pausable, and do not complete or reclassify official school work.

One-question-at-a-time presentation is the default in overwhelmed mode; Show All Questions remains available. The result distinguishes task readiness from motivation, intelligence, disability, or permanent ability.

Step 12 consumes only a supported or student-approved prerequisite result. Other primary blockers redirect before instructional repair, and completing repair recalculates rather than overwrites readiness.
## Prerequisite-help readiness

Readiness is current-task-specific: ready, ready with reminder/live support, another final approach, human/teacher/material/instruction/practice help, different prerequisite, inconclusive or pause. Never label permanent mastery.
# Step 26 blocked integration

A blocked academic action leaves READY_NOW and active Minimum. Only its reviewed resolution action may be actionable; the underlying task remains Blocked/Waiting or Ask First until evidence and reactivation review.
