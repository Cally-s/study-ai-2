# Recovery Verification Policy

Policy `recovery-verification-default` version 1 controls the 90-minute substantial-work threshold, field authority, contextual prioritization impact, freshness, required facts, and provisional transition. Clients cannot lower the threshold or declare readiness.

Deadlines accept teacher, school-system, official course page, application portal, or official calendar authority. Late policy accepts teacher or assignment instructions. Grade weight accepts syllabus, gradebook, teacher, or official assignment page. Submission receipt uses the submission system or teacher. Accepted/No Longer Required require applicable official authority. Students remain authoritative for their progress, understanding, materials, and estimates.

Past-due substantial work requires current still-required and late-policy facts. Tests do not require a late policy; applications require portal deadline/status; meetings require confirmed time. Provisional prioritization is allowed only with visible unresolved facts, unscheduled substantial uncertainty, protected buffer, and recalculation.
# Prioritization gate

The 90-minute substantial-work safeguard also limits overrides: substantial provisional work cannot become confirmed Do Now work. Any verification change makes a dependent priority analysis Outdated.
