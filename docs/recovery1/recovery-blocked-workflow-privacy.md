# Blocked Workflow Privacy

Reasons, clarifications, notes, accommodation functions, technical details/screenshots, group dependencies, emotional support, waiting entities, actions, reviews, and history are private. Teachers/tutors/parents/guardians/public profiles/notifications/ordinary analytics receive none automatically.

Parent summaries exclude raw blockers, accommodation, technical/group/emotional/safety details. Credentials/tokens are never collected. Analytics contain allowlisted event/time only; AI receives minimized structured facts and cannot contact, book, approve, diagnose, share, or change official status.
