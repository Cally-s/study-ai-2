# Verified Tutor Recovery Privacy

A verified tutor may receive only a student-approved Topic Packet: subject, course/grade, narrow topic, learning goal, selected prerequisite or excerpt, attempts, functional language/access preferences, integrity boundaries, and session objectives.

Grades, full plans, other courses, family/medical/diagnosis/emotional/safety data, parent/teacher history, raw diagnostics/conversations, integrity review history, notifications, and metrics are excluded. The verified session relationship is required and access normally expires after session review.
