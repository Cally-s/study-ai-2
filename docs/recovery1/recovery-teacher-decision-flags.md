# Recovery Teacher Decision Flags

Flags cover teacher confirmation, whether a task remains required, unknown late acceptance/current or revised date/submission receipt/revision requirement, unclear instructions, missing materials, pending response, unknown estimate, uncertain capacity, deadline bottleneck, capacity excess, prerequisite blocker, and recommended human support.

The review says “Teacher decisions are needed” when these facts materially affect feasibility and gives a count without a risk score. Students can review or add questions to the existing Recovery Teacher Question Queue. All questions and messages remain private drafts until the student chooses a recipient, reviews exact fields, and explicitly sends. Step 7 never predicts an answer, extension, acceptance, or response time.

Step 8 converts material flags into the smallest useful verification action. It never assumes an answer; official-policy questions cannot be delegated to tutors as authoritative requests.
