# Recovery Prerequisite Concept Model

A prerequisite record identifies the current task, candidate concept, relationship, source type, verification status, content version, and lifecycle status. Verified content-graph suggestions remain distinct from student-confirmed suggestions and unknown claims.

Only the smallest essential concept needed to begin the present task should be selected. Concepts may be confirmed, rejected, or marked outdated without changing an official course record.

Repair scope is separately classified as narrow confirmed/provisional, too broad, multiple, conflicting or unknown. “Factoring” must resolve to a specific method; common-factor extraction and monic-trinomial factoring are distinct.
