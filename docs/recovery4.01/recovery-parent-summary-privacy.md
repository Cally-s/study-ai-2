# Parent Summary Privacy

Minimal is the default. Ordinary selectable fields may include a preferred name, general academic situation, three priorities, selected deadlines, selected progress, and selected support requests. Standard can add selected course/task names, a condensed plan, and high-level communication status. Detailed requires field-by-field choice and renewed review.

Private AI conversations, transcripts, prompts, diagnostics, disability/accommodation/medical information, emotional or safety disclosures, crisis usage, private teacher/tutor notes, other-student data, hidden school records, unapproved messages, and motivation/character judgements are hard excluded—not ordinary choices.

Drafts, exclusions, recipient details, share attempts, custom text, and view state are private student data. Teachers, tutors, parents, guardians, public profiles, notifications, and ordinary analytics receive none before an approved share. A recipient receives only the approved immutable snapshot. No live monitoring is created.
