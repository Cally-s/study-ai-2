# Plan Recalculation Privacy

Triggers, requirement/acceptance, estimates, missed reasons, lower energy, responsibilities, forgot, notes, capacity, buffers, deferred/urgent items, diffs, revisions, and disagreement are private student data. Teachers/tutors/parents/guardians/public profiles/notifications/ordinary analytics receive none automatically.

Parent summaries never receive private reasons or raw revisions; only separately approved snapshot facts. AI receives minimized structured facts and cannot decide authority, infer diagnoses/motivation, activate plans, send messages, or share data. Analytics contain allowlisted event/time only.
