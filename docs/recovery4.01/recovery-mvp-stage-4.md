# Recovery MVP Stage 4 — Human Support

Stage 4 adds reviewed teacher-message drafts, verified-tutor recommendations, student-approved Parent Summary snapshots, course-scoped teacher clarification and authority-based escalation. Recommendation, request, booking, attendance and service-hour approval remain separate. Nothing sends, books, shares or activates automatically.

Release requires verified relationships, minimum sharing preview, expiry, revocation, access history and privacy-safe serializers. The prototype has domain contracts for these flows but lacks a trusted recipient/authorization/delivery backend, so Stage 4 is not production-released.
