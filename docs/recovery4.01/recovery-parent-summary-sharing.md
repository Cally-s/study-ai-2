# Parent Summary Sharing

Copy records Copied by Student. Download records Downloaded by Student. Print records Print View Opened. None mean shared, delivered, read, understood, or accepted.

Email and in-platform delivery require final recipient confirmation and a trusted provider. Secure links require opaque tokens, HTTPS, non-indexing, expiry (seven days by default), revocation, approved-snapshot-only access, and no route into the student account. Links/in-platform access may be revocable; delivered email, copied text, downloads, and printouts are not reliably revocable.

The static prototype deliberately refuses connected sharing and secure-link creation. It never queues sharing offline or updates an old snapshot.
