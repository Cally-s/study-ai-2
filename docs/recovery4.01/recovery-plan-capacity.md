# Recovery Plan Capacity

For each day, the plan stores confirmed focused capacity, Minimum/Stretch minimum and maximum minutes, required breaks, setup/transition, unused focused capacity and protected buffer. The material upper estimate must fit:

`Minimum ≤ focused capacity` and `Minimum + Stretch ≤ focused capacity`.

No-time days schedule no invented window. Uncertain capacity creates at most a provisional action and empty Stretch. Tight plans keep Stretch empty/limited. Infeasible urgent work remains visible outside Stretch with clarification/negotiation actions. Repair time is counted once.

Step 14 repeats these checks per exact local date and reserves breaks/setup/catch-up inside focused capacity while leaving protected buffer unchanged.
