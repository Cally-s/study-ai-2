# Prerequisite Help Diagnostic

Reuse Step 11 for three to five accessible, ungraded, similar-practice questions with I Do Not Know and Skip. Server answer keys remain hidden. Multiple aligned responses plus a supported task relationship and student review are required; one answer, response speed, hints, language or accessibility settings prove neither gap nor mastery. Results may reclassify instruction, practice, material or teacher-policy barriers. Active assessments block answer-bearing help.
