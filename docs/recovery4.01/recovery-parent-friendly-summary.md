# Parent-Friendly Recovery Summary

## Purpose

Step 23 creates a concise, private draft that a student may use to explain selected recovery priorities and request practical support from one parent, guardian, or authorized adult. It is not a live parent dashboard.

## Student control

The student chooses the purpose, type, sections, facts, tasks, dates, progress, support, wording, privacy level, recipient, and sharing method. Every included section and the complete summary require review and approval. Save, Preview, Copy, Download, Print, and link creation never mean recipient delivery.

## Summary sections

The seven defaults are Current Academic Situation, Immediate Priorities, Seven-Day Plan, Important Deadlines, Support Requested, Progress Already Made, and Helpful Family Support. Support Boundaries, teacher status, meeting status, current-unit reconnection, and a custom section are optional. Empty and general summaries are supported.

## Snapshot principle

A shared summary is an immutable, dated snapshot. New work, deadlines, progress, messages, or safety information never appear automatically. An update requires a new version, preview, approval, recipient review, and sharing action.

## Review and approval

Source provenance, freshness, privacy exclusions, included information, recipient status, and strengths-based wording are validated before approval. A material source change invalidates approval while preserving student edits.

## Sharing methods

Copy, plain-text/PDF download, print, secure link, in-platform, and connected email are separate states. This static build supports local Copy/Download/Print state only. Trusted connected sharing and secure links are unavailable.

## Known limitations

There is no authenticated durable database, guardian directory, relationship/custody service, PDF renderer, email/in-platform provider, secure-link service, receipt tracking, or cross-device encrypted synchronization. Manual accessibility testing is not complete.
# Step 24 daily-check-in integration

Raw daily outcomes, barriers, waiting details, optional notes, energy/responsibility/forgot responses, and check-in history never enter a Parent-Friendly Summary. Only separately validated student-selected progress may be proposed in a new approved snapshot.
# Step 25 recalculation integration

Raw triggers, missed reasons, capacity, disagreements, diffs, deferred work, and provisional revisions never enter a parent summary. Only newly selected, validated plan/progress facts may appear in a separately approved immutable snapshot.
# Step 26 blocked integration

Raw blocker reasons, accommodation/technical/group/emotional/safety details, waiting entities, and notes never enter a parent summary. A Parent/Guardian Summary is created only by separate student selection/review/approval.

## Step 27 — Right-Time Human Support

This flow may create one explained primary recommendation and up to two alternatives when authority, context, live interaction, formal support, safety, or supervised work is needed. Students may ask at any time. Only valid, accessible, language-appropriate, materially distinct attempts count toward the two-attempt recommendation and three-attempt AI stop. All sharing is previewed and student controlled; no message, booking, parent notification, official decision, attendance, service hour, Help Credit, or plan change occurs automatically.

## Step 28 — Private Recovery Progress Recognition

Only specific, meaningful, source-versioned actions with sufficient evidence may receive brief recognition explaining why the action matters. Partial, prepared, uploaded, booked, proposed, or provisional states are never overstated as completed, sent, submitted, attended, accepted, approved, or active. Recognition is private, cumulative, non-resetting, dismissible, preference controlled, bundled at natural stopping points, and suppressed during focused work, assessments, meetings, tutoring, sensitive forms, and immediate-safety support. It creates no streak, points, rank, badge, public sharing, grade, matching effect, service hour, Help Credit, safety decision, or automatic notification.

## Step 29 — Better Recovery Metrics

This integration contributes only authoritative, versioned, deduplicated events to clearly defined metric cards. Draft, proposed, uploaded, booked, identified, timed, partial, or provisional states are never overstated as sent, approved, submitted, attended, addressed, resolved, repaired, or completed. Historical progress remains visible beside reopened/current state. Cards may overlap but are never summed; there is no global score, unstable backlog percentage, streak, points, rank, comparison, automatic sharing, or consequential use.

## Step 30 — Supportive Notifications

Academic Recovery reminders are optional, explicitly enabled per channel/category, generic on lock screens by default, capped, deduplicated, expiring, and revalidated against current source versions. All categories respect quiet hours and protected stopping time; only student-selected confirmed session exceptions apply. Focus, assessment, safety, stale/blocked/material/authority states suppress delivery. Disable All, Snooze, Dismiss, and notification opening carry no penalty and cannot change tasks, plans, messages, bookings, submissions, attendance, metrics, recognition, grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, fraud, integrity, or safety decisions. Nothing is automatically shared with another person.

## Step 31 — Integrated Accessibility and Language Bridge

This workflow uses the shared optional AccessibilityPreferences profile plus per-screen/session overrides without losing progress. It must be fully reachable by keyboard and structured for screen readers; support 200%/320px reflow, contrast, Reduced Motion, Text-to-Speech with visible text, reviewed Speech-to-Text, reading levels that preserve expectations/formulas/statuses/English terminology, bilingual/RTL display, plain-text Low-Bandwidth fallback, versioned offline/print/audio alternatives, and generic private previews. No diagnosis or reason is required. Names, codes, URLs, numbers, dates, formulas, negation, authority and status meanings are invariant. Machine translation is labelled; official content requires review; nothing sends, books, shares, submits, activates, or overwrites automatically.

## Step 32 — Learning-First Academic Integrity

This workflow identifies context, active-assessment status and the highest-authority current AI policy before substantial support. It provides bounded explanations, questions, hints, similar practice, planning and draft review while requiring meaningful student contribution and enforcing a cumulative final-answer boundary. Active assessments receive no answer-bearing help. Unknown/conflicting policy routes to teacher clarification. Fabrication, impersonation, deception/evasion, hidden-test/autograder bypass, false completion and automatic submission are neutrally redirected to safe alternatives. Accessibility/language tools are valid contribution methods, not risk signals. Records are private/minimal; no accusation, suspension, report, discipline, grade, matching, service-hour, Help-Credit, public-profile or analytics consequence occurs automatically.
## Step 33 privacy integration
Parent/guardian access defaults to none. A verified recipient receives only the student-selected, previewed immutable snapshot for its stated duration; authorization for an account or tutoring creates no Recovery access. Revocation blocks future StudySpark viewing but cannot erase external copies.
## Step 34 dashboard integration
The private Recovery Dashboard summarizes this source through its exact five-area aggregation contract. It deep-links back here for review or action, preserves authoritative statuses and versions, shows only minimum privacy-safe information, and never automatically starts, completes, sends, books, rolls, shares, or activates a plan change.
