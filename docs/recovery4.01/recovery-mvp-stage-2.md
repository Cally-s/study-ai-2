# Recovery MVP Stage 2 — Triage and Planning

Stage 2 enables Do Now, Ask First, Do Next and Schedule Later; source-grounded explanations; seven exact days; protected capacity; one-to-three-action Minimum and collapsed optional Stretch; observable micro-actions; and one-action ten-minute start. Consider Releasing remains authority-gated.

Blocked, waiting, unknown late work, missing material and material unknown requirements route to Ask First. Large tasks are decomposed rather than demoted. Timers never auto-start or complete work, and plan activation requires student approval. Existing Steps 9–16 provide this prototype loop.
