# Recovery Plan Privacy

Minimum/Stretch membership, order, capacity, buffer, communication/repair actions, completion, blocks, edits, private reasons, optional preferences and history are private student data. Public, teacher, tutor, parent and guardian serializers expose nothing automatically.

Later sharing requires exact recipient/section preview, editing and explicit confirmation. Aggregate analytics contain only allow-listed event names/times—not titles, courses, order, durations, capacity, buffer, notes or Stretch noncompletion.

Plans cannot affect grades, tutor matching, service hours, Help Credits, safety, fraud, integrity, advertising, public badges or streaks. AI, if later used for wording, receives only validated bounded actions/reasons and presentation preferences and cannot decide membership.
