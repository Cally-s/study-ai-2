# Recovery Plan Success Standard

Minimum and Stretch use separate progress. Minimum completion produces `MINIMUM_COMPLETED`/`STABILIZED`; Stretch completion is additional. The primary success message is “Minimum Plan complete. Today’s situation is more stable.” Finish for Today remains primary.

Partial Minimum work is recognized as progress. Blocked/skipped actions trigger supportive replanning while preserving completed work. No-work days may record protected success. No model contains Failed, noncompliant, poor-effort, productivity, responsibility or motivation states.

Stretch noncompletion never enters the Minimum denominator or affects grades, standing, sharing or weekly stabilization.

Weekly success recognizes stabilized, protected no-work and appropriately replanned days. It is never defined as completing every Minimum and Stretch block.
