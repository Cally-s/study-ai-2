# Parent and Guardian Recovery Privacy

Default parent/guardian access is none. Account or tutoring authorization never grants plan, conversation, check-in, or note access. Verified custody/contact restrictions and recipient relationships apply.

Step 23 supplies a student-selected, previewed, immutable Parent-Friendly Summary snapshot with selected priorities, deadlines, plans, support requests, progress, and practical help. Sensitive and unrelated data is excluded. StudySpark revocation stops future access while truthfully warning that external copies may remain.
