# Recovery Mode Privacy

Selections, answers, available time, commitments, Other text and communication-draft inputs are private by default. `OVERWHELMED`, `NEED_TEACHER_HELP`, `NEED_PARENT_FRIENDLY_SUMMARY` and `OTHER` receive sensitive classification.

Teachers, tutors, parents and guardians receive no automatic visibility or notification. Public serialization returns `{}`. Recovery data does not affect grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, account standing, safety/fraud/integrity scoring or advertising.

Ordinary analytics may record the screen, a bounded selected-count bucket, Continue/Pause, technical errors and module lifecycle. It excludes exact modes, Other text, test topics, courses, commitments and communication content. Mode-level research requires a separately reviewed aggregate pipeline with minimum-group thresholds.

Local drafts are user/session namespaced. Production requires retention/deletion/export rules, encryption where appropriate, account-switch cleanup, organization isolation and server-side object authorization.
