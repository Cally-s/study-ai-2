# Parent Support Requests

Students may select quiet study time, calendar or teacher-message checks, materials or printing help, transportation, school contact, device/internet help, fewer interruptions, one brief check-in, encouragement, stopping-time protection, test-material preparation, avoiding new tasks, no support, or custom wording.

Requests should be one-time or date-specific where possible and must not infer availability. Optional boundaries include asking before adding tasks, not reading private StudySpark conversations, checking in once, focusing on Minimum, avoiding comparisons, protecting sleep/meals, finishing when Minimum is complete, and keeping message content private. Punitive or continuous monitoring is prohibited.
