# Accessibility and Language Bridge

> Step 68 release mapping: keyboard/focus/semantic/screen-reader/200%/narrow/contrast/Reduced Motion baselines are required from Stage 1. Stage 6 completes advanced bilingual, speech, captions, transcript, print, and offline coverage; it is not a deferral of basic access.

> Step 67 integration: accessibility capabilities are restored after academic deny combination unless a separate technical failure exists. Transformations preserve task meaning, never reveal hidden answers, never become a policy conflict, and do not reduce independent evidence or create a receipt alone.

> Step 66 integration: Stage 1 projects only the current presentation instructions needed by the response. Accessibility remains independent of academic permission, cannot reduce attempt provenance, and is supported through every state, error, source panel, transparency section, and receipt offer.

Step 63 provides one reusable accessibility and language contract for every Understand, Apply, and Create activity. Required capabilities include keyboard, screen reader, TTS, STT, captions, transcripts, contrast, forced colours, Reduced Motion, Animation Free, reading support, bilingual terminology, dyslexia-friendly presentation, print, audio summaries, Low-Bandwidth/offline content, and accessible math/code/tables/diagrams/media/responses. Students control private preferences and temporary overrides without changing progress or the academic task. All formats use the same competency and evidence criteria. Support creates no penalty, disclosure requirement, diagnosis, age inference, ability score, or badge reduction.
# Step 64 offline delivery

Step 63 profiles project only required settings into signed offline packages. Text-only content, bilingual canonical terms, transcripts, descriptions, print, and permitted audio remain available without media or cloud AI. Offline state keeps progress through display changes and never changes competency. Missing essential accessibility assets block package publication.
## Step 70 success-measure equivalence

Keyboard, screen-reader, TTS, reviewed STT, captions, print, bilingual, RTL, Low-Bandwidth, and offline presentation preserve eligibility and independent status. Approved-language explanations are judged on reasoning, not grammar, accent, length, or English fluency. Presentation preferences do not infer age, diagnosis, ability, income, or risk.
