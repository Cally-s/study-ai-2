# Academic-Term Preservation

Every required term has a stable canonical identifier and version. Activities declare required/optional terms, recognition expectations, permitted translated use, and terms that stay visible canonically. Coverage validation applies to translations, plain language, captions, transcripts, print, audio, and reading support. Missing, hidden, or meaning-altered terms block publication and fall back to validated canonical content with a glossary. Bilingual display always keeps the canonical term beside or visibly associated with its approved explanation.

English Term:
Unsupported claim

中文解释：
缺乏足够证据支持的陈述

Plain-language meaning:
A statement that does not yet have reliable evidence.
