# Accessibility Warning Standard

Step 24 records rule/scanner/policy/source versions, method, outcome, confidence, ownership, evidence and remediation. Heuristics use “Possible issue — human review required”; NOT_TESTED, UNABLE_TO_TEST and ERROR never count as pass.

Thirty-nine controlled issue codes use Critical, High, Medium, Low, or Informational severity and OPEN through REOPENED statuses. Deterministic checks identify missing metadata; heuristic checks use “possible” language and require human judgment. False positives and exceptions require actor, reason, source version, date, and authorized review. Critical blockers cannot be silently ignored.

> Automated checks can identify possible barriers, but they do not prove that a resource is fully accessible. Complete the required human review before publishing.

No percentage or “fully compliant” score is produced.
