# Diagnostic Recommendations

Deterministic, versioned evidence rules produce up to one primary and two additional positive suggestions: Ready to Explore AI Foundations, Practise Verification, Strengthen Responsible Use, Practise Source Selection, Document AI Assistance, or Scope a Guided AI Project.

More than one suggestion may appear. No score, percentage, rank, risk, assigned level, or permanent label is calculated. Suggestions never lock content. Students can override them without a reason and select Understand, Apply, or Create.
