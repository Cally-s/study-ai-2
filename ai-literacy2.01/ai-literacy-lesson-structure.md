# AI Literacy Lesson Structure

Every published lesson uses Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect exactly once in that order. Navigation can move backward; meaningful phases require student work. Lesson completion is separate from competency demonstration and produces only a private evidence candidate.

The static prototype has no durable server/database, production authoring workflow, or real offline service worker.

Step 5 instantiates the structure with a full Understand lesson and classification activity; the shared lesson engine remains the canonical player contract.
