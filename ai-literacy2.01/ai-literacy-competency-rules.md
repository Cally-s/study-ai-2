# Competency Rules

Exploring needs meaningful engagement. Practising needs student participation with guidance. Demonstrated needs accepted, verified student-led evidence and explanation. Applied Independently needs two student-led applications in distinct contexts or a substantial project with design/testing/explanation/reflection. Ready to Revisit overlays prior achievement.

Rules are deterministic and versioned. Diagnostic evidence is capped at Practising. Policy changes create review, not silent historical rewriting.
