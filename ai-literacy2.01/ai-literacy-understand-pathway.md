# Understand Pathway

Students learn what AI is, how outputs are produced, where uncertainty and bias appear, and when AI is inappropriate. The four competencies are Human agency, Embodied ethics, AI foundations, and Problem scoping.

Starter: **What AI Can and Cannot Do**. Students compare fictional, low-risk scenarios, decide whether AI is appropriate, identify the human judgement still required, and explain uncertainty in their own words.

The optional diagnostic may suggest foundations practice, but it never assigns the student to Understand or locks Apply/Create.

Meaningful Understand evidence may support Human agency, Embodied ethics, AI foundations, and Problem scoping status; page visits do not.

Understand lessons use the same Learn–Observe–Practise–Explain–Apply–Reflect structure.

“What AI Is and Is Not” is a complete AI Foundations lesson with Human Agency and conditional Application Skills evidence.
# Human Agency lesson

After “What AI Is and Is Not,” students can open “Who Is Doing the Thinking?” Its primary competency is Human Agency and its evidence remains candidate-only until the Competency Progress Engine validates it.
# Unsupported AI Output

Step 7 follows Human Agency with a six-phase investigation lesson. It generates student-reviewed evidence candidates rather than a truth score or automatic competency status.
# Training Data and Bias

Step 8 combines Embodied Ethics and AI Foundations through synthetic representation audits, contextual fairness questions, and evidence-based redesign without a fairness score.
# Privacy before prompting

Step 9 teaches minimum-necessary disclosure, third-party privacy, redaction, configuration transparency, and secure-channel selection without a privacy score.
# Original-work spectrum

Step 10 extends Human Agency with separate ownership/policy axes, submission readiness, workflow redesign, and fifteen controlled assignment contexts.
# Assignment policy awareness

Step 11 resolves five policy states from current applicable sources and keeps ownership, policy, disclosure, and formal human decisions separate.
