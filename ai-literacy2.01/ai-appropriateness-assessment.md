# AI Appropriateness Assessment

> Step 28 preserves AI, hybrid, non-AI, more-evidence, no-build, and no-deployment conclusions and applies no penalty for choosing a simpler or human-led approach.

Compare human service, static information, search, filters, rules, decision trees, existing tools, community action, hybrid, and AI approaches. Review data authority, error consequences, correction, human oversight, privacy, fairness, accessibility, maintenance, misuse, and policy. The student finalizes the decision; no-AI is valid.

## Architecture outcome

The selected AI, hybrid, rule-based, existing-tool, human-led, non-AI, or no-build decision determines architecture type without preselecting a model.

## Data feasibility

Unknown, prohibited, withdrawn, expired, or unnecessarily sensitive data may change the AI-appropriateness decision toward synthetic, non-AI, no-data, more-evidence, or stop.

## Fairness outcome

Material unequal access, quality, burden, or harm may narrow scope, strengthen human/non-AI fallback, change the solution to non-AI, require more evidence, or stop deployment; it cannot legitimize prohibited high-stakes use.
