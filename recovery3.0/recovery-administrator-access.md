# Recovery Administrator Access

Administrators receive privacy-thresholded aggregate program metrics by default, with minimum group sizes and small-cell suppression. They receive no raw conversations, files, check-in reasons, sensitive details, or individual plan content.

Student-level access requires a defined authorized role, legitimate purpose, policy source, minimum scope, time limit, audit, and human accountability. Role or technical capability alone is insufficient; routine browsing and hidden impersonation are prohibited.
