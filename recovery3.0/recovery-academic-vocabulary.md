# Recovery Academic Vocabulary

Cards include the English term, reviewed translation, plain and academic definitions, pronunciation, part of speech, subject/school-system context, related terms, example, and common misunderstanding. Review states distinguish machine, student, educator, human-translator, needs-review, outdated, and rejected content. Students may save/remove, listen, view examples, practise, print, or download a private glossary. Private task details are never added automatically. Example: Late submission — 迟交作业.
