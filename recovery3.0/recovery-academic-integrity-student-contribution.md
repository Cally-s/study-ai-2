# Student Contribution

Loop: establish context; ask what the student understands; let them choose a direction; provide one bounded scaffold; request a decision/reason/example/draft/calculation/code/source/correction; review it; repeat only as needed; stop before submission-ready output; offer a readiness checklist. Cumulative assistance triggers boundary review after four scaffolds.

Contributions may be typed, reviewed STT, choices, ordering, highlighted evidence, formulas, uploads, oral transcript, “I do not know yet,” or “Show me a smaller first step.” Accessibility/language differences are valid participation. Clicking Continue alone is not substantive contribution.
