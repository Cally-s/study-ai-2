# Recovery Accessibility and Language Bridge

Accessibility and language support applies inside all 40 Recovery Mode surfaces, including safety, tutoring, metrics, notifications, offline/print, and audio. The optional AccessibilityPreferences profile is inherited; response/task/day/session overrides do not change account defaults unless Save as My Default is selected. Applying/resetting settings preserves progress. No diagnosis, medical condition, immigration status, proficiency assumption, or reason is required. Production translation, audio, PDF, offline synchronization, and manual AT evidence remain limited.

## Step 32 — Learning-First Academic Integrity

This workflow identifies context, active-assessment status and the highest-authority current AI policy before substantial support. It provides bounded explanations, questions, hints, similar practice, planning and draft review while requiring meaningful student contribution and enforcing a cumulative final-answer boundary. Active assessments receive no answer-bearing help. Unknown/conflicting policy routes to teacher clarification. Fabrication, impersonation, deception/evasion, hidden-test/autograder bypass, false completion and automatic submission are neutrally redirected to safe alternatives. Accessibility/language tools are valid contribution methods, not risk signals. Records are private/minimal; no accusation, suspension, report, discipline, grade, matching, service-hour, Help-Credit, public-profile or analytics consequence occurs automatically.
## Step 33 privacy integration
Accessibility and language profiles remain private; only selected functional preferences may be shared. Privacy controls preserve keyboard, screen-reader, reflow, contrast, Reduced Motion, Plain Language, bilingual/RTL, TTS/STT review, Low-Bandwidth, and offline access without diagnosis disclosure.
## Step 34 dashboard integration
The private Recovery Dashboard summarizes this source through its exact five-area aggregation contract. It deep-links back here for review or action, preserves authoritative statuses and versions, shows only minimum privacy-safe information, and never automatically starts, completes, sends, books, rolls, shares, or activates a plan change.
## Step 35 teacher-support integration
Teacher-Supported Recovery uses a verified, course-scoped, expiring request packet and structured response. Only student-selected minimum information is visible; teacher authority, privacy, integrity, accessibility, offline drafts, source versions, student review, and approval-before-plan-activation remain enforced.
## Step 36 Recovery Output integration
The Recovery Output aggregates this source into a private, reviewable, versioned student view. Confirmed/estimated/unknown/conflicting/stale status, five-category triage, feasibility, Minimum-before-Stretch, protected buffer, help, review and no-automatic-action rules remain authoritative.
## Step 37 canonical data-model integration
This feature reads canonical tenant/owner-scoped Recovery Case, versioned task/fact/blocker/prerequisite/action/capacity/plan/communication/event records. Its dashboard or snapshot remains derived, privacy safe and non-authoritative; source versions, grants, optimistic concurrency, idempotency, retention and audit rules apply.
## Step 38 layered state integration
Primary lifecycle, wait records, attention flags, system health, versioned priority, action execution/scheduling/review/validity remain separate. Consequential transitions require valid triggers, guards, evidence, actor, version, student control and audit; partial progress and history are preserved.

## Step 39 transparent prioritization integration

This workflow consumes the versioned five-band priority decision only after exclusions and hard gates. It uses plain-language reasons and source uncertainty, never exposes the internal 41-point score, never treats estimated effort as importance, preserves protected capacity and authority boundaries, and recalculates after material source, blocker, policy or student-priority changes.

## Step 40 controlled MVP delivery

This workflow is exposed only when its Recovery stage, feature flag, tenant/account scope, earlier-stage dependencies and evidence gates permit it. Disabled later-stage controls are omitted and a safe earlier-stage alternative remains. Upgrades preserve source/version/history records; rollback blocks new later-stage writes without deleting student data. Privacy, accessibility, Academic Integrity, safety and human authority apply from Stage 1 rather than arriving as later features.
