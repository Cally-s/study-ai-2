# Attendance and Deception Safeguards

Never fabricate attendance, check-ins, timestamps, absence/medical/family explanations, group participation, meeting/chat/revision history or completion. Never impersonate, deceive a teacher, conceal unauthorized AI use, evade plagiarism/AI detection or bypass rules. Safe alternatives include an honest message, correction request, verified attendance review, accurate attribution, contribution explanation and human review. Restrictions do not automatically accuse, suspend, report or discipline.
