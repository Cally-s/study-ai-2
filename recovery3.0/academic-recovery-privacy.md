# Academic Recovery Privacy

Test scope, confusion, diagnostics, mistakes, stop time, progress and not-covered topics remain private and excluded from ordinary analytics/non-owner views.

Ten-minute selection, timer intervals, stops, outcomes, blockers and continuation choices remain private and excluded from non-owner views and ordinary analytics.

Actions, progress, draft evidence, actual time and notes are private and excluded from public/teacher/tutor/adult views and ordinary analytics. Acceptance does not authorize sharing or submission.

## Private by default

Opening, selecting or using Academic Recovery Mode is private account activity. It is excluded from public profiles, class lists, tutor cards, dashboards for other roles, service records, Help Credits, badges, streaks, social notifications and advertising.

## Teacher and tutor access

Teachers and tutors receive nothing automatically. A later help request must have student review, a defined recipient/content scope and final confirmation. Private notes, internal plans, usage history, inferred motivation and diagnoses are never shared through Step 1.

## Parent or guardian access

Minor status or consent for another feature does not grant access. Later sharing must follow the authorized-adult policy, show the student a preview and use an appropriate, defined scope.

## Public-profile exclusion

`toPublicView` returns `{}`. Recovery sessions are excluded from search, social caches and public API representations.

## Analytics restrictions

Allowed events are display, open, mode selection, exit, resume and technical error. The safe event contains only the controlled event, controlled entry source and time. Do not send identity, task titles/content, exact overdue count, private notes, contact drafts or emotional statements. Never derive risk, dependence, motivation, responsibility or overwhelm scores.

## Data retention

Production retention is not established. A server implementation must publish retention/expiry rules and owner deletion/export behavior before rollout. Step 1 local drafts remain removable and must never be retained as disciplinary or scoring evidence.

## Offline-device data

Local keys are namespaced to the current demo user and removable at account switching. A production shared-device implementation requires encryption where appropriate, reliable sign-out cleanup, expiry and account-switch tests.

Step 2 selections and answers remain private. Exact selected modes and free text are excluded from ordinary analytics and all other-role/public serializers. See `recovery-mode-privacy.md`.

Step 3 concern, due context, available time and first action remain private; see `calm-intake-privacy.md`.
## Step 4 private inventory

Work titles, courses, dates, statuses, estimates, weights, dependencies, instruction/material information, application/meeting context and teacher-question drafts remain owner-only. They are excluded from public, teacher, tutor, parent/guardian, service-credit, matching, safety, fraud, integrity and ordinary analytics surfaces. Removal changes only the private recovery list.
## Step 5 sources and candidates

Pasted text, descriptions, documents, screenshots, connector state, extracted fields/excerpts and rejected candidates are private. Owner serializers omit raw sources, storage keys, tokens and prompts; ordinary analytics/logs omit content. Deleting raw sources does not silently delete confirmed inventory items.
## Step 6 capacity privacy

Availability, commitments, sleep, concentration/break patterns and functional scheduling supports remain owner-only. Generic unavailable labels minimize medical/family/work details. Capacity never enters public/role dashboards, scoring, grades, matching, credits or ordinary analytics.

## Step 7 feasibility privacy

Recovery Load, classifications, bottlenecks, decisions, estimates, and negotiation/deferral views remain owner-only. No teacher, tutor, parent, guardian, public, grade, matching, safety, fraud, integrity, service-hour, Help Credit, or ordinary analytics surface receives them automatically.

Step 8 facts, excerpts, corrections, confidence, readiness, actions, conflicts and drafts follow the same owner-only boundary. Role dashboards and ordinary analytics receive none automatically.
# Step 9 private priorities

Categories, ranks, importance, overrides, release candidates, and history remain owner-only. They are excluded from public and role views, ordinary analytics, grades, matching, credits, service hours, safety, fraud, integrity, discipline, and advertising.

Step 10 explanations, claims, sources, feedback and private reasons inherit the same restrictions. Later sharing needs an exact editable preview and explicit recipient confirmation.

Step 11 readiness, diagnostic answers, evidence, disagreements and bridge history are owner-private. Role/public serializers remain empty; drafts require explicit student approval and authorization before sharing.

Step 12 answers, hints, corrections, decisions, self-assessment, reminder cards and repair history inherit the same owner-only boundary. Ordinary analytics receive event names only.

Step 13 plan membership, order, capacity, completion, blocks, edits, reasons and Stretch choices are likewise owner-private and excluded from role/public views and ordinary analytics.

Step 14 exact dates, focus order, breaks, reserve, no-work days, calendar preview and replanning history remain under the same private boundary.
## Missed-week data

Absence/attendance, lesson, concept, material, requirement and check-in data remain owner-private and purpose-limited. No external message, booking, calendar action, official record change or sharing occurs from plan creation/acceptance.
## Extended-gap data

Gap/course/pathway/replacement/accommodation/support/communication/multiweek data require purpose-limited owner storage and exact sharing previews. Accommodation files and gap reasons are excluded by default.
## Prerequisite-help data

Raw statements, answers, attempt/history/readiness and handoffs are sensitive owner data. No automatic audience gets access; AI and previews receive only minimum authorized fields.
## Overwhelmed and safety data

Keep ordinary overwhelm and restricted minimum safety records separate where practical. Sharing/notifications/analytics exclude distress text and safety detail; disclosed policy governs authorized access.
## Teacher-message data

Draft/recipient/source/personal/attachment/response data are private and purpose-limited. Exact previews and explicit action govern every recipient/copy; analytics exclude content/contact data.
# Step 23 parent-summary privacy

Drafts are private by default. Hard exclusions cover AI conversations/prompts, diagnostics, disability/accommodation/medical data, emotional/safety disclosures, crisis use, private staff/tutor notes, other-student data, unapproved messages, and judgements. Recipients receive only one approved immutable snapshot; there is no live monitoring.
# Step 24 daily-check-in privacy

Outcome, barrier, waiting, note, action-link, estimate, capacity, and plan-day snapshot data remain student private. No teacher, tutor, parent, guardian, staff, public profile, notification preview, or ordinary analytics receives raw check-in data automatically.
# Step 25 recalculation privacy

Triggers, requirement/acceptance, estimates, reasons, capacity, deferral, urgency, disagreements, diffs, and proposals are private. External roles and ordinary analytics receive none automatically; only approved plan state follows trusted activation.
# Step 26 blocked privacy

Reasons/clarifications, functional access needs, technical/group/emotional details, waiting entities/actions/reviews remain student private. Credentials are never collected; external roles/notifications/analytics receive none automatically.

## Step 27 — Right-Time Human Support

This flow may create one explained primary recommendation and up to two alternatives when authority, context, live interaction, formal support, safety, or supervised work is needed. Students may ask at any time. Only valid, accessible, language-appropriate, materially distinct attempts count toward the two-attempt recommendation and three-attempt AI stop. All sharing is previewed and student controlled; no message, booking, parent notification, official decision, attendance, service hour, Help Credit, or plan change occurs automatically.

## Step 28 — Private Recovery Progress Recognition

Only specific, meaningful, source-versioned actions with sufficient evidence may receive brief recognition explaining why the action matters. Partial, prepared, uploaded, booked, proposed, or provisional states are never overstated as completed, sent, submitted, attended, accepted, approved, or active. Recognition is private, cumulative, non-resetting, dismissible, preference controlled, bundled at natural stopping points, and suppressed during focused work, assessments, meetings, tutoring, sensitive forms, and immediate-safety support. It creates no streak, points, rank, badge, public sharing, grade, matching effect, service hour, Help Credit, safety decision, or automatic notification.

## Step 29 — Better Recovery Metrics

This integration contributes only authoritative, versioned, deduplicated events to clearly defined metric cards. Draft, proposed, uploaded, booked, identified, timed, partial, or provisional states are never overstated as sent, approved, submitted, attended, addressed, resolved, repaired, or completed. Historical progress remains visible beside reopened/current state. Cards may overlap but are never summed; there is no global score, unstable backlog percentage, streak, points, rank, comparison, automatic sharing, or consequential use.

## Step 30 — Supportive Notifications

Academic Recovery reminders are optional, explicitly enabled per channel/category, generic on lock screens by default, capped, deduplicated, expiring, and revalidated against current source versions. All categories respect quiet hours and protected stopping time; only student-selected confirmed session exceptions apply. Focus, assessment, safety, stale/blocked/material/authority states suppress delivery. Disable All, Snooze, Dismiss, and notification opening carry no penalty and cannot change tasks, plans, messages, bookings, submissions, attendance, metrics, recognition, grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, fraud, integrity, or safety decisions. Nothing is automatically shared with another person.
