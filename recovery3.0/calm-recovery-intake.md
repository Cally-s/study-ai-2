# Calm Recovery Intake

Step 3 follows horizon and situation selection. It asks only: “What is worrying you most right now?”, “What is due first?”, and “How much time do you realistically have today?” It shows the canonical incomplete-information and central messages, never the full backlog, overdue count, grade prediction or red warning wall.

The default presents three short sections; Overwhelmed/narrow presentation uses one question at a time without auto-advance. Every question accepts an honest unknown state. The student can switch presentation, edit, pause/resume and review needs.

Step 2 may suggest a concern, but confirmation/change/clear remains required. Later routing reuses stable dedupe keys. Test Tomorrow stays urgent; monthly backlog remains hidden; Overwhelmed receives one small action; unknown due context can later support a teacher clarification draft.

After review, explicit Continue creates one deterministic, editable, source-grounded first action—not a recovery plan. It must fit available time, protect essentials, avoid graded work/active-test answers and share nothing.

Answers are private, user/session scoped and excluded publicly/analytically. Offline answers and deterministic actions use known data only; current deadlines and cloud AI output are never fabricated. Production authorization/sync and manual browser/AT evidence remain unavailable.
