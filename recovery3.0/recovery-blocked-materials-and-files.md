# Blocked Materials and Files

Missing instructions, rubric, notes, slides, review sheet, lab data, starter code, template, group file, or feedback routes through authorized course/cached/offline/official attachments/student-permitted copies before a reviewed request or accessible-format request.

Dependent work remains blocked until required material is available. StudySpark never fabricates files/data/documents, downloads unauthorized material, requests answer keys, or exposes private storage. Available, offline, alternate, partial, missing, restricted, permission, drafted/sent/waiting/outdated/unknown states remain distinct.
