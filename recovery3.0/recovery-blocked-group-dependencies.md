# Blocked Group Dependencies

Students may need a shared file, decision, another section, response, meeting time, or group-space access. Reviewed in-platform messages, shared-space review, independent portion, teacher guidance, individual fallback, or short check-in are valid actions.

Dependent work stays off READY_NOW; independent work may continue and a review point is set. No personal phone/email disclosure, blame/threat, uncooperative label, repeated automatic message, or invented group-policy decision is allowed.
