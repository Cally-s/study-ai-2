# Backlog Intake

## Missed One Week

Ask affected courses, approximate dates, known recent lessons/assignments/assessments/projects/announcements and known deadlines. The reason for absence is never required. Useful help may begin before a complete inventory.

## Missed One Month

Begin with one to three courses, known deadlines, current-learning importance, contact status, optional human supports, realistic seven-day time and fixed commitments. Do not display every overdue item first or assume late work is accepted.

## Combined selection

Both selections remain active. Monthly scope represents the full backlog; the recent week identifies current urgent work. Shared courses and deadlines are asked once.

## Human support and workload limits

Teacher, tutor, guidance counsellor, authorized adult or another trusted adult may be suggested. No contact is automatic. When work exceeds available time, later planning must say not everything fits rather than compressing it into an unsafe schedule.
