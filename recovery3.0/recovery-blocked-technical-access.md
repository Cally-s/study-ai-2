# Blocked Technical Access

Website blockers distinguish load, sign-in, permission, broken link, service error, device/browser/assistive-technology compatibility, and weak connection. Actions may check verified status, supported device/browser steps, low-bandwidth/offline alternatives, alternate access/submission, technical support, current teacher link, or accessibility settings.

Support may collect selected device/OS/browser/error/time/service and optional previewed/redacted screenshot. It never asks for or stores passwords, authentication/recovery codes, tokens, secret answers, full history/recording, unrelated tabs/messages, or medical data.
