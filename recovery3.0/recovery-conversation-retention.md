# Recovery Conversation Retention

Students can select session-only/non-retention, selected expiry, until deletion, managed retention, restricted safety retention, or legal hold. They may delete eligible messages, conversations, summaries, embeddings, and indexes and export eligible content.

Sent teacher messages, submissions, tutor-service records, protected safety records, and required legal/security audit records remain separate. Provider retention is disclosed. “Deleted from active systems” never means immediate removal from every backup.
