# How to Use StudySpark

## Start an academic recovery plan

Choose **Help Me Catch Up** from the dashboard, Study Planner, AI Coach, course cards, mobile navigation or a supportive overdue notice. Read the privacy and due-date notices, then choose Quick Rescue for the next 24 hours or Full Recovery for a seven-day planning draft. Selecting an option does not make a plan; use its Start/Build button only when ready. Choose Not Now or Return to leave without penalty. Nothing is sent to another person unless a later step lets you review and explicitly send it.

Step 20 provides eleven versioned public guides: sign-in, accessibility, TTS, STT, Study Rooms, offline materials, assignment upload, saved AI responses, personal information, reporting and draft recovery. Each canonical guide has plain-language ordered steps, search/context mapping, printable text, matching audio/video scripts, Low-Bandwidth text and isolated practice. Screenshots/video/PDF/multilingual assets remain unavailable and are never substituted for text.
Step 21 quick actions should receive contextual public help explaining response-only scope, original preservation and explicit default saving.
