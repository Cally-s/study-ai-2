# Printable Plan Privacy

Student name is optional and excluded by default; a blank Name line is used. Filenames use course/version only. Email, phone, address, guardian/medical/disability/safety/fraud/integrity/private-chat/token/key data are excluded. Printing/download cannot influence completion, attendance, engagement, grades, matching, ratings, safety, fraud, integrity, service hours, Help Credits, restrictions or advertising. Downloaded files may remain on shared devices; authenticated downloads are unavailable.
