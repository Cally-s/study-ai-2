# Fairness Evaluation Lab

> Step 28 carries affected-user findings, unequal harms, human-review limits, and deployment recommendations into the final reflection without converting them into a score.

Purpose: compare quality, access, errors, burdens, benefits, and harms across relevant scenarios for an exact project/system version. It asks the ten exact questions from “Which users could be affected?” through “Should the system be deployed at all?” The gate blocks broad suitability claims, real-stakeholder sharing beyond authorized limited tests, public-safe deployment-ready export, deployment recommendations, consequential use, and project completion while unresolved. This static prototype lacks authoritative production gate enforcement and live evaluation.

## Iteration after mitigation

Fairness-driven changes are retested across affected and regression scenarios, checked for new disparities and recorded in a new fairness evaluation version with residual risk.
