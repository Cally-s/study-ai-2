# Claim–Evidence Support Reasons

Direct support matches the claim as written. Narrower support justifies a more limited statement. Indirect support requires another reasoning step. Context explains the topic without establishing the claim. Repeated-claim-only means the source restates the claim without evidence.

Reviews identify logical, causal, scope, population, date, and quantitative leaps. Before-and-after evidence does not by itself prove causation; firsthand experience does not establish a population statistic; an analogy alone does not establish a scientific fact.
