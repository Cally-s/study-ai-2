# Response Card Mode Contracts

Coach Me stays within the ladder. Hints expose one active level. Guiding Questions asks one question. Simpler Language preserves terminology and support strength. Similar Examples do not solve the original. Reasoning Check finds the first issue; Challenge requests a defense; Quiz preserves feedback timing. Source Verification reports evidence status. Do Not Give Me the Final Answer protects every section. Integrity Intervention stays constructive, Student-Contribution assistance preserves agency, and permitted model answers remain labelled and separate.
