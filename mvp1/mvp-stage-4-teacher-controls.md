# MVP Stage 4: Teacher Controls

Stage 4 depends on Stages 1–3 and adds authorized Assignment Policy Builder, allowed modes, attempt/disclosure/receipt/final-answer rules, immutable publication, student policy display, Teacher AI Literacy Dashboard, and human review/correction.

Stage 4 uses the same Stage 1 policy identity/versions. Teachers cannot weaken platform safety/accessibility, view private conversations by default, publish unknown fields, or delegate misconduct decisions to the model. Dashboard projections show authorized submitted records and instructional needs without hidden scores. Human-review corrections append history rather than overwriting it.

Release gate includes authorization/tenant tests, server enforcement, privacy boundaries, review history, prior regression, production build, and no Critical issue. Current result: **Blocked by earlier stages and production infrastructure**.
