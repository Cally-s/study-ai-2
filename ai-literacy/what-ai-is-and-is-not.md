# What AI Is and Is Not

This Understand lesson teaches: “Not all software is AI, and not all AI is generative AI.” Its primary competency is AI Foundations, with Human Agency and meaningful Application Skills as secondary mappings.

Across Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect, students classify function, method, inputs, output, AI involvement, limitations, and human responsibility. Completion creates candidate evidence only. The static prototype lacks durable server/database and real provider integrations.
