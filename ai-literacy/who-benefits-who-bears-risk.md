# Who Benefits, Who Bears the Risk, and Should We Release It?

## Learning goal
Use actual project evidence to reason about distributed benefit, harm, exclusion, accountability, resource use, and release.

## Six phases
Learn introduces benefit distribution, harm, exclusion, shifted labour, accountability, dependence, alternatives, and deployment. Observe compares a limited vocabulary tool with a stopped high-risk predictor. Practise uses controlled cases. Explain requires the student to defend evidence and uncertainty. Apply completes the canonical reflection. Reflect records consequences and evidence that could change the decision.

## Evidence-linked reflection
Every required answer links to scoped users, tests, failures, fairness and accessibility findings, data decisions, stakeholder feedback, oversight, and final limitations.

## Viewpoint neutrality
Review evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and accountability—not whether the conclusion supports AI. No pro-deployment conclusion is required.
