# Recovery Progress Frequency and Bundling

Show at most one immediate acknowledgement at a natural stopping point and no more than three standalone messages per day. Related events within the 20-minute policy window are bundled by task, course, day, teacher clarification, prerequisite repair, or recovery stage. Dedupe keys and source versions prevent refresh, import, reconnect, and cross-device repeats. Dismissal/cooldowns reduce repetition without deleting history. No popup interrupts focused work.
