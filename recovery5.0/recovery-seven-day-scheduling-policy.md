# Seven-Day Scheduling Policy

Hard constraints include confirmed daily capacity, buffer/sleep/commitments, deadlines, prerequisite/material/instruction/teacher gates, daily block/subject limits, breaks/setup and active-assessment restrictions. Upper material estimates validate fit.

Soft preferences include early teacher contact, nearest deadlines, prerequisite first, demanding work in confirmed concentration windows, related-subject grouping, fewer switches, a small catch-up reserve and optional Stretch last. The scheduler does not maximize task count or minutes.

Precision defaults to `ORDER_ONLY`; approximate/exact windows require confirmed availability and review. All plans retain policy/algorithm/source versions and a code-based trace without private reasoning.
