# Recovery Priority Factor Standard

Each recommendation records all 17 factor codes and the value considered. Deadline proximity uses past due, 24-hour, 72-hour, seven-day, later, no-deadline, unknown, and not-applicable bands. Unknown late-work acceptance is not proof of rejection or permission to work.

Acceptance and required status gate action. Grade impact can inform but never decide alone. Dependencies and blocking distinguish work that enables other work from work awaiting a prerequisite or external decision. Actionability separately checks instructions, materials, estimates, prerequisites, and teacher decisions.

Student importance is private and editable. Completion leverage distinguishes nearly finished, started, not started, submitted, unknown, and not applicable; Submitted is never Accepted. Remaining time is a range or Unknown, never fabricated as zero. Deadline fit comes from the current feasibility analysis and preserves sleep, essential commitments, and protected buffer.

Verification readiness and confidence remain separate. Conflicts, stale facts, missing critical information, or substantial uncertainty produce Ask First or an explicitly provisional result. Protected traits, accessibility use, motivation, responsibility, safety, fraud, and integrity indicators are never factors.

Step 10 maps each displayed material factor to a typed explanation claim, its fact IDs, source label/version, last checked time, verification label and confidence. Derived rank/capacity claims are explicitly labelled calculated records.

Prerequisite evidence is a visible task-readiness factor only after the Step 11 check. Unknown or inconclusive evidence remains labelled and cannot be converted into a hidden score.
