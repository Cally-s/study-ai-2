# Recovery Prioritization Readiness

- **Ready for Prioritization:** blocking facts are current/confirmed and material estimates usable.
- **Ready with Estimates:** no blocking official fact is missing; estimates remain visible and conditional.
- **Needs Clarification:** material required, status, deadline, policy, revision, submission, instruction, material, or time facts are unknown.
- **Blocked by Conflict:** credible current sources materially disagree.
- **Outdated:** source/work versions changed.
- **Excluded:** accepted, no-longer-required, archived, or outside current recovery.

Verified mode requires every active item to be Ready or Excluded. Provisional mode lists unresolved facts, preserves uncertainty through later exports, schedules no substantial uncertain work, consumes no buffer, assumes no teacher decision, and remains recalculable. Step 8 creates no final order or schedule.
