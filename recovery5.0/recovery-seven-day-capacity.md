# Seven-Day Capacity

Each date uses its confirmed focused minutes after required breaks/setup and preserves that day’s protected buffer. Minimum + Stretch + activated/reserved catch-up must not exceed focused capacity. Uncertain time is unplanned; zero-capacity dates remain protected no-work days.

Stretch uses only safe unused focused capacity. A tight or infeasible day keeps Stretch empty/limited and exposes urgent unfitted work separately. No school, work, appointment, transport, meal, sleep, family/personal/activity or unconfirmed time becomes a recovery block.
