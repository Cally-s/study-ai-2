# Recovery Priority Explanations

StudySpark explains why a task is recommended. It does not show a mysterious score or treat the recommendation as final. Step 39 explanations are deterministic summaries of visible factors, hard gates, uncertainty, source status and What Could Change; they never disclose the internal score, weights, thresholds, hidden reasoning or another student's information.

The optional “Why This Action” explanation uses coded precedence and never exposes other tasks on the one-action screen.

Step 15 distinguishes action priority from assignment priority and shows sources, uncertainty, estimate differences, dependencies and placement effects.

## Purpose

Step 10 explains every active Step 9 category, within-category rank, and immediate action from current validated decisions. It never creates a new category, schedule, school fact, release decision, or hidden reasoning transcript.

## Required sections

Owner views contain recommendation, rank and count, concise reasons, immediate action, verification and confidence, capacity, uncertainty, material sources and last-checked data, What Could Change, useful Why Not Another Category comparisons, and student controls.

## Category, rank and action explanations

Do Now explains urgency, actionability and fit. Ask First names the unknown/blocker and useful small action. Do Next explains why it follows the immediate crisis. Schedule Later explains later timing. Consider Releasing explains evidence and the private-plan safeguard. Rank text compares visible factors only within a category. Actions remain separate from tasks and say whether anything is sent.

## Verification, fallback and limitations

Confirmed, Confirmed by You, Estimated, Assumption, Unknown, Conflicting Information and Needs Recheck remain distinct from confidence. Deterministic text is authoritative and works without AI. The prototype is memory-backed and has no live source refresh, authenticated server, durable sync, complete translation provider, production AI wording, or human accessibility evidence.

## Step 11 continuation

The next phase may ask what blocks the task and offer a short prerequisite check. Its task-specific evidence is not a grade, diagnosis, or permanent mastery claim.

After repair, current task-start readiness, repair minutes and remaining barriers invalidate the old explanation. Regeneration uses the normal deterministic explanation rules and preserves repair history.

Plan-item explanations add the stabilization goal, bounded action, fit and what-could-change details. Stretch reasons must state that noncompletion does not reduce success.

Seven-day explanations add Why This Day, Why This Order and coded What Could Change conditions without private model reasoning.
## Missed-month explanations

Every course action exposes its authority, verified facts, uncertainty, gate/dependency, current-access value and capacity effect. Waiting for an authorized response is described as valid progress.
# Step 25 recalculation integration

Every revised priority shows source status, duration range, actionability, and a visible reason such as prerequisite, current unit, assessment, teacher decision, material, near completion, accepted work, student importance, human support, capacity, buffer, or deferral.
