# Review and Catch-Up Space

Review blocks cover completed work, mistakes, teacher responses, capacity, priority changes, week progress, tomorrow preparation and replanning. Catch-up reserve is different from protected buffer: it is a small 10–20-minute portion of focused capacity, may remain unused and requires explicit student activation.

Allowed reserve uses are a small overrun, missed Minimum action, mistake review, replan, teacher follow-up or student-selected catch-up. It cannot hide infeasibility, reduce essential Minimum work or be prefilled with Stretch. Unused reserve is not wasted.
