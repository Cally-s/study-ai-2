# Remaining Estimate Revision

Original and previous remaining estimates, actual time, sources, confidence, and history remain stored. The revised range covers unfinished work only: completed subactions, setup, drafts, sources, calculations, code, prerequisite repair, and teacher communication are not counted again.

Low/Unknown confidence retains a range and scheduling caution; its minimum cannot force a fit. Prerequisite repair and material retrieval are separate actions. Replacement work receives a separate estimate.
