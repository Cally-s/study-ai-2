# Stretch Plan Standard

Stretch means additional valid work only when time, capacity and energy remain after Minimum. It must be actionable, sufficiently verified, safe to leave incomplete, non-urgent, independent of unresolved teacher/material barriers and within unused focused capacity.

An empty Stretch Plan is valid and supportive. Stretch cannot use protected buffer or uncertain time, displace Minimum, reduce success, become overdue/failed, create a missed event, break a streak, trigger a warning or roll automatically. Students may later keep, move or remove an optional item.

Across seven days, Stretch remains daily, optional and gated behind that day’s Minimum/dependencies. It never fills catch-up reserve or urgent overflow.
