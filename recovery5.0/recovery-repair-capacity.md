# Recovery Repair Capacity

Quick Rescue allows 5–20 minutes. Full Recovery allows at most one preferred focus block by default. Required breaks, setup/transition time, confirmed focused capacity and stopping time are inputs; protected buffer, sleep, meals, care, work, school, transport and essential responsibilities are unavailable.

If the full path does not fit, offer a five-minute explanation, a later block, tutor/teacher help, or Save and Pause. Test-Tomorrow repair consumes real preparation capacity. Shared repair time is counted once and uses the earliest linked deadline.

Creating, changing or completing a repair invalidates feasibility and priority outputs. Completed repair time remains historical while remaining repair time becomes zero.

Minimum-plan generation consumes the same repair minutes rather than duplicating them. Remaining plan capacity is recalculated before any optional dependent practice is exposed.
