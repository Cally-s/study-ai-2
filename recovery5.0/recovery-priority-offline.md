# Recovery Priority Offline

Offline mode may display the last owner-authorized cached categories, explanations, policy version and source watermark. It labels them cached, cannot refresh official facts, recalculate from unavailable sources, send messages, approve release, delete work or claim synchronization.

Local priority preferences queue with idempotency and base versions. Reconnection compares source and plan versions; conflicting edits require review. Internal scores are not written into shareable caches, URLs or analytics.
