# Recovery Prerequisite Repair Path

Test Tomorrow reuses one bounded Step 12 repair that supports the most selected topics and fits confirmed pre-test capacity; other gaps remain not covered/help/later.

Step 16 may select one valid next repair stage but never skips sequence, duplicates repair time or marks the full repair complete.

Decomposition links to Step 12 rather than copying prerequisite records. Dependent production actions remain blocked until repair is completed or safely reviewed.

## Purpose

Step 12 answers: “What is the smallest concept bridge that could make the current task understandable and actionable?” It operates only after a supported or student-approved prerequisite gap.

## Minimum-concept principle

Targets must be narrow. “Factoring” is narrowed to a method such as factoring `x² + bx + c`; greatest-common-factor extraction remains a different concept. Broad, conflicting, unknown and unreviewed AI matches cannot become ready paths.

## Five required stages

Every path contains exactly: Plain-Language Explanation, Worked Example, Guided Practice, Independent Check and Readiness Decision. These stages are optional, pausable and ungraded.

## Bounded repair

The default has one example and two distinct practice items. No automatic extension is allowed. The student may explicitly add one example or guided item; afterward the path recommends human help or a later return.

## Duration rules

Quick Rescue is 5–20 minutes. Full Recovery uses at most one preferred focus block. Confirmed focused time, setup, breaks and stopping time are respected; protected buffer is never repair time.

## Readiness decisions

Decisions include Continue, Continue with Support, one more example/item, tutor, teacher, instructions, materials, pause and inconclusive. They are private, current-task-specific, time-limited and revisable.

## Student control

Students may simplify, pause, resume, skip, reject the match, ask for human help, choose one extension, return provisionally, delete eligible responses and control sharing.

## Known limitations

The prototype uses memory storage, reviewed synthetic mathematics fixtures and caller-supplied integrity flags. It has no production authentication, curriculum graph, answer-key service, content provider, delivery service or durable offline synchronization.

Step 13 may include one active repair as a Minimum action. The validated repair duration is counted once and dependent task/practice actions remain blocked until readiness or a supported provisional attempt.

Seven-day scheduling places the repair focus block before every dependent block and preserves shared repair deduplication.
## Missed-week repair

At most one essential repair is placed before dependent current work. The repair uses the existing bounded explanation/example/practice/check contract and never expands into recreating the full missed lesson.
## Extended-gap reuse

A bounded repair is scheduled before dependent current work and shared within a course where valid. It never expands into a missed-unit or full-course remediation path.
## Bounded alternate approaches

Step 20 orchestrates one valid repair at a time. Distinct representations are versioned; human help appears after two unsuccessful valid approaches and a third student-selected approach is the hard maximum.
