# Recovery Priority Fairness and Privacy

The engine uses only task-level academic factors with documented purpose and source. It does not infer diagnosis, mental health, character, motivation, honesty, responsibility, ability or future failure. Accessibility tools, translation, tutoring, connection limits and working speed cannot lower importance.

Internal scores, weights, thresholds and private override reasons are absent from all non-owner serializers and privacy-safe analytics. Fairness review checks factor provenance, proxy leakage, category outcomes, explanation quality and override denial patterns without ranking students. Production requires authenticated tenant isolation, encryption, retention controls and reviewed audit access.
