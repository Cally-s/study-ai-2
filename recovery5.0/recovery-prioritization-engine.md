# Transparent Recovery Prioritization Engine

Use scores to support transparent prioritization, not to replace judgement, source verification, student choice, or authority boundaries.

Step 39 applies exclusions and hard gates before a configurable internal calculation. It ranks only valid, actionable work into Do Now, Ask First, Do Next, Schedule Later, or Consider Releasing. The decision record stores inventory, verification, feasibility, policy and algorithm versions; deterministic recalculation replaces stale recommendations while retaining history.

StudySpark explains why a task is recommended. It does not show a mysterious score or treat the recommendation as final. Scores and weights never appear in student, teacher, parent, tutor, analytics, URL, export, offline-share, or public views.

Priority is separate from schedule, task size, next action, completion, release authority and student worth. The prototype is owner-keyed memory, not production persistence.

## Step 40 controlled MVP delivery

This workflow is exposed only when its Recovery stage, feature flag, tenant/account scope, earlier-stage dependencies and evidence gates permit it. Disabled later-stage controls are omitted and a safe earlier-stage alternative remains. Upgrades preserve source/version/history records; rollback blocks new later-stage writes without deleting student data. Privacy, accessibility, Academic Integrity, safety and human authority apply from Stage 1 rather than arriving as later features.
