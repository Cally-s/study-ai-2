# Recovery Prioritization Privacy

Priority categories, ranks, factors, student importance, override reasons, release candidates, and history are private recovery data. Public, teacher, tutor, parent, and guardian serializers return no priority data. Sharing is never automatic.

These records do not affect grades, tutor matching, service hours, Help Credits, safety, fraud, academic-integrity, discipline, advertising, or public profiles. Ordinary analytics accept only allowlisted event names and exclude item identity, subject, grade, factors, importance, category, override reason, release basis, time, and capacity values. Logs should use request/error codes only.

No AI provider is required by the deterministic engine. A future provider must receive the minimum authorized fields and may not decide categories or releases. Cached offline review cannot refresh official facts or recalculate; reconnection sends, releases, deletes, and changes nothing automatically. Production requires authenticated owner authorization, encrypted persistence, retention/export/deletion, organization isolation, and durable account-switch cleanup.
