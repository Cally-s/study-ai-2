# Recovery Source Privacy

Pasted lists/course text, documents, screenshots, connector state, imported records, descriptions, OCR text, excerpts, candidates and rejected drafts are private student data. They are excluded from public, teacher, tutor, parent/guardian, directory, notification, service-credit, matching, scoring and ordinary analytics/log views.

Source owner serializers omit raw text, storage keys, provider URLs, paths, tokens, credentials, hidden metadata and internal prompts. Analytics contains only reviewed event name/time. Provider processing is unavailable; no training or deletion claim is made.

Retention options support deletion after confirmed import, keeping until recovery completion or required temporary retention. Students may delete raw source alone or raw source plus unconfirmed drafts while retaining confirmed recovery items. Local deletion is distinguished from external/provider/backups. Production needs encrypted storage, processor registry, export/deletion policy and shared-device cleanup.
