# Teacher-Message Date Proposals

Recommendations use confirmed estimate/capacity, breaks/setup, Minimum plan, sleep/buffer, commitments, school days and acceptance information. Dates are exact and student approved; meeting windows include date/time/timezone. Status distinguishes realistic confirmed/provisional, tight, does-not-fit and missing estimate/capacity/acceptance. A proposal never changes or implies an official deadline; no date may depend on buffer, sleep, meals, school, work or essential commitments.
