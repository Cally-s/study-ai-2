# Recovery Task Decomposition Templates

Deterministic templates cover Presentation, Essay, Lab report, Coding project, Research project, Application, Reading, Corrections and Test preparation.

The History Presentation fixture contains: open instructions (3), highlight deliverables (5), choose the event (5), create headings (10), find one reliable source (15), record three notes (10), draft Slide 1 (15), draft Slide 2 (15), add citations (10), and rehearse the introduction (10).

Templates are not requirement sources. Missing instructions produce only course-page, downloaded-file and reviewed teacher-question actions. Unsupported requirements are never invented.
