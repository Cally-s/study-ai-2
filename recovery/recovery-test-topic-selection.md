# Test Topic Selection

Only teacher, school-system, confirmed review-sheet or student-confirmed scope is eligible. Extracted/AI-suggested topics remain provisional and cannot appear as confirmed.

The deterministic first plan selects at most three, fewer for limited time or Overwhelmed Mode. Factors include confirmed scope, teacher review/emphasis, prerequisite value across topics, student confusion/request, transfer value, partial understanding, available materials and ability to improve within time.

Reasons are visible and coded. No probability, predicted question or predicted score is generated. Other topics remain “Topics Not Covered Tonight,” optional Stretch or later review.
