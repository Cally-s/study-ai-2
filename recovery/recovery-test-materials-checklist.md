# Test Materials Checklist

Checklist items may include calculator, formula/reference sheet, notes/textbook, periodic table, dictionary, approved software/device and required login/location details. Every item keeps `CONFIRMED_ALLOWED`, `CONFIRMED_NOT_ALLOWED`, student-reported, unknown, conflicting or not-applicable status.

Unknown permission creates a confirmation action and never a recommendation to use the material. No message, download, print or calendar action occurs automatically.
