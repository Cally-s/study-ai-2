# Recovery Verification Actions

Actions include checking a course page, instructions, syllabus, submission history, gradebook, or application portal; confirming dates/required status/policy/revision/requirements/materials/prerequisites; estimating time; asking a teacher/program office/tutor; reviewing conflicts; keeping Unknown; or Not Now.

They normally take 5–15 minutes, are reversible and source grounded, and never complete graded work. Teacher questions cover official requirements/policy/dates; tutors may help with concept prerequisites or estimates but cannot confirm official policy. Draft questions group by course/teacher/session/urgency and remain editable, removable, reorderable without dragging, splittable, and unsent. No private capacity, medical, family, employer, accessibility, or Recovery Load details are included automatically.
# Priority separation

Verification actions keep their own Urgent, Important, Optional priority. A teacher question may be an urgent immediate action while the graded task remains Ask First. Drafts are never sent automatically.
