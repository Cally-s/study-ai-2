# Recovery Support Output Intake

## Teacher-help intake

Collect authorized course/teacher context, request type, relevant work/date, included/excluded facts, tone, language and desired outcome. Wording requests clarification or possibility; it never states that acceptance or flexibility was granted. Step 2 stores inputs only and sends nothing.

## Parent-friendly-summary intake

Collect purpose, included/excluded sections, language, detail and tone. Exclude diagnoses, accommodation documents, private AI conversations and teacher drafts by default.

## Recipient separation and review

Teacher and parent outputs are separate records with separate recipients/content exclusions. Later sharing requires an exact preview, editable text, authorized recipient and explicit final confirmation. Parent/guardian authority remains governed by the separate authorized-adult policy.
