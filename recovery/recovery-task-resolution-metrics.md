# Task Resolution Metrics

Valid types are Completed, Submitted, Confirmed No Longer Required, Confirmed Replaced, and Scheduled Under an Approved New Deadline. Resolved does not always mean academically completed. Submitted does not mean accepted; approved new deadline does not mean completed. Replacement keeps the original history and links a separate task. Reopened-for-revision work remains historically resolved during the window but is removed from currently resolved state and shown separately. Task/cycle dedupe prevents inflation.
