# Workload and Risk Metrics

Workload realism is a current status: Manageable, Tight but Feasible, Not Currently Feasible, Teacher Decisions Required, Capacity Uncertain, No Confirmed Capacity, or Outdated. A counted transition requires approved replanning that preserves buffer, sleep, breaks, fixed responsibilities, and honest estimates.

Risk Identified is not Addressed. Addressed requires confirmed actionable work in progress, waiting with owner and review point, mitigation, or resolution. Addressed is not always resolved. Display open/reopened risk state beside historical progress.
