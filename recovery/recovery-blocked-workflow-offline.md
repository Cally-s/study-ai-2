# Blocked Workflow Offline

Cached reason/clarification/resolution drafts/review points and provisional waiting states may save offline. External availability, recipients, messages, responses, service status, files, acceptance, and support require reconnection validation.

Nothing sends/books/shares/activates/rolls over offline or on reconnect. Source versions are revalidated; material changes require review. Idempotency prevents duplicate blocker/action/waiting records within supported storage.
