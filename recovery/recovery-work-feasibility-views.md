# Recovery Work Feasibility Views

Primary precedence is: Excluded Current Work; Work to Clarify; Work to Negotiate; Work That May Be Deferred; Work to Complete.

- **Work to Complete:** confirmed, actionable remaining work. Inclusion does not promise it fits or schedule it.
- **Work to Clarify:** materially unknown status, acceptance, date, requirements, materials, estimate, dependency, or teacher response.
- **Work to Negotiate:** confirmed work that cannot fit before a deadline and may need an authorized decision.
- **Work That May Be Deferred:** a provisional option for flexible, optional, outside-horizon, or non-urgent work.
- **Excluded Current Work:** accepted, no-longer-required, archived, removed, or submitted work with no follow-up action.

Secondary flags are explanatory only. One assessment owns each item’s minutes, so appearing in more than one explanation never double-counts workload. Moving an item requires review. Step 7 does not establish final priority or deferral.

Step 8 keeps Ready/Ready with Estimates items actionable, moves material unknown/stale items to Clarify, retains infeasible confirmed work in Negotiate, and keeps deferral provisional. A title and estimate alone never move an item to Complete.
# Step 9 integration

The Step 7 views remain editable source views. Step 9 derives five private recommendation categories without replacing, mutating, or relabelling those feasibility records.
