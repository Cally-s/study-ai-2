# Test Tomorrow Short Diagnostic

The existing Step 11 pattern is reused for 3–5 questions total, never per topic. Questions are similar, one-concept where possible, ungraded, untimed by default, and offer “I Do Not Know” and skip.

Results route to ready, recall, worked example, guided practice, likely prerequisite gap, material/scope problem, inconclusive or human help. No percentage, letter grade, mastery or predicted test score is produced. Started assessments disable answer-bearing diagnostics.
