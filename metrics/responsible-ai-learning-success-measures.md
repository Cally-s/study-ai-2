# Responsible AI Learning Success Measures

These measures show evidence that students verify information, explain thinking, improve prompts, protect privacy, disclose accountable use, respond to feedback, evaluate fairness, and transfer learning independently. The 13 stable identifiers cover the 11 requested indicator areas without combining them into a score.

Every measure uses eligible versioned evidence and a published immutable definition. Prompt totals, chat time, answer count, streaks, and activity rank are operational-only if retained and never learning outcomes. Records contain references and decisions, not private prompts, conversations, reflections, exact warnings, accessibility history, or hidden reasoning.
