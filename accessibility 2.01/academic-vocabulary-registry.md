# Academic Vocabulary Registry

The Step 12 terminology architecture is extended locally with AcademicTerm-like records and subject-scoped senses. Fixtures include Hypothesis, ambiguous Function senses, Opportunity cost, Cellular respiration, Chemical energy, Thesis statement and Conditional offer.

Each sense contains an ID/code, subject, controlled part of speech, plain/academic English definitions, approved example, optional misconception/correction, relations, pronunciation and language translations. Review statuses are Approved Glossary, Human Reviewed, Teacher Reviewed, Automatic, Review Pending, Rejected and Outdated. Rejected/outdated translations are not current.

Translation priority remains organization glossary, reviewed sources, approved dictionaries, validated automatic data, then English fallback. Registry version `2026.1` is saved with personal snapshots. This prototype has no durable reviewer, correction, pronunciation asset, dictionary or migration service.
