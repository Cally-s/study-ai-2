# AI Accessibility Commands

Step 22 keeps accessibility-command preferences private, excludes diagnoses/status narratives from AI inputs, and prohibits provider training by default. Quick actions do not create broader sharing or recording consent.

Eligible finalized `.message.ai` responses receive one consistent 11-action toolbar: Listen, Make Simpler, Add More Detail, Translate, Show Bilingual, Define Key Words, Show Step by Step, Give Another Example, Create Audio Summary, Download for Offline Use and Print Review Plan. Default scope is this response. Commands preserve original response/version and never explain why a setting was selected.
