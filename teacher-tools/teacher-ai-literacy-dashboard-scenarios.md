# Dashboard Scenarios

Scenario types include diagnostic, privacy, integrity, sources, bias, prompts, and system design. Results use explicit criteria and formative evidence statuses. Retry and human review remain available. Missing or incomplete scenario evidence creates no misconduct, ability, motivation, or honesty inference.

## Step 56 integration
Teacher-authored school scenarios and integrity cases reuse this catalog. They must be fictional, synthetic, composite, or deidentified, and cannot ask whether a real learner is guilty or dishonest.

## Step 57 assessment results
Scenario results show title, competency, progression, attempt date, response-level evidence, explicit criteria, first clarification, guided/independent condition, retry/review state, and version. Instructional needs use thresholded criteria gaps—not counts, speed, prompts, receipts, hints, or accessibility use—and never rank students.
