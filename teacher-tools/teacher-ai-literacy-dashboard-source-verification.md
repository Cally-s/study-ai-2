# Dashboard Source Verification

Source summaries distinguish retrieval, identity/existence, exact claim support, currentness, quotation accuracy, citation quality, contradiction, and unresolved evidence. Search snippets and invented sources are not verification. The dashboard links authorized summaries and preserves uncertainty rather than turning source gaps into student-risk labels.

## Step 56 integration
Approved-source records are course-scoped and human-approved. Authenticity, currentness, suitability, and exact claim support remain separate. Source-verification challenge answer keys stay server-side.
