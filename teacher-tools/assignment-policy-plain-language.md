# Assignment Policy Plain Language

Every published policy needs a clear student explanation. Supported example: “You may use StudySpark to understand concepts, ask for hints, and review your own draft. You may not use AI to write paragraphs for submission.” Text is sanitized, limited to 1,500 characters, may have validated bilingual versions, and cannot contain scripts, hidden commands, threats, discriminatory rules, or accessibility-as-cheating claims. Structured settings remain authoritative and conflicts block publication.
