# Policy Conflict and Human Review

StudySpark may identify a mismatch between a trusted policy decision and a request, but never decides misconduct. The reminder-first workflow explains policy, redirects to permitted support, records the intervention, offers student context, checks a published escalation rule, and requests authorized human review only when required. The six statuses are No Conflict, Policy Reminder Shown, Student Changed Request, Possible Conflict — Context Needed, Teacher Review Requested, and Resolved. No consequence is automatic.

## Step 56 boundary
Teacher-created lessons and uploaded-policy drafts cannot trigger a conflict outcome until the relevant policy version is intentionally published. Lesson-tool records provide context only and never decide guilt or consequences.
