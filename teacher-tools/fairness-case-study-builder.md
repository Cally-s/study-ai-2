# Fairness Case Study Builder

Every fairness case addresses representation, language, accessibility, error harm, affected people, human oversight, mitigation, a non-AI alternative, and whether deployment is justified. Sensitive traits are never inferred about real people. Teachers review context and limitations before publication.

## Step 57 use
Assessment rubrics accept multiple justified mitigations and a justified **Do not deploy** decision. They evaluate design reasoning rather than a single hidden architecture or equal numerical outcomes.
