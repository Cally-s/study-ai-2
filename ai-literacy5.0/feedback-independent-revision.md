# Independent Feedback Revision

The student reviews intention, answers the revision question, selects or creates a strategy, edits the existing version independently, saves a new version, compares original/revised text, explains changes, checks resolution, evidence/rubric alignment, meaning, and voice. AI cannot apply text or mark completion without student-authored content.

## Step 20 — Revision With Judgment

Step 20 adds explicit used/partial/rejected/alternative decisions, four-part comparison, justified-rejection review, and independent verification to this same revision history.
