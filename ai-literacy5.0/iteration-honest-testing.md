# Honest Testing

Predefine expected behaviour where practical; include normal, edge, failure, safety, privacy, accessibility, fairness, low-confidence, review, and outage cases. Preserve passed, failed, partial, inconclusive, invalid, blocked and not-run results; keep privacy-safe failed evidence; explain invalid/excluded runs and corrections; record provenance/repeats; do not cherry pick, modify raw output, or change expectations after the run.
