# Translation Assistant Case

Seven synthetic scenarios compare standard/beginner English, Mandarin-English, French-English, speech-to-text, misspelling, and screen-reader use. Bilingual outputs lose English terms, speech users correct more errors, and screen-reader users miss source labels. Mitigation preserves terms beside translations, validates terminology, confirms speech input, labels sources accessibly, retains a static fallback, and retests. Only tested terms/languages are reviewed; broader support and human language review remain unresolved.
