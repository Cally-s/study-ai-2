# Tutor and Teacher Sharing Standard

Step 27 complete preferences are never a tutor/teacher endpoint. Only separately granted, allowlisted functional fields are projected for the named recipient/scope until expiry or revocation.

Step 23 keeps teacher and tutor authoring authority separate. Tutor preview requires an active session/resource relationship and grants no course publishing or learner-setting authority.

Sharing defaults to nothing. Previewable functional fields include explanation language/level, captions, text-only participation, assistive reading/input choices, large text, reduced motion, and low bandwidth. Diagnoses, medical information, accommodation documents, full profiles, private transcripts, recordings, histories, reasons, and privacy requests are excluded.

Tutor and teacher grants must remain separate, recipient- and session/course-scoped, server-authorized on every read, auditable, expiring, and revocable. The current prototype creates previews only, not real grants.
