# Competency Portfolio Privacy

Public output excludes email, phone, address, student number, precise location/schedule, social accounts, direct contact, EXIF, comments/revision history, hidden slides/cells, internal filenames/paths, creator metadata, private receipts/reflections/feedback/group notes, safety content, diagnoses, and unrelated records. Parents receive no automatic access. Students can correct metadata/evidence links without suspicion. Revocation preserves private and required institutional records.
# Step 61 message preflight

AI-generated portfolio summaries pass through Step 61 before provider dispatch. Public Portfolio destination decisions continue to use Step 59 eligibility and sharing rules. Privacy findings, drafts, false-positive choices, secrets, medical/safety content, and audit events are never portfolio evidence or public content.

UJ-040, UJ-041, and UJ-043 add teacher-view, reasoning-evidence, and parent-report privacy assertions.
