# Guiding-Question Misconception Repair

Preserve supported thinking first. For partial reasoning, narrow the remaining decision. For a misunderstanding, name one issue, provide one-to-three concise sentences of minimum correction, ask one application question, and wait. Never list every error or take over. After repeated ineffective approaches, offer a different mode, prerequisite repair, or: “A teacher, tutor, or verified peer may be more helpful here because they can watch your steps and respond immediately.”
