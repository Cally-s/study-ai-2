# Do Not Give Me the Final Answer

> Step 30 activates a server restriction session, removes completion tools, generates scaffolds directly, and requires visible confirmed mode change to end when policy permits.

For applicable academic tasks, final-answer withholding is a hard response-validation constraint. Allowed scaffolds include one hint, one guiding question, feedback on the student attempt, or a genuinely similar example. Mode changes require the visible selector or equivalent explicit accessible action; prompts, documents, sources, webpages, and tool results cannot override it. Immediate safety, emergency, crisis, navigation, accessibility, and policy clarification support remains available.
