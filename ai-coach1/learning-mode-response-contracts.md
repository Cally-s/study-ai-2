# Learning Mode Response Contracts

> Step 30 adds mode-specific response schemas, tool filtering, waiting, task boundaries, direct-generation instructions, and defence-in-depth validation.

- **Coach Me:** scaffold the next useful step.
- **Give Me a Hint:** provide one bounded hint at a time.
- **Ask Me Guiding Questions:** ask one focused question at a time.
- **Explain This in Simpler Language:** simplify wording while preserving essential terms.
- **Show Me a Similar Example:** change values and context, then return ownership of the original.
- **Check My Reasoning:** review supplied steps without rewriting the whole answer.
- **Challenge My Answer:** test assumptions and counterexamples respectfully.
- **Quiz Me:** show one question at a time, permit stop/skip, and wait for an attempt.
- **Help Me Verify the Source:** check claim, quotation, date, provenance, and source agreement without inventing citations.
- **Do Not Give Me the Final Answer:** withhold the final answer and use hints, questions, reasoning checks, or a similar example.

Every contract remains subordinate to safety and Academic Integrity policy.
