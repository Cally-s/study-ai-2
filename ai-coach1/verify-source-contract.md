# Verify Source Contract

> Source-comparison quiz questions use approved or verifiable sources, server-held rubrics, accessible source pairing, and configured feedback release without invented provenance.

> Conflicting-evidence and source-reliability challenges require verified source provenance and accurate scope/method representation; unavailable verification is reported, never replaced with invented evidence.

Extract claims, classify them, identify suitable evidence, review exact support, date/currentness and quotation accuracy, compare sources, disclose disagreement and uncertainty, then ask for revision. Never invent title, author, publisher, date, quotation, DOI, URL, excerpt, or review. No reliable source is not equivalent to false.
