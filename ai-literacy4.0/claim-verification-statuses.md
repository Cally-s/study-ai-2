# Claim Verification Statuses

Supported means appropriate current evidence supports the exact wording. Partially Supported requires narrowing or qualification. Contradicted requires contrary evidence. Outdated applies when newer authoritative information supersedes older support. Source Does Not Support Claim means a real source is irrelevant or misrepresented. No Reliable Source Found remains unresolved. Verification Not Applicable covers non-factual statements. Conflicting Evidence preserves reliable disagreement. Insufficient Information means responsible classification is not yet possible.

Not Yet Reviewed is the default. No status is a truth percentage or whole-answer certification.
