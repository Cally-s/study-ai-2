# Impact Reflection

> This is the canonical reflection extended by Step 28. Existing reflection IDs and references are preserved; no duplicate impact reflection or deployment-decision system is created.

Reflect on benefits, harms, exclusion, errors, collected and avoided data, human work, maintenance, accessibility, fairness, misuse, computing cost, feedback, failure, expert review, shutdown, and reasons not to expand or deploy. Disclose AI assistance.

## System-card relationship

Impact findings, limits, stop conditions, and reasons not to deploy feed linked card sections while remaining identifiable as student analysis.

## Iteration consequences

Reflection carries observed improvement, what worsened, unequal benefits/burdens, accessibility/privacy/fairness/safety, workload, maintenance, scope, AI appropriateness and deployment decision into impact records.
