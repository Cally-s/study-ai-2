# Appropriate AI Citation and Disclosure

> **Step 50 AI Use Receipt contract:** Meaningful validated events—not transcripts, clicks, timing, accessibility, safety, or model recollection—compose an immutable private receipt. A separate physically redacted shareable version preserves the exact eleven labels, policy/map snapshots, AI-versus-student provenance, preview and intentional recipient confirmation, immutable share history, and no automatic sharing, scoring, surveillance, or discipline.


> **Step 49 AI Use Map contract:** This feature uses the immutable, tenant-scoped AIUseMapResolution. General guidance never replaces verified policy; source authenticity and required disclosure remain enforced conditions.

Students review accurate suggested disclosure before intentional sharing. AI Use Receipts begin only after meaningful assistance, not when the map is viewed or a use is planned. Accessibility presentation use and safety support are excluded by default. Invented citations, quotations, pages, and hidden AI involvement cannot be authorized through free text; clearly labelled synthetic practice sources require a verified instructional exception.
