# Answer Verification Privacy

Answers, extracted claims, queries, opened sources, notes, relationships, disagreement, revisions, records, reflections, and evidence are private by default. Step 9 runs before external search, file/tool use, or speech transcript storage.

Raw content is excluded from ordinary analytics, logs, URLs, public profiles, and model training by default. Teachers, tutors, guardians, administrators, and other students receive no automatic access. Production must add retention, deletion, and audited access.
