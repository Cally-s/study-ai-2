# AI Coach Policy Resolution

> Step 67 integration: resolved `AI_NOT_ALLOWED` triggers general-guidance/accessibility-only boundaries; `POLICY_UNKNOWN` triggers guided support with a final-answer lock. Neither the model nor client may reinterpret the trusted status or invent an exception.

Step 2 loads the immutable effective assignment-policy version, allowed modes, restricted actions, attempt/disclosure/source/receipt rules, final-answer rule, dates, and teacher explanation. Precedence is platform safety, protected workflow, safeguard snapshot, organization, school, programme, course, assignment, task context, active assessment, selected mode, then preference. A lower rule cannot weaken a higher one; conflicting rules use the most protective academic boundary.

Expired or unknown policy becomes `POLICY_UNKNOWN`: accessibility remains available, while support is limited to clarification, concepts, questions, and policy-safe coaching. Final answers remain locked. Assignment ownership and course membership are validated, and historical runs retain the exact policy version and snapshot.
