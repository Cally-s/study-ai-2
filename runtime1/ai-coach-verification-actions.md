# AI Coach Verification Actions

Verification actions are ordered and concrete: open a named source location, confirm an exact quotation, recalculate from original numbers, confirm a policy date, test a rule with an example, or ask the teacher about a specific permission. Each may link to a claim, source, or policy version.

“Check the answer” is invalid. A checkmark alone does not establish source support. Offline source actions remain pending, and completing an action does not automatically create formal evidence.
