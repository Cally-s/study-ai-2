# Quiz Offline Standard

Visible answer drafts may save locally with quiz/source version; hidden answers, scoring keys, teacher feedback and restricted content never enter the cache. Saved answers are not a submission and reconnection never submits a quiz by itself.

Production timed/high-stakes assessments require authenticated server time, signed start/deadline metadata and policy governing offline work/submission. Device clock alone cannot extend/shorten deadlines. An explicit queued submission must pass current authorization, deadline and version checks and use idempotency. Expired/conflicting results require honest review; duplicate prevention cannot change correct answers or grading policy.
