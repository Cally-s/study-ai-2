# Prompt Iteration Stop Rules

Stop when the goal is met and verification is complete, AI is inappropriate, policy restricts use, privacy requires another channel, a teacher/tutor/expert is needed, or revisions show diminishing returns. Verification, an official source, calculator/code tool, non-AI method, rejection, or human help may be better than another prompt.

After repeated unsuccessful revisions to the same issue, examine missing evidence, model limitation, task mismatch, policy, or human-support needs rather than rewarding retry count.
