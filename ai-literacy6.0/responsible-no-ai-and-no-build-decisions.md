# Responsible No-AI and No-Build Decisions

> Step 28 treats a supported non-AI redesign, no-public-release, no-deployment, pause, or stop decision as successful academic completion rather than failure.

Non-AI deliverables and no-build decisions are full outcomes. High-stakes prediction, diagnosis, punishment, surveillance, and sensitive inference require stopping or specialist review. These decisions create positive ethics evidence and carry no competency penalty.
