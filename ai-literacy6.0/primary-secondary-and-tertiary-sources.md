# Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary Sources

Primary sources provide original evidence/records/data/experience; secondary sources analyze or synthesize; tertiary sources compile; mixed sources combine; unclear remains unresolved. These classifications are claim specific.

Primary is not always strongest. A systematic review may outweigh one small study for a broad conclusion; a firsthand account is primary for one person’s experience, not a population statistic; current official policy is primary for the policy itself.
