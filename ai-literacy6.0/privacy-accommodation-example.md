# Accommodation Redaction Example

Original: “My full name is [FULL NAME], my student number is [STUDENT NUMBER], and I need help writing a message about my medical diagnosis.” Safer: “I am a secondary student. Help me write a general message requesting a meeting about an accommodation.” Remove the name/number, generalize diagnosis to functional need, preserve the meeting/template goal, and use `[Student Name]`, `[Course]`, and `[Requested Meeting Time]` only later in the official school channel.
