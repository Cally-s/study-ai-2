# Prompt, Response, and Task Problems

A prompt problem includes unclear goals, missing context, unsuitable help/attempt rules, excessive constraints, absent source/verification plans, privacy risk, or policy uncertainty. A response problem includes ignored instructions, unsupported claims, invented citations, calculation/code/quotation failures, missing limitations, or excessive detail.

A task problem means AI may be unnecessary or inappropriate, or a teacher, tutor, official source, secure channel, expert, group member, physical observation, or safety support is required. The next action may be revision, rejection, verification, another tool, human support, or stopping.
