# Tutoring Request

## Step 27 — Right-Time Human Support

This flow may create one explained primary recommendation and up to two alternatives when authority, context, live interaction, formal support, safety, or supervised work is needed. Students may ask at any time. Only valid, accessible, language-appropriate, materially distinct attempts count toward the two-attempt recommendation and three-attempt AI stop. All sharing is previewed and student controlled; no message, booking, parent notification, official decision, attendance, service hour, Help Credit, or plan change occurs automatically.

## Step 28 — Private Recovery Progress Recognition

Only specific, meaningful, source-versioned actions with sufficient evidence may receive brief recognition explaining why the action matters. Partial, prepared, uploaded, booked, proposed, or provisional states are never overstated as completed, sent, submitted, attended, accepted, approved, or active. Recognition is private, cumulative, non-resetting, dismissible, preference controlled, bundled at natural stopping points, and suppressed during focused work, assessments, meetings, tutoring, sensitive forms, and immediate-safety support. It creates no streak, points, rank, badge, public sharing, grade, matching effect, service hour, Help Credit, safety decision, or automatic notification.

## Step 29 — Better Recovery Metrics

This integration contributes only authoritative, versioned, deduplicated events to clearly defined metric cards. Draft, proposed, uploaded, booked, identified, timed, partial, or provisional states are never overstated as sent, approved, submitted, attended, addressed, resolved, repaired, or completed. Historical progress remains visible beside reopened/current state. Cards may overlap but are never summed; there is no global score, unstable backlog percentage, streak, points, rank, comparison, automatic sharing, or consequential use.
