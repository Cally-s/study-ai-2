# AI Coach Minimum Recording

After validation, the runtime may retain the decision run/steps, session update, one response-content reference, assumptions, actions, warnings, source/claim records, transparency snapshot, receipt-offer state, and privacy-safe audit. It does not duplicate the prompt or response.

Rejected candidates, chain of thought, scratchpads, token reasoning, privacy findings, removed text, secrets, raw safety content/audio, diagnoses, inferred traits, full source content, and credentials are excluded. Audit contains actor/tenant/session/run and version/status codes only, is not teacher-facing by default, and never becomes a behavior or ranking profile. Production retention remains tenant-configured.
