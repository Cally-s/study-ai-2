# Core Rule: AI Not Allowed

`ASSIGNMENT_AI_NOT_ALLOWED` preserves keyboard, screen reader, TTS, reviewed STT, captions, transcripts, resizing/spacing, contrast, Reduced Motion, dyslexia presentation, print, approved translation, bilingual terms, and alternative input. Accessibility changes presentation without solving or interpreting the task.

Only detached general study planning/strategy/concept review, protected support, and editable teacher-clarification drafting remain. Assignment-specific hints, examples, research, debugging, outlines, revision, model answers, and final answers are denied. Ambiguity uses the restrictive interpretation. The rule creates no discipline or automatic teacher/parent alert.
