# Core Rule: Low Confidence

`LOW_CONFIDENCE_REQUIRES_NEXT_STEP` requires a concise evidence-based reason plus a specific verification action. High-impact uncertainty may add authorized human support. Confidence uses labels, never arbitrary percentages, and describes support—not the model’s personality, student ability, or a probability guarantee.

Low or Insufficient Evidence does not mean false. Recommended actions point to the unresolved claim, source, calculation, date, quotation, assumption, policy, or person who can resolve it.
