# Policy Conflict Evidence Minimization

Review packages contain references, safe summaries, relevant policy/capability/condition, minimal excerpts only when necessary, alternatives, submitted context, and evidence limitations. They exclude full conversations, unrelated prompts, private drafts/receipts, unsubmitted reflections, safety/counselling content, medical/accommodation diagnoses, immigration/family information, tutor conversations, hidden reasoning, system prompts, detectors, counts, keystrokes, and browser activity.
