# Student System Card Review

## Step 59 portfolio sharing
Only a selected submitted System Card version may enter a sanitized share. Secret scanning excludes keys, tokens, internal URLs/prompts, confidential references, and teacher-private comments. Later drafts remain private.

## Step 58 Create evidence
Create Assessment reuses intentionally submitted Step 23 System Card versions. Review remains separate and cannot edit the card, expose secrets, infer authorship/honesty, or automatically recognize Create.

Only System Cards intentionally submitted or shared through Step 23 may be reviewed. Teachers use criteria references and student-facing feedback. The original submission is immutable, and review cannot add hidden risk scores, probability estimates, rankings, honesty judgments, diagnoses, automatic grades, or automatic competency decisions.
