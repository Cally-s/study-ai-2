# Integrity Case Study Builder

Case studies teach evidence-based decisions without asking whether a named learner is guilty, honest, suspicious, or likely to cheat. They use fictional, synthetic, composite, or deidentified situations; separate observations from interpretations; include a non-punitive human-review path; and preserve student response choices.

## Step 57 use
Assessment responses are hypothetical and never create a Step 55 case, misconduct alert, consequence, intent inference, or evidence about the respondent's behavior. Students explain ownership/policy issues, missing context, permitted support, and responsible next actions.
