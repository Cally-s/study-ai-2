# Dashboard Evidence

Eligible evidence comes from validated scenarios, source work, submitted reflections and receipts, capstone checkpoints, competencies, Teach-Back, Independent Retry, and contribution checkpoints. Every item preserves source/version, criteria, date, sharing scope, review state, and guided/independent conditions. Missing evidence remains Not Started, Not Submitted, or Not enough evidence yet. Evidence statuses describe one artifact and never become permanent student labels; formal conclusions may require human review.
