# Plain-Language Authoring Standard

Step 24 keeps complexity warnings heuristic and requires human meaning/equivalence review against the exact original version.

Plain-language drafts must preserve the source InstructionMeaningContract: required actions, order, exact controls, inputs/outputs, conditions, exceptions, warnings, dates, units, academic terms, integrity and safety restrictions. Vague labels and multi-action sentences are advisory warnings. Drafts are generated from the original source version, validated, human-reviewed and separately published; simplification never lowers academic expectations.
