# MVP Privacy and Analytics

Accessibility/language/transcript/low-bandwidth/study-mode/audio/simplification use remains private and excluded from public profiles, rankings, grades, matching, attendance, credits, safety/fraud/integrity scores, standing and advertising. Tutor sharing remains explicit and functional-field-only.

Analytics uses a controlled aggregate event allowlist. Exact content, transcript/voice data, diagnosis, language history, private notes, learner IDs, rankings and ability/dependency scores are rejected or omitted. The client stores demonstration events in memory only; production purpose, retention, processor and consent policy is unavailable.
