# Explanation Level Academic Invariants

All levels share one original source ID/version/hash and one `AcademicContentContract`. The following cannot be derived from or changed by `explanationLevel`: academic level, course/grade, curriculum expectation, learning objective, success criteria, assessment construct or correct answer.

The contract preserves required terms and definitions, facts, reasoning/instruction order, names, numbers, units, dates, variables, signs, exponents, equations, formulas, answer choices, code, diagrams represented in source metadata, conditions, exceptions, warnings, citations, quotations and academic-integrity restrictions. Qualifications such as “may,” “some,” “often,” “approximately,” “unless,” and “except” are meaning-bearing.

Generation must start from the original, not a prior adaptation. Detectable omissions, unauthorized content, high-stakes text and active-assessment answer exposure fail closed. Exact validation does not prove semantic entailment, factual equivalence, preserved nuance or teacher review; the interface says so and retains the original.
# Step 12 bilingual invariant

Bridge language and mode are presentation metadata. They cannot change academic level, course/grade, objective, source version or explanation level. English and bridge blocks share the contract hash and semantic source block; independent semantic equivalence is still unavailable.
