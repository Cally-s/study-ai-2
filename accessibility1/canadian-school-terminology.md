# Canadian School Terminology

Local synthetic records expand OSSD, OSSLT and IEP. OSSD/OSSLT are scoped to Ontario; IEP explicitly notes provincial/board variation. Every record is `REVIEW_PENDING`, non-authoritative, has no fabricated URL/effective date, and displays the school-system notice.

No requirements, deadlines or eligibility rules are asserted. Production records require province/territory, board/institution scope, term/abbreviation, definition/conditions, effective date, official source URL/title, last verification, review status/version and translated terminology. Students should verify current meaning/actions with their school and official source.
