# Custom Reading Comfort Options

Step 23 treats reading-comfort preview as a generic functional preset, not evidence of diagnosis or a complete accessibility test.

These reading options may make text more comfortable to use. Different students prefer different combinations. No diagnosis is required, and the settings do not change the academic content of your lessons.

## Principles and controls

Every option is independent and optional. Students may use word, letter, line, and paragraph spacing; reading width; logical alignment; an approved reading-font stack; reduced decorative italics/fonts; reduced visual clutter; paragraph/line highlighting; and a reading ruler in any combination. StudySpark remains usable with all options off.

The suggested preset writes explicit Relaxed letter/word spacing, Expanded line/paragraph spacing, Short width, logical alignment, reduced decorative typography, and reduced clutter. It does not enable a font, current-line highlight, ruler, TTS/STT, academic simplification, or lower level. Individual edits mark the result customized; session Undo is available.

## Fonts and language

Only StudySpark default and system-provided font stacks are exposed. No remote URL or uploaded font is accepted. A language/script fallback replaces Latin-only stacks for RTL/CJK content while preserving the preference for supported blocks. Code, math, forms, tables, identifiers, and non-space-separated scripts are excluded from disruptive typography.

## Reading focus and ruler

Current Paragraph and experimental Current Line use aria-hidden, non-focusable, pointer-transparent overlays. The logical anchor is a prose block; Range geometry is used when available and paragraph geometry is the fallback. Resize, scroll, font readiness, and dynamic content schedule recalculation.

The ruler supports Highlight Band, Focus Strip, and Dim Surrounding Content in Small/Medium/Large sizes. Manual and Follow Reading Focus are exposed. Follow TTS is unavailable because the prototype has no reliable speech-boundary provider. Visible keyboard buttons move, resize, hide controls, reopen controls, or disable the ruler; dragging is never required. Exact position, reading speed, and history are never persisted or analyzed.

## Privacy and academic preservation

Settings reuse the private AccessibilityLanguage profile and existing device snapshot. They are excluded from public profiles, matching, grades, safety/fraud/integrity scoring, service hours, Help Credits, restrictions, and analytics. They never rewrite AI or lesson content.

## Limitations

Automated source tests pass. Current Line and ruler are PARTIAL pending real Range geometry, zoom/reflow, controller collision, selection/copy, screen-reader, forced-colours, RTL, Study Room, and performance testing. No medical or universal-benefit claim is made.

TTS can follow at sentence level through a pointer-transparent overlay. Reliable word boundaries are not claimed; a word request falls back to sentence. Ruler synchronization is PARTIAL and manual verification remains pending.

Step 8 preserves all ordinary text fields and reading options. The fixed review panel is static, scrollable, reflowing, and contains no animated waveform. Dictation never changes reading-ruler or dyslexia preferences.

Step 9 captions/transcripts preserve source wording and academic terminology. Caption size/background are independent from reading presets; history/search remain ordinary semantic text. No caption preference changes ruler, font, spacing or source content.

Step 10 output remains ordinary semantic text and inherits font, spacing, width, focus and ruler settings. Required academic terms are visible text rather than color-only emphasis. Adaptation never changes dyslexia-friendly preferences.
# Step 11 explanation levels

Explanation level and dyslexia-friendly presentation remain independent. Generated text inherits the active font/spacing display; selecting a level does not infer diagnosis or ability. Combined ruler/focus manual testing remains PENDING.
# Step 12 script safety

Bridge blocks inherit active display settings, but script-specific letter/word spacing is not automatically increased by this module. Language Bridge selection does not imply diagnosis. English/Chinese/Arabic plus ruler/focus combinations remain PENDING.
# Step 13 vocabulary card

Cards inherit text/spacing/display settings and retain English plus translation. They do not infer diagnosis or modify source text. Script-specific typography, ruler and focus combinations remain PENDING.
# Step 14 multilingual responses

Response blocks inherit active presentation while language-specific spacing safeguards remain independent. Split-level simpler English is presentation, not diagnosis/proficiency inference. Cross-script/ruler/focus manual testing remains PENDING.
# Step 15 preservation

Text-first mode keeps source text/spacing/fonts/ruler independent and removes no curriculum content. Draft/status UI inherits display preferences. Combined offline/reflow/ruler testing remains PENDING.

All Step 16 text tools inherit independent reading settings. Switching mode preserves them and never replaces structured content with media-only tasks.
Step 18 printable source text remains selectable and semantic with readable spacing; no font choice changes the academic content. Physical font/line/page verification is pending.
Step 20 guides inherit reading/display preferences and remain complete plain text; no media-only instruction is permitted.
