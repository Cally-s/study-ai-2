# AI Feedback Mode

> Step 30 reasoning feedback identifies the first meaningful issue and waits; challenge feedback uses one supported challenge and requires defence or revision.

> “Check My Reasoning” and “Challenge My Answer” reuse feedback behavior while requiring a student attempt where needed and avoiding full-answer rewrites.

Purpose: help students see, understand, and revise their own work. A meaningful student attempt, reviewed provenance, policy, and privacy check are required first. Feedback may address clarity, organization, reasoning, evidence, rubric alignment, grammar patterns, counterarguments, and reader questions. It cannot rewrite or apply changes. The static prototype lacks durable server enforcement and live feedback.

## Step 20 — Revision With Judgment

Step 20 consumes Step 19 issue/version records rather than generating a second feedback system.

## Community projects

Feedback can review problem framing, workflow, testing, fairness, accessibility, and impact. It remains advice for student judgment, not an automatic change.
