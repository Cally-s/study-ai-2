# Create Pathway

> Step 28 adds the six-phase lesson “Who Benefits, Who Bears the Risk, and Should We Release It?” and separates Create-level completion from deployment authorization.

Students scope responsible AI systems for real needs without receiving a complete project. The four competencies are Citizenship in the era of AI, Ethics by design, Creating AI tools, and Iteration and feedback loops.

Starter: **Scope an AI Project**. Students decide the problem, affected people, AI appropriateness, non-AI alternatives, data, privacy/bias/safety risks, human review, success/failure criteria, and community benefit. Only synthetic or authorized data is permitted.

Create always remains available, regardless of diagnostic completion or recommendation.

Independent Create evidence requires student-led decisions across distinct contexts or a substantial designed, tested, explained, and reflected project.

Create lessons retain all six phases and never auto-generate the student’s project or explanation.

## Community AI Project Pathway

“From Community Need to Responsible Prototype” is the Create-level capstone. Students may finish with AI, hybrid, non-AI, human-led, further-research, or responsible no-build decisions.

## Step 22

“Define the Problem Before the Tool” and Problem Scoping Studio are mandatory before Step 21 design and build stages.

## Step 23

“Document the System, Its Limits, and Its Responsibilities” teaches the canonical living AI System Card for all project outcomes.

## Step 24

“Design the System, Not Just the Model” teaches accessible full-system architecture and gates functional AI/hybrid prototyping.

## Step 25

“Plan the Data Before You Use It” and Data Responsibility Studio gate every dataset action and provide the StudySpark Safe Sample Data Library.

## Step 26

“Test Who Benefits, Who Is Missed, and Who Bears the Harm” provides a scenario-based Fairness Evaluation Lab and broad-use/completion gate.

## Step 27

“Test, Learn, Revise, and Explain” provides an immutable Iteration and Feedback Studio and final/completion/competency-review gate.
