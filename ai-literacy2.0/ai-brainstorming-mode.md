# AI Brainstorming Mode

Purpose: expand possibilities without completing assignments. Allowed outputs are bounded questions, perspectives, topics, categories, research directions, stakeholder groups, constraints, assumptions, and project-direction seeds. Students set boundaries, choose/reject/combine, add their own idea, verify assumptions, and develop independently. The static prototype has no live generator or durable server enforcement.

## Step 19 — AI Feedback

Step 19 routes blank drafts to guiding questions, a private outline, or Brainstorming Mode instead of generating a draft and calling it feedback.

## Community projects

Brainstorming may explore needs, questions, alternatives, risks, and test ideas, but must not presume that AI is appropriate or fabricate stakeholder findings.
