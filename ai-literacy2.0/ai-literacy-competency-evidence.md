# Competency Evidence

> Evidence-based private-prototype, non-AI, no-deployment, and project-stop conclusions are valid Step 28 candidates and receive no automatic competency status.

Accepted evidence includes meaningful scenarios, error correction, verification, prompt improvement, integrity reasoning, source/privacy/bias decisions, system evaluation, scopes, architectures, prototypes/tests, iteration, reflection, disclosure, revisions, explanations, research, projects, and authorized teacher/tutor/portfolio artifacts.

Every record preserves owner, competency, type, source/context/version, student contribution, support, verification, Academic Integrity, privacy, validity, and policy version. Only accepted, verified, integrity-safe evidence supports final decisions.

Lesson candidates add lesson/phase/version provenance and require completed Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect phases.

System-classification evidence records purpose/method/output/AI-involvement/responsibility reasoning and treats responsible uncertainty as valid evidence.
# Step 6 evidence

Human Agency candidates include version, phase/activity, competency mapping, student contribution, AI support, support level, verification, Academic Integrity status, summary, source version, and privacy classification. Student review precedes ingestion.
# Unsupported-output evidence

Only meaningful investigation, explanation, correction, and application can become evidence candidates. Opening, listening, time, prompt count, or lesson completion cannot.
# Training-data/bias evidence

Evidence requires a student-authored audit, evidence/harm explanation, task-appropriateness decision, and meaningful redesign. Passive viewing, identity disclosure, or a simple “biased” label is not evidence.
# Prompt-privacy evidence

Evidence records the student’s minimization, transformation, third-party/secure-channel reasoning, preserved goal, and review. No raw secret or private record belongs in evidence.
# Ownership-spectrum evidence

Evidence records assignment goal, student/AI contributions, verification, disclosure, policy, readiness, next step, plan, and explanation—never percentages, detector output, metadata, style, or accessibility use.
# Policy-awareness evidence

Evidence records requested task, authority, source/provenance/version, scope/currentness, state, disclosure/conflict, uncertainty, and safe action—never a compliance score.
# Prompt Coach evidence boundary

Prompt Coach evidence remains private until student review. The lesson cannot award Demonstrated or Applied Independently; the Competency Progress Engine makes any later decision from accepted evidence.

# Step 13 boundary

Prompt/response version IDs, hypothesis, comparison mode, revision types, policy/privacy, and verification status accompany candidate evidence. Retry count, prompt length, and AI-generated comparison text are excluded.

Step 14 candidates include answer version, reviewed claim/source IDs, claim statuses, student decisions, privacy and policy. Search count, source count, and AI extraction alone are excluded.

Step 15 candidates include claim/source/comparison versions and student weight explanations; automatic rankings and source counts are excluded.

## Step 16 — Claim–Evidence Map

Claim-map candidates retain answer, claim, source, relationship, reason, limitation, and record versions plus student review.

## Step 17 — AI Citation and Disclosure

Step 17 candidates preserve activity, policy, source, revision, receipt, and disclosure versions with student acceptance or decline.

## Step 18 — AI Brainstorming

Step 18 candidates preserve goal/boundaries, generation, decisions, student idea, combination/provenance, assumptions, and development versions.

## Step 19 — AI Feedback

Step 19 candidates preserve draft/issue/basis/decision/revision/version/voice/policy evidence with student review.

## Step 20 — Revision With Judgment

Step 20 candidates preserve feedback versions, suggestion decisions, semantic comparison, explanation, provenance, verification, rubric/policy, and receipt links.

## Step 21 evidence

Eligible records include need verification, stakeholder planning, appropriateness analysis, architecture, data minimization, tests, revisions, impact reflection, provenance, and responsible stop decisions. Human review remains required.
