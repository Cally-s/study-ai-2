# Cite the Evidence, Disclose the Assistance

Purpose: teach original-source citation, AI-output attribution, and AI-use disclosure as distinct responsibilities. The learning goal is to verify AI-suggested references, describe actual AI and student contributions, create a private AI Use Receipt, and approve a policy-specific disclosure. Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect are implemented. The static prototype lacks durable server records and live citation services.
