# Important Assessment Translation Standard

Important-assessment translations must display: “Machine translation may not perfectly represent the original assessment wording. Compare the translation with the original and follow the teacher’s or school’s authorized version.” Original authorized wording remains authoritative.

Allowed support requires explicit assessment/language policy and must preserve construct, wording relationship, answer choices and hidden answers. Tested vocabulary or reading-comprehension wording is prohibited when translation/simplification changes the skill. Teacher/accommodation-authorized alternates require real metadata. The prototype blocks active assessment multilingual controls by default and refuses hidden/teacher content; important English-only responses carry the notice.
