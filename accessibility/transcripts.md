# Transcripts

Verbatim, edited-for-clarity, bilingual, audio-description-integrated, and final-live transcripts preserve title, speaker relations, timestamps, required terms, exact quotations, source references, meaningful sounds, and necessary visual descriptions. Edited text cannot change meaning. Machine output is labelled until reviewed. Users can open, search, jump, print, download, and report problems. Student transcripts remain private and protected answers/safety content stay outside ordinary lesson systems.
