# Explanation Level Standard

Status: implemented as a deterministic prototype; manual testing PENDING.

| Level | Presentation intent | Step 10 mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1: Beginner | Basic idea first, short sentences, required terms defined | `BEGINNER_FRIENDLY` |
| Level 2: Simple | Direct sentences and plain supporting vocabulary | `PLAIN_LANGUAGE` |
| Level 3: Standard Academic | Original approved academic presentation | `STANDARD_ACADEMIC` |
| Level 4: Advanced | More precise detail about the same concept | `DETAILED_TECHNICAL` |

Level 1 is not lower-grade curriculum and Level 4 is not later-course curriculum. Required terminology, equations, code, quantities, qualifications, exceptions, warnings, citations and correct answers remain visible and exact where the contract requires them. No selection supports conclusions about intelligence, literacy, language proficiency, disability or ability.

Structures are orthogonal: `STANDARD`, `STEP_BY_STEP`, `GLOSSARY_FIRST`, `EXAMPLE_FIRST`. Enrichment requires an explicit request, source permission, a clear enrichment label, and an unchanged objective. This prototype has no enrichment or approved example provider and reports those limitations.
