# Language-Equivalence Review

Review compares objectives, required terms, assignment/privacy/safety/final-answer policy, evidence/rubric criteria, source relationships, confidence, assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, action, difficulty, answer availability, and prohibited assistance. Meaning matters more than word count. “May” cannot become “will,” “policy unknown” cannot become “allowed,” and “human review required” cannot become misconduct. High-impact policy and ambiguous assessment translations require authorized human review and immutable versions.
