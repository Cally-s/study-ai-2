# MVP Quality Checks

Stage 5 reuses the Accessible Resource Studio, shared warning engine/issues and Step 24 rule/results/human-task/publication-gate contracts. Automated checks never establish total accessibility; generic teacher previews are read-only simulations. Required human review remains separate.

Barrier feedback is a local draft with controlled categories and optional description; it requests no diagnosis, causes no retaliation and is not submitted without a production API. Server publication enforcement, real resources, browser/media/PDF scans and human sign-off remain unavailable.
