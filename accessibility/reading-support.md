# Reading Support

Profiles are Original, Plain Language, Shorter Sentences, Supported Academic, One Step at a Time, Advanced Academic, and Teacher Custom. They change sentence structure, chunking, definitions, examples, spacing, and navigation while preserving the objective, required terms, policies, sources, uncertainty, answer boundary, and rubric. Original content remains available for comparison. Preferences never become age, diagnosis, reading-ability, intelligence, maturity, or rank signals and never lower competency expectations.
