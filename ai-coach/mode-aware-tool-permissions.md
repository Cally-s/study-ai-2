# Mode-Aware Tool Permissions

> **Step 42 integration:** Substantive educational help is presented through the validated Standard AI Response Card in the exact eight-section order. Trusted mode, policy, ladder, checkpoint, source, confidence-basis, verification, next-step, and AI-contribution data remain authoritative; the card creates no permission, exposes no hidden reasoning, uses no numerical confidence, and cannot leak a prohibited answer.

> Intervention action buttons carry server-resolved ladder/mode/tool permissions; blocked solvers, essay/code/proof generators, answer keys, source fabrication, and live-item retrieval never run.

> Step 39 tool permissions are the intersection of context, published assignment policy, active ladder level, required student work, selected mode, sources, disclosure/log/review, and final-answer gates.

> Tool allowlists are the intersection of mode needs and the published assignment permission, conditions, permitted sources, active-assessment status, and final-answer rule; the client cannot expand them.

> Context resolution intersects mode tools with verified or declared allowed tasks before provider calls; blocked drafts and attachments are preserved locally and never sent to a task-specific model.

> Step 36 permits scoped question generation, isolated key/rubric validation, approved source retrieval, bilingual review, and minimal student-response evaluation while blocking active-assessment/test-bank retrieval and current-question answering tools.

> Step 35 permits minimum-input classification, counterexample/boundary checks, student-code tests, source verification, stakeholder/accessibility review, and ethical checklists while blocking original-task solvers and profiling tools.

> Step 34 permits minimum rule/formula/intermediate/source/test checks only; full math/code/writing/proof solvers and answer keys remain blocked.

> Step 33 permits skill classification and synthetic/approved practice generation but blocks original-task solvers, answer keys, full generators, and assigned-text reconstruction.

> Guiding turns may use concept/formula/theorem lookup, minimum intermediate checks, and source verification; full solvers and generators remain blocked.

> Step 31 permits concept/glossary and minimum-input intermediate checks but removes full solvers and completion tools from every hint request.

Glossary/concept lookup, source verification, rubric interpretation, minimum-input intermediate checks, syntax error detection, accessibility transformation, teacher messages, safety, and platform help may be allowed. Full solvers, code/essay/proof/translation/lab generators, answer-key lookup, and assessment retrieval are restricted for the original task or blocked by policy/assessment state.
