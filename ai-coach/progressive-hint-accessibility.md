# Progressive Hint Accessibility

The semantic live hint card announces the exact level/purpose, hint, “Was this enough?”, actions, waiting state, policy maximum, and final-level message. Exact buttons have 44-pixel targets and support keyboard, screen readers, 200% zoom/mobile reflow, high contrast, reduced motion, bilingual/RTL, text-only and Low-Bandwidth layouts. Trying moves focus to the work input with “Show your next step.”
