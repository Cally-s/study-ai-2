# Learning Mode Privacy

> Quiz settings, responses, reasoning, mistakes, adaptations, confidence, notes mode, reattempts, and summaries remain private and create no timing, accessibility, language, ability, grade, honesty, or cheating inference.

> Challenge answers, evidence, defences, revisions, disagreements, and final positions remain private; counts and political, ethical, or personal content never create scores, profiles, misconduct evidence, or automatic reports.

> Student answers, uncertainty, errors, drafts and revisions are private; error count/status never becomes ability, confidence, honesty, grade or potential evidence.

> Skill profiles, examples and transfers are private; example count or transfer status never becomes ability, dependence, honesty, grade, or potential evidence.

> Guiding records remain private and minimal; question count and response categories never become ability, diagnosis, honesty, grade, or potential profiles.

> Hint levels, counts, prerequisite requests, and repeated help must not infer ability, diagnosis, motivation, honesty, grades, or potential.

> Step 30 violation records store codes and operational metadata only—never leaked answers, blocked bodies, hidden reasoning, or private source content.

Selections are private by default and must not infer intelligence, motivation, disability, mental health, honesty, or future performance. Teachers and parents do not receive full mode history automatically. Change events exclude message text. Opening or changing the selector creates neither competency evidence nor an AI Use Receipt; meaningful assistance may be added to a student-reviewed receipt without automatic submission.
