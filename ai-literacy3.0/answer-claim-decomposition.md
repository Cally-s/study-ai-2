# Answer Claim Decomposition

One sentence may contain multiple atomic claims requiring different evidence. AI-suggested extraction is a draft: students review, split, merge, edit, or mark non-claims. Each claim remains linked to its answer span and preserves qualifiers such as “may,” “in this study,” and exact dates.

Facts are separated from opinions, recommendations, examples, analogies, predictions, preferences, and creative content. A factual premise inside an opinion or fictional example becomes its own claim.
