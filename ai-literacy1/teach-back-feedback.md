# Teach-Back Feedback

Structured feedback uses: Teach-Back Feedback; What your response shows; What needs clarification; Why this matters; Try next. It begins with specific submitted-response evidence, addresses only the first material gap/misconception, and gives one manageable repair, verification, retry, or transfer action without rewriting the answer. Students can retry, request a hint/simpler prompt/different type/format, pause, or ask a person. Repeated unresolved evidence or teacher-specific judgement recommends human support rather than endless near-identical prompts.
