# Who Is Represented? Training Data and Bias

This six-phase Understand lesson teaches students to investigate how training patterns and human design choices shape representation, possible bias, harms, and task appropriateness. Embodied Ethics and AI Foundations are primary; Human Agency, Problem Scoping, Safe and Responsible Use, and meaningful Ethics by Design are secondary. Learn, Observe, Practise, Explain, Apply, and Reflect use controlled synthetic outputs. The static prototype lacks production persistence, live generation, and competency ingestion.
# Data minimization connection

Step 9 reinforces that collecting more personal/sensitive data is not automatically responsible or necessary for inclusion, evaluation, or bias mitigation.
