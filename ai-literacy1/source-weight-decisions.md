# Source Weight Decisions

Weight is claim specific: most weight, strong supporting, limited, contextual only, should not be relied on, different weight for different claim parts, no decisive source yet, or cannot determine. Students explain evidence, relevance, currentness, originality/authority, independence, limitations, agreement, and final claim wording.

There is no universal government/university/news/blog hierarchy, credibility percentage, automatic ranking, or permanent source rating.
