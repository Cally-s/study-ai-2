# Unsupported Claims

Unsupported by Current Map means the connected evidence does not adequately support the claim as written. It does not mean false and does not mean contradicted. Contradicted requires reliable contrary evidence. No Reliable Source Found means unresolved, not false.

Students may narrow, qualify, correct, split, remove, or leave a claim unresolved, request more evidence, or request human review. Responsible uncertainty is an acceptable outcome.
