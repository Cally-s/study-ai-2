# AI Response Field Ownership

Server-only fields include identity, tenant, versions, type/status, mode/context/policy, answer withholding, source trust, confidence, trusted assumption/verification state, sharing, hashes, and timestamps. The model proposes constrained educational wording only. Student corrections, outcomes, responses, disclosures, and sharing are separate authenticated events and never directly mutate a published record. Prompt, retrieved, model, and client content cannot override trusted fields.
