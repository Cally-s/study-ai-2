# Recovery Data Deletion

Eligible plans, versions, work inventory, notes, conversations, files/derivatives, voice/transcripts, offline/audio records, optional recognition/metrics/history, notifications, and unsent drafts can be requested for deletion.

Official sent messages/submissions, confirmed attendance/service-hour audit, safety/security records, legal holds, and required educational retention may limit deletion. Status distinguishes requested, validation, active deletion, derivative pending, backup pending, partial completion, completion, policy/legal blocks, and error. Limitations are shown before confirmation and never silently fail.
