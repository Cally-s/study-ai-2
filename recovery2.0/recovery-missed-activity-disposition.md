# Missed Activity Disposition

Every activity receives a visible disposition: learn the essential concept, complete access work, complete accepted missing work, ask for clarification, review a concise summary, review later, optional Stretch, exclude by confirmed status or do not recreate. Warm-ups, discussions, repeated practice, replaceable demonstrations, non-graded activities, superseded exercises, enrichment and irrelevant segments are not automatically recreated. Reasons and sources remain visible and student-correctable.
