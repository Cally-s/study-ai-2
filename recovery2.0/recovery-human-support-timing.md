# Human Support Timing

Student-requested help is available immediately. After one valid attempt, support may be optional. After two materially distinct valid attempts on the same narrow misunderstanding, human support is recommended. After three, AI explanation stops and live support is strongly recommended. Technical interruptions, inaccessible or language-inappropriate delivery, active-assessment restrictions, and non-distinct attempts do not count.

Official decisions and supervised tasks route immediately to their authorized role. Possible immediate safety concerns pause academic planning and route to immediate human support. Ordinary dismissed recommendations use a 24-hour cooldown; changed or new evidence may reopen review.
