# Recovery Explanation Claim Standard

The 25 controlled claim types cover deadlines, acceptance/requirement, grade impact, dependencies/prerequisites, knowledge/actionability/blocking, teacher clarification, student importance, progress/time, capacity/deadline fit, protected buffer, verification, release, actions, categories, ranks, provisional warnings and overrides.

Every material claim references current fact IDs or a controlled derived calculation. It records typed value, verification status, confidence, privacy-safe source label, source version, last checked time, materiality, reason code, display order and explanation version. Unsupported dates, percentages, prerequisites, teacher decisions, sources and capacity statements are rejected.

Source changes or stale/conflicting material facts invalidate the explanation. High confidence never promotes an estimate or extraction to Confirmed. Storage keys, internal URLs, credentials, hidden notes and free-form model reasoning are excluded.
