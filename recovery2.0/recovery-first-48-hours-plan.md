# First 48 Hours Plan

Two exact local dates contain at most five Minimum actions: review concise course-specific teacher messages, identify major assessments, request essential materials, request one authorized human-support meeting, and complete one already-actionable current task. At most two Stretch actions are optional. Full old assignments stay out unless verified, accepted, urgent, actionable and capacity-safe. Preparing/sending requests and waiting for responses count as progress; responses are not required for stabilization.
