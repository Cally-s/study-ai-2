# Missed Month Multiweek Plan

Progressive detail uses exact First 48 Hours actions, detailed Week 1 actions and staged goals for Weeks 2–4; two-, three-, six-, eight-week, school-defined or decision-pending horizons are allowed. Courses have independent streams/stages. Weekly Minimum fits confirmed capacity; Stretch is optional, may be empty and never rolls or uses buffer. Urgent confirmed work that does not fit stays visibly unfitted with its decision and recommended human action. Completed work survives replanning.
