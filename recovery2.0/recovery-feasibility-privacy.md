# Recovery Feasibility Privacy

Workload totals, focused capacity, Recovery Load, classification, bottlenecks, decision flags, views, proposed deferrals/negotiations, notes, and teacher-question drafts are private student recovery data.

Public, teacher, tutor, parent, and guardian serializers return no feasibility data. The result does not affect grades, matching, service hours, Help Credits, safety, fraud, integrity, discipline, or advertising. Safe analytics contain an allowlisted event name and timestamp only—not titles, courses, dates, times, ratios, private commitments, or question text.

Later sharing requires an exact preview, named recipient, selected fields, student edits, and explicit confirmation. AI may phrase only validated totals/classification/reason codes and must not perform authoritative arithmetic. Medical, family, employer, diagnosis, accommodation, credential, and unrelated-course data are excluded.
