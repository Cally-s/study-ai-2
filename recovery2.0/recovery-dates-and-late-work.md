# Recovery Dates and Late Work

## Original and current dates

Original due date and current/revised due date are separate structured values. Each stores a value, timezone, precision, source, source version and confirmation date. Precisions are exact date/time, date only, approximate, unknown and not applicable. Sources distinguish confirmed systems from student-entered/imported/unknown data.

A date-only value remains `YYYY-MM-DD`; it never gains an invented 11:59 p.m. time. Approximate dates remain approximate. Revised dates never overwrite original dates. Unconfirmed values never appear source-confirmed.

## Late-work acceptance

Late-work states are Not Applicable, Late Work Accepted, Accepted Under Certain Conditions, Late Work Not Accepted and I Do Not Know. Unknown is the default for overdue student-entered items with no policy. Item status and late-work acceptance are independent: Submitted is not Accepted, item Accepted is not a general policy and one item/course policy is not reused elsewhere.

## Teacher-confirmation safeguard

When a date has passed, status is neither Accepted nor No Longer Required, late acceptance is unknown and remaining work is at least the configurable 90-minute default (or may be substantial because it is unknown), show exactly:

> We should confirm this with your teacher before spending several hours on it.

Actions may add a private draft question, mark Waiting for Teacher, continue with uncertainty, update late-work information or defer. Nothing is sent, removed, prioritized or scheduled automatically.
## Extracted dates

AI/OCR/parser dates enter as unconfirmed candidate fields. Relative, ambiguous, missing-year and conflicting forms retain source text and interpretation until student correction/confirmation. Extraction never changes official dates or establishes late-work acceptance.

## Step 7 deadline windows

Current/revised dates take precedence while original dates remain historical. Unknown dates and unknown late acceptance create clarification flags. Confirmed deadline windows exclude post-deadline capacity; date-only values receive no invented school-specific time.

Step 8 records deadline and late-policy verification status, confidence, source/version and last checked separately. Conflicts remain visible; stale dates cannot authorize verified prioritization; past-due substantial work with unknown acceptance receives the teacher-confirmation safeguard.
