# Human Support Accessibility Testing

Automated source checks cover semantic headings and native buttons, visible focus, 320px/200% reflow hooks, forced colours, reduced motion, RTL, simplified/dyslexia/reading-ruler modes, low bandwidth, and print. Manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, high-contrast, Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text, Language Bridge, RTL, low-bandwidth, cognitive/disclosure, and representative-user journeys remain NOT RUN.
