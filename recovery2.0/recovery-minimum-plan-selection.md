# Minimum Plan Selection

Candidates come from current priority actions, bounded Ask First clarification, submission checks, Step 12 repairs, material/instruction actions, verified first steps, nearly complete accepted work and human-support requests. Raw multi-hour or active-assessment work is excluded.

Selection is deterministic and constraint-first: capacity/safety, deadline stabilization, high-value clarification, essential repair, blocker removal, student importance, nearly complete valid work, low task-switching, protected buffer, then the smallest sufficient action count. There is no opaque aggregate score.

Every item records its stabilization goal, source, duration/range, verification, reasons, current actionability and what could change. Urgent work that does not fit appears separately with the gap and next action; it is never disguised as Stretch. Teacher-dependent major work stays out while a bounded draft/review action may enter.

Seven-day placement retains this membership and applies daily capacity, deadline, dependency, contact timing, focus-block and subject-switch constraints.
