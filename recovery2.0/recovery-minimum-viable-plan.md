# Minimum Viable Recovery Plan

Test rescue Minimum contains only the most useful fitting diagnostic/repair/example/recall/correction/material steps. Stretch remains optional and stopping on time preserves success.

Ten-minute selection prefers incomplete ready Minimum actions. Completion updates Minimum only with evidence; elapsed time, partial progress and blockers do not complete it.

Decomposed actions preserve Minimum success and optional Stretch. Later actions remain scheduled later or unfitted; smaller labels do not hide workload.

## Purpose
Step 13 selects the smallest realistic stabilization set, not the backlog. Every plan has a Minimum level and supports an explicitly optional Stretch level.

## Minimum Plan
Minimum actions are verified and actionable now, or bounded clarification/support actions. Defaults are one to three actions. Applicable goals include reducing critical uncertainty, protecting the nearest deadline, unlocking an urgent task, repairing one prerequisite, obtaining material, completing one first step, confirming submission/acceptance, requesting help and protecting rest.

## Stretch Plan
Stretch contains zero to two safe additional actions that fit only unused focused capacity. It may be empty. It is never required, overdue, failed or automatically rolled forward.

## Capacity and success
Upper duration estimates validate fit. Breaks/setup stay inside the planning envelope and protected buffer remains unchanged. Completing all current Minimum items counts as success even when no Stretch item is completed. A zero-capacity protected day is also valid.

## Student control and limitations
Students review, edit, reorder without dragging, preview level changes, remove Stretch, pause, replan and restore the system recommendation. The static prototype lacks production authentication, persistence, source adapters, messaging, scheduling and durable offline sync.

Step 14 assigns the reviewed actions to seven exact dates and focus-block order without changing Minimum/Stretch meaning or success.
## Missed-week Minimum

Minimum contains essential missed learning, current-access work and confirmed accepted assignments that fit. Lower-value review is Stretch or later; completing Minimum is success even when older activity is not recreated.
## Missed-month Minimum

Weekly Minimum contains decision/current-access actions, bounded repair, current work and only verified accepted/replacement work that fits. Stretch may be empty and does not determine success.
## Overwhelmed view

Show only one to three today Minimum actions. Completing Minimum is enough; Stretch stays hidden and does not reveal automatically.
## Teacher contact action

Reviewing/sending one teacher question can be a Minimum action when it unlocks decisions; Copy/Open Email remain incomplete delivery states.
# Step 23 parent-summary integration

An approved Minimum Plan may be condensed into a student-controlled Parent-Friendly Summary. Stretch, private capacity reasons, buffer calculations, and private notes remain excluded by default; sharing does not alter the plan.
# Step 24 daily-check-in integration

Daily outcomes update Minimum status only from validated evidence. Partial progress counts; incomplete Minimum work is not rolled automatically, and completion may validly lead to Finish for Today without revealing Stretch.
# Step 25 recalculation integration

Completed/partial Minimum remains preserved. When capacity falls, Stretch and optional work leave active scheduling first; Minimum actions may be split but are never compressed into buffer/sleep or moved automatically.

## Step 28 — Private Recovery Progress Recognition

Only specific, meaningful, source-versioned actions with sufficient evidence may receive brief recognition explaining why the action matters. Partial, prepared, uploaded, booked, proposed, or provisional states are never overstated as completed, sent, submitted, attended, accepted, approved, or active. Recognition is private, cumulative, non-resetting, dismissible, preference controlled, bundled at natural stopping points, and suppressed during focused work, assessments, meetings, tutoring, sensitive forms, and immediate-safety support. It creates no streak, points, rank, badge, public sharing, grade, matching effect, service hour, Help Credit, safety decision, or automatic notification.

## Step 31 — Integrated Accessibility and Language Bridge

This workflow uses the shared optional AccessibilityPreferences profile plus per-screen/session overrides without losing progress. It must be fully reachable by keyboard and structured for screen readers; support 200%/320px reflow, contrast, Reduced Motion, Text-to-Speech with visible text, reviewed Speech-to-Text, reading levels that preserve expectations/formulas/statuses/English terminology, bilingual/RTL display, plain-text Low-Bandwidth fallback, versioned offline/print/audio alternatives, and generic private previews. No diagnosis or reason is required. Names, codes, URLs, numbers, dates, formulas, negation, authority and status meanings are invariant. Machine translation is labelled; official content requires review; nothing sends, books, shares, submits, activates, or overwrites automatically.
## Step 34 dashboard integration
The private Recovery Dashboard summarizes this source through its exact five-area aggregation contract. It deep-links back here for review or action, preserves authoritative statuses and versions, shows only minimum privacy-safe information, and never automatically starts, completes, sends, books, rolls, shares, or activates a plan change.
