# Deadline Risk and Teacher Contact

Statuses distinguish On Track, Tight, At Risk, Not Feasible, Deadline Unknown, Acceptance Unknown, Teacher Decision Required, Passed, Conditionally Accepted, and Outdated. Capacity ranges and source status remain visible; impossible promises and invented dates/acceptance are prohibited.

Risk may prepare a Step 22 draft to ask about acceptance, priority, clarification, materials, realistic proposed date, replacement, meeting, assessment scope, support process, or pending decision. Draft, Ready for Review, Sent, and Waiting remain distinct. Nothing sends automatically. Urgent unfitted work stays visible.
