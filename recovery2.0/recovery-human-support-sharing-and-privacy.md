# Human Support Sharing and Privacy

Sharing is minimum necessary and always previewed. Tutors see the approved topic and concise learning issue, not full diagnostics or emotional disclosures. Teachers see relevant course questions, not unrelated support history. Counsellors receive an approved coordination agenda, accessibility staff only relevant functional/formal-support context, and parents only separately approved content. Safety handling is separated from ordinary academic handoffs.

Recommendation text, private evidence, handoffs, disability/medical information, and support history are excluded from public profiles, ordinary notifications, grades, matching, fraud/integrity scoring, analytics, and logs.
