# Human Support Tutoring Integration

A recommendation may prefill a 25-minute request with course, topic, grade, and approved issue summary. Production matching must require active verified subject and grade qualifications, availability, format, language, accessibility experience, teaching style, ratings, response rate, and previous success. Protected characteristics are excluded.

Safe booking remains a separate student-reviewed flow with consent when required, secure meeting details, approved locations, and no private-home minor sessions. Pre-session preparation is optional. Booking is not attendance; attendance is not service-hour approval. Official service hours remain non-transferable and verifier approved; Help Credits remain separate.
