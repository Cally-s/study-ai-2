# Recovery Exceptional Access

Exceptional reasons are immediate safety, mandatory safeguarding, legal order, security incident, account recovery, required school review, data repair, and authorized privacy investigation.

Access requires an authorized human role, stated purpose, specific resources, minimum data, start/expiry, audit, post-access review, and student notice when lawful and safe. Highly sensitive access may require two-person approval. AI may flag a concern but never authorizes access. Safety uses its separate protected process.
