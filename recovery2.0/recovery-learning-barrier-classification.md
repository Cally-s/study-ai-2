# Recovery Learning Barrier Classification

Classification is deterministic and task-specific. Precedence is: teacher-policy problem, missing-material problem, instruction problem, prerequisite problem, practice problem, then time-management problem. Time management is considered only after the other barriers are ruled out.

Primary and secondary barriers, evidence status, confidence, unknowns, and previous results are retained separately. A student may disagree or request human review. The feature never creates a medical, disability, intelligence, motivation, responsibility, or permanent-learning diagnosis.

During repair, instruction, material and teacher-policy findings stop concept remediation and redirect to the corresponding barrier. Demonstrated understanding may expose a practice or time-management need without creating another prerequisite path.
## Step 20 reclassification

A student-stated prerequisite remains provisional. Diagnostic/task evidence may reclassify the barrier as instruction, practice, material, teacher policy or time-management rather than forcing prerequisite remediation.
# Step 26 blocked integration

Blocked reasons map to conceptual, instructional, teacher decision, material, technical, collaboration, accessibility, requirement, personal-support, safety, or unknown categories without diagnostic/motivation/ability inference.
