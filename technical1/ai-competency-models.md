# AI Competency Models

Competency definitions have stable IDs and publishable definition versions. Evidence references the exact definition and artifact versions plus provenance, support condition, status, limitations, and review references. `AICompetencyProgress` is a disposable projection rebuilt from non-invalidated eligible evidence. `SOURCE_VERIFICATION_PENDING`, task-invalid, and human-review-required records cannot produce demonstrated status. Independent status requires explicit independent evidence; there is no global literacy score or student rank.
