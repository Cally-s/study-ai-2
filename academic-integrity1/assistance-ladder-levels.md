# Assistance Ladder Levels

> Intervention buttons expose only currently permitted level contracts; no button becomes a hidden completed-response, solver, or policy bypass.

Level 1 clarifies deliverables without answering. Level 2 explains only the needed concept. Level 3 reuses one Step 32 question. Level 4 reuses Step 31's three-hint maximum. Level 5 uses a genuinely different Step 33 example. Level 6 checks actual student-authored work through Step 34. Level 7 gives focused revision feedback without rewriting. Level 8 verifies claims/calculations/sources/code/reasoning and asks for student reflection; it is not a final-answer stage.
