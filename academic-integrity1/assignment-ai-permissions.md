# Assignment AI Permissions

> Restricted, conditional, source, attempt, disclosure, log, review, and final-answer states map to specific supportive intervention reasons rather than a generic refusal.

> Ladder levels resolve from specific permissions rather than a broad AI toggle, so sparse policies may allow concepts and checking while restricting hints, examples, revision, verification, or final answers.

The sixteen independent permissions are Concept explanations, Definitions, Bilingual explanations, Hints, Guiding questions, Similar examples, Practice questions, Brainstorming, Outline feedback, Source verification, Rubric interpretation, Draft feedback, Grammar feedback, Revision suggestions, Code debugging, and Final-answer generation. Each maps separately to learning modes and tools; allowing one never enables another.
